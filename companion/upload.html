1. Which song?

Tracks and charts go to this song. A chart alone also makes the song if it does not exist yet.

2. Tracks (stems or one mix)

Drop WAV / MP3 / FLAC / M4A files here, or
Several stems at once is fine ("click" and "guide" stems go to the in-ear output automatically). The Central Control finds the tempo, key, downbeat and big hits itself.

3. Chord chart / lyrics

Drop a PDF, a photo of the chart, or a text / ChordPro file here, or
Section names (Verse, Chorus, Bridge…) become sections; chord lines are dropped for the projector lyrics.
Or paste the chart text

Pad sounds

Drop WAV / MP3 pad sounds here, or
They appear in Import Sound → "Uploaded over Wi-Fi" on the Central Control.

Keys sounds (SoundFonts)

Drop .sf2 / .sf3 SoundFont files here, or
They appear under Instrument → Piano & keys → Sounds, marked "yours". Free banks: FreePats, Salamander piano, Musical Artifacts…
