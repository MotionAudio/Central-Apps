Configure

What each button, knob, slider and pedal does. Give a knob, slider or the encoder the Touch select job, then tap any setting on the screen and that knob changes it.

One layout for everything: each button, knob and slider does the same thing whether a song has tracks or not (the pads follow the tracks). A button can also do something else when held.

Buttons: the first action happens on press, the hold action after holding for a second. Knobs / sliders / expression: pick a continuous control. Encoder: a step action for turning, plus press and hold actions. Learned USB and MIDI inputs appear here too.

Learn

Control board inputs

Wire a button, knob or slider to any usable pin, press Learn, then press or turn it: the Central Pedal finds the pin or ADS1115 channel by itself. The defaults below match the wiring diagram.

Your pedals, controllers and keys

MIDI footswitches, pedals, knobs and sliders, or a key on the computer's keyboard. Press Add new control, say what it is, name it, then press it, turn it or slide it when we ask. After that, give it a job on the Configure tab.

Default wiring

Buttons: pin → switch → GND. Knobs and sliders: 3.3 V – wiper → ADS1115 – GND. LEDs on the MCP23017. Full guide with the I2S DAC and the 3.5-inch screen: wiring.html

Output routing

Send each bus to one output (mono) or a pair (stereo); several buses on one output are mixed. 1–2 are the headphone jack, 3–4 the DAC board, 5 up a USB audio interface. Quick set-ups: 4 mono, 2 stereo, or everything on 1–2.
