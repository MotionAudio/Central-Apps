What I'm building next

These are coming, one at a time. Each is built from the same Central I/O boxes: a controller that does the thinking, output boxes with 16 outputs each and input boxes with 16 inputs each. None of it is for sale yet, and details will change before each one ships.

The PedalThe In-EarsCentral Wave

Three products, one set of boxes

Each one is its own product. They come in this order.

The Pedal

On Kickstarter in July 2027

Every channel the pedal plays, on its own output.

The pedal has six outputs on the back. With Central I/O, each stem, the click, the cues and the pads get their own output at the desk. Plug the pedal's I/O port into the controller, stack an output box on top, and spread the pedal's channels across it in Central Control. Add output boxes for more outputs.

  • One network cable from the pedal to the controller
  • 16 outputs per output box, up to 16 boxes
  • Set up in Central Control, not at the box

The In-Ears

Coming November 2027

The band's in-ear mixes, each musician running their own.

The controller builds up to 32 stereo in-ear mixes from your sound board (AES67, including Dante boards in AES67 mode), the pedal and the input box. Each musician sets their own mix from Central Usage on their phone, and the output box feeds the wired packs or the wireless transmitters.

  • Up to 32 stereo mixes, each with its own EQ, compressor and reverb
  • One output box covers eight musicians
  • The input box takes the stage mics and instruments

The boxes: Controller + Output Box + Input Box

How the boxes do it

Central Wave

Coming March 2028

Every speaker in the room its own mix, timing and EQ.

Place your speakers on a map of the room and plug one to eight measurement mics into your board. The controller measures the room and sets every speaker so the sound is even from the front row to the back wall. During the service it keeps listening and makes small changes, and anything it sets, you can change.

  • One output per speaker or amp channel
  • Measures the room with your own mics
  • Follows the service, or stays where you left it
