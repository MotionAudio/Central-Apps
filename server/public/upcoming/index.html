What I'm building next

Central I/O: three boxes that go with the pedal and Central Control. A controller that does the thinking, an input box with 8 inputs and an output box with 16 outputs. All three come out together on Kickstarter in July 2027. None of it is for sale yet, and details will change before it ships.

The three boxesWhat they can do

The three boxes

All three come out together on Kickstarter in July 2027.

The Controller

Kickstarter, July 2027

The brain. It runs the software for whichever job you need.

The controller connects to your church network, your sound board and the pedal, and it tells every other box what to do. Inside is a Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5. One long colour screen across the front shows what it's doing, every channel's level and how fast each network link is running. Plug a computer into the USB-C port on the front to change its settings.

  • AES67 to and from your sound board, and Dante boards in their AES67 mode
  • Runs up to 16 input and output boxes
  • The rack version powers every box stacked on it
How it's set up

The Input Box

Kickstarter, July 2027

8 inputs, for mics, instruments and boards without AES67.

Plug in an XLR or a 1/4-inch cable on each of its 8 combo jacks. You set the gain, the pad and phantom power from Central Control. It brings the band's mics and instruments in on stage, and the channels of a board that has no network card.

  • 8 combo jacks: XLR or 1/4-inch
  • Gain, pad and 48 V phantom on every input
  • Set from Central Control, not at the box
How it's set up

The Output Box

Kickstarter, July 2027

16 outputs, for stems, in-ears or speakers.

Each output box adds 16 balanced XLR outputs. An output can carry one of the pedal's stems, an in-ear mix (stereo mixes use two) or the feed to one speaker. The controller tells each box what goes where.

  • 16 balanced XLR outputs
  • Add boxes for more outputs
  • A signal light for every output
How it's set up

Every box comes as a rack version or a standalone one. The rack boxes stack on spring pins, with no cables between them. A standalone box has its own power adapter and goes wherever a stack can't reach.

Three things they can do

The boxes are the same for all three. Only the software on the controller changes.

The Pedal

July 2027

Every channel the pedal plays, on its own output.

The pedal has six outputs on the back. With Central I/O, each stem, the click, the cues and the pads get their own output at the desk. Plug the pedal's I/O port into the controller, stack an output box on top, and spread the pedal's channels across it in Central Control.

  • One network cable from the pedal to the controller
  • 16 outputs per output box
  • Set up in Central Control, not at the box

You need: The controller and an output box.

How the boxes do it

The In-Ears

July 2027

The band's in-ear mixes, each musician running their own.

The controller builds up to 32 stereo in-ear mixes from your sound board (AES67, including Dante boards in AES67 mode), the pedal and the input box. Each musician sets their own mix from Central Usage on their phone, and the output box feeds the wired packs or the wireless transmitters.

  • Up to 32 stereo mixes, each with its own EQ, compressor and reverb
  • One output box covers eight musicians
  • The input box takes the stage mics and instruments

You need: The controller and an output box, plus an input box for stage mics.

How the boxes do it

Central Wave

Software addition, February 2028

Every speaker in the room its own mix, timing and EQ.

Place your speakers on a map of the room and plug one to eight measurement mics into your board. The controller measures the room and sets every speaker so the sound is even from the front row to the back wall. During the service it keeps listening and makes small changes, and anything it sets, you can change.

  • One output per speaker or amp channel
  • Measures the room with your own mics
  • Follows the service, or stays where you left it

You need: The same controller and output boxes. It's new software, not new hardware.
