Packs for your whole team

Your church's account holds every key and slot, and you hand them out from the Teams page. Each pack has a main admin, admins, users, Central Pedal Control and Central Services slots. The pedal is separate, so you can start with what you already have.

Sound plans

More pianos, synths, instruments, plugins, pedals and pads for every app, a year at a time. Every plan includes Central Pads. Each plan comes as a package key by email: paste it in any app's Settings → Packages → Add a package. It works on three of your devices, each a different app (say Central Usage, Central Control and your Central Pedal). The starter sounds stay free.

For your church: every plan for your team

A package key with every plan (Central Pads too) for each person, a year. Hand them out on the Teams page; take one back any time and give it to someone else.
