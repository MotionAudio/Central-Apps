Get the band, the desk and the screens on the same page.

I'm Sam, and I make Central. It's a pedal and a handful of apps that work together. The worship leader plays pads and tracks from the pedal on the floor. Whoever's at the desk runs lights, lyrics and in-ears from a computer. And each musician gets their own chart and mix on a phone or tablet.

Central Production is free

It's a recording studio that runs on your computer or in your browser. Record and arrange a song, make your own pads and instruments, or build a plugin or a pedal board, and send it straight to your other apps. The desktop app and the browser open the same projects.

  • Record, edit and mix, with your own VST3 and CLAP plugins
  • Make pads, loops and instruments for the pedal and the apps
  • No key needed. If you have a sound plan, its sounds show up inside

Central Tracks

A library of pads and multitracks from other worship teams, plus hymns we've arranged. We grade each track for sound quality, and you can listen to a short clip before you add it.
