Getting started

Which apps do I need?

Most churches need Central Control for the desk, Central Usage for each musician, Central Services for the schedule and Central Pedal Control to get the pedal ready. A church pack has all four. Add the Central Pedal if you want pads and tracks at your feet. See Apps → Compare.

How do I set up the pedal?

Plug in the power, a monitor on HDMI 1 if you like, and headphones or the desk on the headphone jack. Join the church Wi-Fi from Settings → Wi-Fi & devices, then build your setlist in Central Pedal Control and press Upload. The setup guide in the Help Center goes step by step.

How do the apps find each other?

On the same network, they find each other by themselves: the pedal, the desk computers and the musicians' devices. If a network keeps devices apart, type the pedal's address (on its Settings → About) into Connect.

Keys and accounts

Where are my keys?

Account → My products lists every key you or your church owns, with the devices each one is on and a Copy button. They're in your email too.

How do I move a key to a new computer?

Account → My products → Release next to the old device, then enter the key on the new one. Central Control keys are for one computer; Central Usage and Central Pedal Control keys work on two devices.

What is the 3-device rule for sound plans?

A sound plan's package key works on three of your devices at once, each a different app: say Central Usage, Central Control and your pedal. It can't be three of the same app.

How do renewals work?

Central Control keys include ten releases of updates (buy during 10.x and you're covered to 11.0); renew for $20 any time. Sound plans and Central Utility renew yearly, and Central Tracks Unlimited monthly, until you cancel.

Plans and billing

What does each plan include?

Central Keys: pianos, organs, mallets, pads and synths. Central Instrument: basses, guitars, strings, brass, drums and loops. Central Pads: 82 pads in every key, with Central Pads Extra. Central Plugins: 32 better effects. Central Pedals: 55 new pedals, cabinets and 68 boards. Every plan includes Central Pads.

What is Central Utility?

118 seasonal and Christian backgrounds with 51 matching slide themes and lower thirds, 14 click sounds, 24 lighting looks, countdowns and stream overlays. $50 a year, and included in All plans ($270) and every church bundle.

What are church bundles?

Every sound plan plus Central Utility for each person on your team, at a lower price per person: 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20 people.

Can I get a refund?

Tell us within 30 days if something isn't right. Our refund policy page has the details.

How do I cancel?

Account → Billing opens Stripe's billing page, where you can cancel a plan or subscription. It stays active until the end of what you paid for.

What happens if a plan ends in the middle of a service?

Nothing stops mid-song. A pad, pedal, plugin or sound that's already playing with that plan keeps going until the song ends: the music stops or the setlist moves to the next song. Then it shows that it needs the plan and is bypassed, on the pedal, in Central Usage, in Central Control's mixer and in the Studio. You can't add anything new from that plan in the meantime. Renew it and everything comes back on by itself.

Sound and audio

I hear crackles but no music on the pedal's headphone jack.

Set Settings → Output device → Headphone jack buffer to Biggest. The Pi's own jack needs a bigger buffer than a sound card. Test sound also steps it up by itself when it finds dropouts.

Can I use an audio interface?

Yes, for inputs and outputs. Plug it in and it appears under Settings → Output device; its outputs come after the headphone jack (1-2) and the DAC board (3-4).

There's no sound at all.

Go through the No sound checklist in the Help Center: Test sound, Check where the sound goes, Play on each sound card, then the cable and headphones.

Lights and video

How do I add my real lights?

In Central Control's Lighting tab, Find a real light… searches 2,272 fixtures by maker and model. No match? Make a profile… lets you list the channels yourself.

Can the lights follow the songs by themselves?

Yes. Auto lights follow each song's sections; your own scenes fire where you put them. Simulate a setlist to see it before Sunday.

How do I stream?

Give a computer the Stream mix job. The Video tab sends the program to RTMP or SRT destinations, with slides, lyrics and lower thirds on top.

Central Tracks and Make & sell

Can I hear a track before I buy?

Yes. Every pad, loop, instrument and track has a 30-second preview on the website and in the apps.

How do I publish a track?

On the home page, Central Tracks → Publish your own track, or use Make & sell for sounds, pads, instruments and plugins. We check every upload before it goes live.

How are creators paid?

Tracks: 75% of each sale, plus a share of the subscriber pool when subscribers add your tracks. Make & sell items: a share of each sound plan's money, by downloads and the stars we give. We pay monthly through Stripe.

Planning Center and CCLI

How do I connect Planning Center?

A main admin presses Connect Planning Center on Account → Planning Center & CCLI (or in Central Control's Settings), signs in and allows it. Until then you can import a plan from a file.

Does it report to CCLI for us?

It counts every time a song's words reach a screen and fills in the report. Download it from your Account page in the layout CCLI asks for, and enter it on CCLI's site.

Shorts and social media

How are shorts made?

After a service recording of 10 minutes or more, the built-in AI picks the strongest sermon moments and worship high points and makes vertical clips with captions. You review every one before it's posted.

Where can shorts be posted?

YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, once each is connected in Central Control → Shorts → Settings. You can always download and post by hand.

Troubleshooting

An update failed.

Settings → Software updates → Try again, or download the newest version from Downloads. On Linux, an update waits for the computer's own updates first and tells you the real reason if it can't install.

It says it can't reach the website.

Check the device is online, and look at the status page to see if sign-in or downloads are down. The apps keep working offline.

The pedal shows as offline.

Make sure it's on and on the same Wi-Fi. Some networks keep devices apart: ask for that to be allowed, use a cable, or type the pedal's address into Connect.
