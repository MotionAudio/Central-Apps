How accounts work

Your church has its own account. It holds the keys and slots, and adds the main admins, admins and users.

The short version

Your church has one account of its own. It buys, pays and holds every key and Central Services slot. From it you add your main admins, admins and users, and each of them gets an email with their own sign-in, app and key.

The church account

The church signs up with its own email, like office@yourchurch.org, and the church's name. It's the church's login, not a person's, so it stays put when people come and go.

  • It buys and pays. The card and the receipts are in the church's name.
  • It holds every key and every Central Services slot, and hands them out from the Teams page.
  • It adds the main admins, admins and users, changes their roles and takes people out.
  • It doesn't use a Central Services slot.

Keep the church account's password somewhere your leadership can find it, like the church office's password manager. It's the account that owns your keys.

Main admins

A church can have up to two main admins. You make one by promoting an admin.

  • They have the same controls as the church account: roles, adding and removing people, billing and buying.
  • Their computer runs Central Control as the hub, with an A key. Every device pairs with it.
  • The second main admin's computer can keep in sync with the hub and take over, planned or in an emergency. See The second main admin's computer.
  • To step back, a main admin can go back to being an admin.

Admins and users

Admins run Central Control on their own computer with an A key (one computer per key): lights, cameras, the mix, in-ears, slides and lyrics, whichever jobs they choose for that computer. One computer can do several jobs on one key. They can buy for the church and hand out keys.

Users are the musicians and singers. They run Central Usage with a U key on up to two devices, say a church iPad and their phone at home, with the same account and settings on both.

Central Services slots

Central Services is sold as slots, not keys: $5 each, paid once, and every pack comes with some. Each person on your worship and production teams uses one slot, except the church account. Take someone off every team and their slot is free for the next person.

Sound plans and package keys

The sound plans add sounds to every app, a year at a time: Central Keys (pianos and synths), Central Plugins (EQ, compressors, reverb, delay and more), Central Instrument (bass and guitar sounds, and what your instrument can become), Central Pedals (pedals for anything with a 1/4" or 1/8" input) and Central Pads (pads in every key for the pad pedal - it comes with every other plan too). Each comes as a package key, starting with S, that you get by email and can always find under your account icon → Purchases.

  • On a device: Settings → Packages → Add a package, and paste the key. It works on three of your devices at a time, each a different app - say Central Usage, Central Control and your Central Pedal, all with your account (never two pedals or two Central Usages). Release one in Settings → Packages or on your account page.
  • Your own: anyone can buy plans for themselves. They renew every year; manage the card and renewal on your account page.
  • For the church: a bundle gives a package key with every plan for each person (4, 8, 12, 16 or 20 people). The church account, the main admins and admins give them out on the Teams page, and each person gets theirs by email. Take back stops that key, and a new one is ready for someone else.
  • Free trial: a trial code includes every plan; the trial package keys stop when the trial ends.

Central Pedal itself needs no key. Central Pedal Control still uses a P key, Central Control an A key and Central Usage a U key.

Setting up, start to finish

  1. Create the church account on the website with the church's email and name.
  2. Buy a pack (or single keys and slots). Codes for discounts or a free trial go in the cart.
  3. Add people on the Teams page: name, email and role for each main admin, admin and user, then Done. Each person gets an email with a link to make their account, their app's download link and their key. People who haven't answered get a reminder after 3 and 7 days.
  4. Promote a main admin (or two) from the admins in People.
  5. Everyone installs their app and types their key. See Install the apps.
  6. The main admin sets up the hub and pairs devices: Central Control on the sound desk computer, a pairing PIN, and every other device types it. See First-time setup.

Signing in safely

  • The church account, main admins and admins sign in with their email, password and a 6-digit code sent to their phone, once per device.
  • Every sign-in has Forgot password?
  • Move to… on the Teams page puts someone on another team; they get the new app's key by email.
  • Delete my account is in Account. It frees your keys and takes you out of your church.
