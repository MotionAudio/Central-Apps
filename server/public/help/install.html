Before you start

Every app is on the download page, which picks the right file for the device you open it on. Install the apps for your role, sign in, type your key, and they update themselves from then on.

The Central Pedal itself comes ready: see The Central Pedal.

The apps aren't signed with a paid Apple, Microsoft or Google certificate yet, so each device asks once the first time. The steps below show what to press.

Windows

  1. Download the .exe for your app from the download page (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit).
  2. Open it. Windows says "Windows protected your PC".
  3. Click More info, then Run anyway. It installs and opens by itself.

Mac

  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac: Apple Silicon for M1 and newer, Intel for Macs from before 2021.
  2. Open it and drag the app into Applications.
  3. Open the app once. The Mac refuses because it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
  4. System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down, click Open Anyway next to the app's name, and confirm.
  5. Allow the microphone when it asks. The audio interface, the in-ears, the tuner and the instrument need it.

If the Mac says the app "is damaged", open Terminal and run the line for that app:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Central Control.app"
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/CentralUsage.app"
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Central Pedal Control.app"

Linux

The easy way: open Terminal (Ctrl+Alt+T on Ubuntu), paste the line for your app, press Enter and type your password when it asks. It finds the newest version, checks the download, and installs it with everything the app needs. Ubuntu, Debian, Mint and Pop!_OS get the .deb; any other Linux gets the AppImage in ~/Applications with a menu entry. Run the same line again any time to reinstall or update.

Central Control

curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.org/install.sh | bash -s control

Then open Central Control from your app menu, or type central-control. Installing it also fixes any old menu entries and desktop icons.

Central Usage

curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.org/install.sh | bash -s usage

Central Pedal Control

curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.org/install.sh | bash -s pedal-control

Central Services

By hand

  • A .deb: download it from the download page, then sudo apt install ./<the file>.deb in the folder you saved it to. The ./ matters. Each app installs next to the others.
  • An AppImage: right-click it → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing file as program, then double-click it. AppImages need FUSE 2 (sudo dnf install fuse-libs on Fedora, sudo pacman -S fuse2 on Arch).

Central Control's mixer, in-ears and recording reach your audio interface through PipeWire's JACK: the .deb recommends pipewire-jack, and sudo apt install pipewire-jack adds it if it's missing.

Android

Central Usage, Central Pedal Control and Central Services run on Android 6 or newer.

  1. On the phone or tablet, open the download page and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the .apk from Downloads or the browser's notification.
  3. Allow installs from your browser: Android asks once. Settings → turn on Allow from this source → back → Install.
  4. Open the app on the church Wi-Fi. Central Usage: tap Find the main admin. Central Pedal Control: set up your songs, then Upload to the pedal.

iPhone and iPad

The App Store apps aren't out yet. Today:

  • Central Usage runs in Safari, served by the main admin's computer: join the church Wi-Fi, open http://<main admin's address>:8095/cu/, choose User 1 or User 2 and type the pairing PIN, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and always has the hub's version. The address is in the main admin's Central Control, Settings → This computer.
  • Central Services works in Safari on the website: open Central Services.
  • Central Pedal Control on an iPad: on the same Wi-Fi as the pedal, open http://padpedal.local:8080/control/ in Safari (or the pedal's address with :8080/control/), then Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app, with ● Live, Upload and Download. The App Store version follows.

The Central Pedal

We build every pedal and ship it with its card in and its software installed, tested, and it needs no key, so there's nothing to install. The first time it starts, set it up from your phone:

  1. Plug in the power and your outputs. A screen on HDMI 1 (the micro-HDMI port next to the power socket) is optional.
  2. On your phone, connect to the Wi-Fi "Central Pedal Setup". The pedal makes it the first time it starts. The setup page opens by itself (if it doesn't, go to http://10.42.0.1).
  3. Pick the church Wi-Fi and type its password.
  4. Type a 4-digit code. If you use Central Control, type the main admin's code: that pairs the pedal with it. If not, pick any 4 digits. Central Pedal Control uses the same code to Upload songs.
  5. Press Upload. The pedal joins the Wi-Fi and is ready. If the password was wrong, "Central Pedal Setup" comes back in about a minute and the page says why.

More in First time on.

Updates arrive by themselves: the pedal looks every time it starts and installs a new version after a 10-second notice, never while something plays. With no internet at church, download the pedal update, copy it to a USB stick, and on the pedal use Settings → Update software from USB….

Signing in and your key

The first time an app opens, it asks for your key and your Motion Audio account. Your key is in the email you got when you bought it or when your church invited you, and on the website under your account icon → Purchases. Central Control also asks for your church's name and which jobs this computer will do.

  1. Sign in. Use the account your invite email made. Admins can share one account if your church wants; each user has their own. Main admins and admins also get a code by text on a new device.
  2. Type your key: 16 letters and digits.
  3. Join the Wi-Fi if the device isn't online yet.

Updates

  • Windows, Mac and Linux apps look for a new version every time they open and install it after a short notice, never while something plays. You can turn automatic installs off in the app's settings and check for updates there yourself.
  • Android apps look every time they open; the new .apk downloads and Android shows Update. One tap.
  • Central Usage in Safari always has the hub's version: nothing to do.
  • Songs, setlists, settings, your key and your sign-in are never touched by an update.

Uninstalling

  • First, release the key (Settings → License → Release key) so you can use it on another device.
  • Windows: Settings → Apps → the app → Uninstall.
  • Mac: drag the app from Applications to the Bin.
  • Linux: sudo apt remove central-control (or centralusage, central-pedal-control, central-services). For an AppImage, delete the file in ~/Applications and its menu entry in ~/.local/share/applications.
  • Android: press and hold the app → Uninstall.
