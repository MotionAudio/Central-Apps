Out now

You can download these today. Central Production is free and runs in your browser.

Central Pedal Control

Out now

This is where the week's prep happens. Build the setlist, add songs and tracks, set up the pads and the instrument, and decide what each button on the pedal does. When you're done, press Upload and it all goes to the pedal.

See what it does

Central Control

Out now

Central Control runs the room from the desk: lights, lyrics, slides, cameras, the stream, in-ears and the run sheet. If you have more than one computer back there, give each one its own jobs and they work together.

See what it does

Central Usage

Out now

Central Usage is the app for the people on stage. Each musician and singer gets their own chord chart, the lyrics, their instrument sound and their own in-ear mix. It works at church and at home.

See what it does

Central Services

Out now

Central Services is the church's schedule. It shows the services, who's been asked, who said yes, who's away and what's being played, and everyone has it on their phone.

See what it does

Central Production

Out now

Central Production is a free studio for making your own sounds. Record a song, build a pad or an instrument from a few samples, or put together your own reverb. Then use it in your apps, or sell it on Make & sell.

See what it does

Coming

None of these are for sale yet, and some details will change before they ship.

Mid 2027

Central Pedal

Coming mid 2027

The pedal at the worship leader's feet. It plays your tracks and pads, shows the band the chart and the lyrics, and runs your guitar or keys rig, all from 8 footswitches and a 7-inch touch screen.

See what it does

July 2027

All three Central I/O boxes come out together on Kickstarter.

Central I/O Control Box

On Kickstarter, July 2027

The brain of Central I/O, at your sound desk. It puts every channel the pedal plays on its own output (The Pedal) and builds the band's in-ear mixes (The In-Ears).

See the Control Box

Central I/O Input Box

On Kickstarter, July 2027

8 inputs, for mics, instruments and sound boards without AES67. You set the gain, the pad and phantom power from Central Control.

See the specs

Central I/O Output Box

On Kickstarter, July 2027

8 balanced outputs, for the pedal's stems, in-ear mixes or the speakers in the room.

See the specs

February 2028

A software addition for the Control Box.

Central Wave

Coming February 2028

Every speaker in the room gets its own mix, timing and EQ. It's new software for the same Control Box.
