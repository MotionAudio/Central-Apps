What it's for

Central Services is your church's schedule. Schedulers make services and ask people from the worship and production teams; everyone sees who's serving and answers yes or no.

  • The church account, main admins and admins open on the whole schedule.
  • Up to four schedulers, picked by a main admin from the main admins and admins, make services and ask people. Until you pick them, the main admins and admins are the schedulers.
  • Everyone else opens on My requests and taps Accept or Decline.

Everyone sees every team: who's on it, their role, and who said yes or no. Requests go out by email too, so people who never open the app can still answer.

Slots

Central Services is sold in slots, not keys. Each person on your teams uses one slot. The church account doesn't use one. Take someone off every team and their slot is free for the next person.

  • Every pack comes with slots: Lite 15, Regular 18, Plus 20, Ultra 30 (Ultra can add 10 more for $35).
  • More slots are $5 each, paid once. A main admin or admin buys them on the website.
  • Schedulers see how many are in use at the top of the Teams screen.

Getting in

  1. Get the app for Windows, Mac, Linux or Android from the download page, or open Central Services on the website in any browser.
  2. Sign in with your Motion Audio account, the one from your church's invite. It knows who you are and which church you're in.
  3. Admins confirm their phone. On a new device, main admins and admins also get a 6-digit code by text. Add your mobile number the first time.

Not in a church yet? Ask your main admin to add you on the Teams page of the website, or join with your church's code when you make your account.

My requests

Everything you've been asked to serve at, with the date, the team, your role and the service's notes. Tap Accept or Decline, and add a note if you like ("I can do the second service only").

The day before, from 9 a.m. (or as soon as the service is less than 18 hours away), everyone who said yes gets "you're serving tomorrow" with their role and the notes, and anyone who hasn't answered gets a nudge. Nobody who said no gets one, and nobody gets two.

Schedule

Every upcoming service with its date, title and notes, its song list, who's away that day, and each team's people with their answers: yes, no or not yet. Schedulers also see Ask more people, ✕ to take someone off a service, Edit songs and Delete.

Make a service (schedulers)

  1. + New service. Give it a title, the date and time, and notes if you like.
  2. Add the songs, one a line: title, key and BPM. They're in the request email and the day-before reminder.
  3. Pick who to ask: whole teams or particular people. The screen shows who's away that day, and asking a whole team skips them.
  4. Save. Everyone asked gets an email and sees it in My requests.

Away

Mark the days you can't serve: a first day, a last day and a note ("Vacation", "Work trip"). Schedulers see who's away, and asking a whole team skips you on those days. A request you hadn't answered for one of those days turns into a no. Schedulers can mark someone else away too.

Teams

  • Up to four worship teams and four production teams.
  • Schedulers press + New worship team or + New production team, Rename or ✕ a team, and + Add people with a name, email and role (Bass, Lights…). ✕ next to a person takes them off the team.
  • A main admin chooses the schedulers here: up to four, from the main admins and admins.
  • At the top, Slots: how many are in use, how many are free, and a link for main admins and admins to add more on the website.

Account and app settings

Account has the privacy policy, Contact support (a real person answers by email) and Delete my account. In the apps, ⚙ App holds the website's address and updates: the computer apps can install a new version by themselves when they open.

Troubleshooting

  • "Your church doesn't have Central Services yet." A main admin or admin gets slots on the website: every pack comes with some, or $5 a person.
  • Every slot is in use. Take someone who no longer serves off the teams, or add slots on the website.
  • I didn't get the email. Check your spam folder, then open My requests in the app or on the website: everything's there too.
  • Forgot your password? Every sign-in has Forgot password?
