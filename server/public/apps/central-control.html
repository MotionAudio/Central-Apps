What it's for

Central Control is the app at the desk. The main admin and the admins use it to run lights, slides and lyrics, cameras, the mix, the in-ear mixes, talkback and the recording, all following the song that's playing.

The main admin's copy is the hub. Every other computer, phone, tablet and Central Pedal pairs with it, and it holds the team together: who has which role, which jobs each admin has, the service lock and the services with their run orders. With no Central Pedal at all, the hub plays the tracks, charts and lyrics itself in its player window.

  • Main admin (up to two per church): everything, plus the pairing PIN, the role PIN and the Devices tab where every device gets its role and jobs.
  • Admin: the tabs for the jobs the main admin gives it: Lighting, Production (cameras), Mix, In-ears, or Slides & lyrics. General means all of them.

Musicians and singers use Central Usage instead. It has their chart, lyrics, instrument and in-ear mix, and it can't be made an admin.

Your key and your computer

Central Control needs an A key (an admin key). Each one works on one computer at a time and comes with ten full releases of updates from the version you bought it at: buy at 9.4 and you get every update through 10.4. Renewing a key for $20 adds ten more. Central Control never stops working if you don't renew.

It runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), on Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel, and on Linux (Ubuntu, Debian and the like, or any Linux with the AppImage). See Install Central Control.

To move a key to another computer, use Release key in Settings → License, or release it on the website under Account → Your church.

First-time setup

Set up the main admin's computer first. It's usually the sound desk computer, and it has to be open during the service.

The main admin's computer (the hub)

  1. Install and open Central Control. The first time, it asks for your Motion Audio account and your A key. Sign in with the church's account or your own.
  2. Choose Main admin. Give the computer a name people will recognise, like Sound desk.
  3. Pick its inputs and outputs. Do this when the computer is the sound desk and has the audio interface.
  4. Choose two different 4-digit PINs. The pairing PIN is shared with everyone: every device types it to join. The role PIN is only yours: it's what moves a computer between the user side and the admin side.
  5. Add tracks. On the Devices tab, press ▶ Open the player on this computer, or pair a Central Pedal. The player window is the Central Pedal app built into Central Control; nothing else to install.

Every other admin computer

  1. Install Central Control and sign in with your account and your A key.
  2. Choose Admin and give the computer a name (Lights, Cameras…).
  3. Type the pairing PIN. It finds the main admin's computer on the network by itself. If it doesn't, type the hub's address: it's in the main admin's Settings → This computer.
  4. Wait for your jobs. Without the role PIN, the computer starts as User 1 and the main admin is asked to make it an admin. The main admin allows it on the Devices tab and picks its jobs.

Phones and tablets can open the user side straight from the hub: http://<hub address>:8095/cu/ in Safari or Chrome. Musicians get the full app with Central Usage.

The tabs

The tabs along the top are Devices, Service, Chord chart, Instrument, Lighting, Mix, In-ears, Lyrics, Slides, Video, I/O and ⚙ (Settings). An admin only sees the tabs for its jobs. F1–F9 switch tabs.

The top bar always has your countdown: to the service, and to your own next part (Lights: Sermon in 4:10, you're on), with a warning a minute before. Tap it for the run order with your parts marked.

Devices

The main admin's control room for the team.

  • Every device with its role and jobs, and how its connection is doing: the round trip to the pedal, the in-ear buffer and dropouts, CPU, battery and version, marked good, fair or poor. It shows which devices run Central Usage.
  • Next to each one: Change role, Jobs, Lock / Unlock, Rename and Remove. Remove sends a device back to its first-launch setup.
  • Talk group: who hears the main admin's Band talk or Production talk. Users start in the Band, admins in Production.
  • What each Central Pedal does: Track player plays the tracks and sends the charts and lyrics (one at a time; everyone follows it), Foot controller drives the track player from its footswitches and knobs, Instrument rig plays only its own instrument, and Backup player follows the track player silently and takes over if it stops. You can add as many pedals as you like.
  • ▶ Open the player on this computer: the hub plays the tracks itself when no Central Pedal does.
  • Service lock: one button locks every device, or one at a time. The I/O tab, settings, fixtures, lighting outputs and video sources close; everything used during the service keeps working.
  • Second main admin's computer: Keep … in sync. See The second main admin's computer.

Service

Up to four services, each with its day and time, the roles it uses and a run order: songs from the setlist, welcome, announcements, sermon, prayer and anything else, each with its minutes and the roles involved.

  • Start, Next, Back and Stop move the whole team through the run order. Everyone's countdown follows it, and the stage display shows the service countdown too.
  • Tick Record it and the audio host records every input and a stereo mix while the service runs.

Chord chart and Instrument

The Chord chart follows the song and the section the pedal is playing. Pick your instrument and it's transposed for it: concert pitch, guitar with a capo and chord shapes, B♭, E♭ and F horns, or Nashville numbers. Part shows any instrument's part instead: guitar (chart with capo and diagrams, or tab), bass, keys (both hands), vocals with the words under the notes, trumpet, clarinet, alto and tenor sax, horn in F, trombone, violin, cello and drums.

The Instrument tab is the Central Pedal's guitar, bass and keys rig running on this computer: the pedalboard, the keys sounds and layers, the tuner and the looper. Your own VST3 and CLAP plugins work on Windows, Mac and Linux, Audio Units on the Mac and LV2 on Linux: Add pedal → Your plugins for effects, and Piano & keys → Your instruments for Kontakt, Keyscape, Pianoteq and the like.

Lighting

A light controller that follows the song. It has three parts: Show, Fixtures and Output.

Show

  • The timeline shows the song's waveform, its sections in their colours, the hits as yellow lines, and your cues. Click the timeline to add a cue; drag a cue to move it. It snaps to the bar, beat, ½ beat or ¼ beat.
  • Each song has a mode: Auto (the sections and hits run the lights), Semi-auto (the same, with your own cues on top) or Manual (only your cues).
  • Tell it the show: type something like "dimmers blue 20% for the verse, then chorus full white, strobes on the hits" and it makes the presets and the cues.
  • ● Record: while the song plays (on the pedal, or ▶ Preview here), everything you press goes on the timeline. Record again over any part to replace just that part.
  • Lights: every light under the plot. Change one light's colour and level live, then + Cue with these colours.
  • Songs: every song on the pedal, with or without tracks, plus your own. A scene programmed at 93 BPM stays on its bar and beat when the song is played at 112.
  • Presets: colour, second colour, white, amber, UV, strobe, motion (effects, mover position, fan, phase) and timing.

Fixtures

The patch: each light's type from the fixture library (from QLC+ and Open Fixture Library), its universe, address and group, and where it sits on the stage plot.

Output

  • One DMX output per universe: USB-DMX (ENTTEC Pro and compatibles), Art-Net or sACN, with a monitor for each universe.
  • MIDI out to Lightkey, QLC+ or a lighting console, if you'd rather run the lights there.
  • The click: hear it while you program, have the lights follow it, and send it to an audio output.
  • Timecode (MTC or LTC) and the phone remote.

Mix

The mixer runs on one admin computer, the audio host: on the I/O tab, choose This computer is the audio host. It receives every track from the Central Pedal on its own channel, together with the click, the pads, the tracks mix and the instrument.

  • Every track has a strip: fader, meter, pan, mute, high-pass, a 3-band EQ with its curve and a compressor with a gain-reduction meter. Click a strip's name for the details.
  • Tracks out: summed to two outputs of the interface, or each track on its own output.
  • A track keeps its settings from song to song: "Drums" stays "Drums".
  • Listen here plays the main mix or any in-ear mix on this computer's headphones.
  • Record the service: every input of the interface or Dante (up to 128), the pedal's tracks as they arrive and a stereo program mix, one WAV per source at 48 kHz / 24-bit, in a folder named with the date, time and the service name. The program also becomes an MP3 for the sermon podcast. The button shows the time and the free disk space.
  • Save mixer… and Open mixer… keep the whole setup in a file.

Other admins with the Mix job mix the same mixer from their own computers.

In-ears

  • Channels (up to 128): each is a Central Pedal channel or an interface input, with a name, Tracks or Band, and whether users see it.
  • Mixes (up to 64, stereo): each has a name, an owner and where it goes. Wired goes out of the interface or Dante on the output pair you pick, to in-ear transmitters, with no delay; Auto-assign pairs numbers them in order. Local goes back over Wi-Fi to its owner's device. A mix can go both ways.
  • Below: the selected mix's send levels. You can listen to any mix, copy a mix or free it.

Talkback

Hold TALK to talk, or tap it once to keep it on. The main admin picks 🎸 Band or 🎬 Production, or ticks the mixes that should hear it, and can dim the music in their ears while talking. The talker's screen shows a red ON AIR. Everyone else's talkback goes only to the main admin's headphones.

Lyrics and slides

Lyrics follow the Central Pedal. Set the size, colour, alignment and a picture or video background. While the tracks play, paging stays inside the current section. ♫ Lyrics + chords from the vocal listens to the song's vocal track and writes a lyric chart with each chord on the word it lands on. It runs on this computer with an offline speech model (Settings → Lyrics from the vocal: Accurate, about 500 MB, or Fast, about 140 MB; downloaded once).

Slides: add pictures, videos and text slides. Click one to preview it; double-click it or press Go live to show it. The outputs are this computer's second screen, the Central Pedal's HDMI 2 (set it to Slides on the pedal's I/O tab) and NDI on the network. NDI needs the free NDI runtime from ndi.video.

Video

For the main admin and admins with the Production job. Up to 48 cameras: NDI, USB and HDMI capture, SRT, RTMP and RTSP, and phones. Main (full screen) and Stream outputs with Cut and Auto, a lyrics lower third and the slides, streaming to several places at once, NDI out for OBS or vMix, recording, and two stream audio sources from the interface or Dante.

I/O and Settings

I/O is laid out the way the signal flows: 1 In (the Central Pedal, its audio, MIDI) → 2 This computer (audio host, interface or Dante, listening) → 3 Out (screens and lights). Under Audio from the Central Pedal, pick the same connection the pedal uses: Ethernet (the best), Wi-Fi (a little more delay; set the buffer) or an audio interface cabled in.

Settings (⚙) has this computer's name and role, and on the main admin the pairing PIN, the role PIN and the hub's address. It also has the optional AI model for the lights, the speech model for lyrics, the license (with Release key and Contact support) and updates. Forget role puts a computer back to the first question.

The second main admin's computer

A church can have two main admins. The other main admin's computer can keep a full copy of the hub and take over whenever it needs to.

  1. On the hub, open Devices. Under Second main admin's computer, press Keep … in sync next to it. That computer has to be an admin with Central Control, and online.
  2. Wait for the first copy. It copies the song library and tracks, the lighting rig and cues, the slides, the mixer, the cameras, every paired device and the PINs. After that it copies only what changed, every minute. The Devices tab shows when it last did.
  3. To take over, on the in-sync computer open Settings and press Take over as the hub, then type the main admin's role PIN.
  • If the hub is on, it makes one last copy and hands over. The old hub becomes an admin computer and keeps itself in sync with the new one, so you can swap back any time.
  • If the hub is off or broken, it asks you to confirm and uses the last copy. When the old computer comes back, it sees the new hub and steps down by itself.
  • Every phone, tablet, computer and Central Pedal stays paired and finds the new hub by itself. Each person's own preferences, screens and microphone stay on their own computer.

Common tasks

Pair a new device

On the new device, choose its role and type the pairing PIN (main admin's Settings → This computer). A Central Pedal asks for the same PIN on its own screen. Then pick its role or job on the Devices tab.

Change the pairing PIN

Settings → This computer on the main admin. Devices that are online switch over by themselves; the others ask for the new PIN.

Lock everything for the service

Devices → Service lock. Unlock with the same button when the service is over. Anyone can also lock their own computer with 🔓 so nothing they set changes; hold 🔒 for two seconds to unlock.

Record the service for the podcast

On the audio host, press Record the service on the Mix tab, or tick Record it on the service. The stereo program becomes Program.mp3 when you stop.

Troubleshooting

  • "The main admin's computer was not found on this network." Check that Central Control is open on the hub and that both are on the same network. If the network blocks discovery, type the hub's address in Settings → This computer.
  • "The center on this computer did not start." Another program is using port 8095. Close it, or restart the computer, then open Central Control again.
  • Two computers both set up as main admin. On the one that shouldn't be, Settings → Forget role, and set it up as an admin.
  • No sound from the pedal in the mixer. The pedal's I/O tab and this computer's I/O tab must use the same connection (Ethernet, Wi-Fi or audio interface), and this computer must be the audio host.
  • Linux: the app won't open from the menu after an update. Run the install line again (see Linux). Since 9.3.1 the program is central-control; old menu entries and desktop icons are fixed when it installs.
  • Still stuck? Settings → License → Contact support. A real person on our team reads every message.
