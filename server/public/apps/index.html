The Central apps

Pads and tracks at the worship leader's feet, the service run from the desk, every musician's chart and mix on their own device, and the schedule for everyone.

Five apps, one team

Each app does one job for one kind of person. They find each other on the church network, so the chart on the bass player's phone, the lights on the stage and the lyrics on the screen all follow the song the pedal is playing.

Central Pedal

The worship leader or the keys player, on stage

A pedal you play with your feet. Pads, multitracks with the click and spoken cues, chord charts, lyrics and a guitar and keys rig, on a touch screen with 12 footswitches, knobs and an expression pedal. We build it on a Raspberry Pi 4 and ship it ready to play.

Central Pedal Control

Whoever gets the songs ready

The Central Pedal app on your computer, tablet or phone. Build the setlist, add songs and tracks, set up pads, footswitches and the instrument at home, then press Upload and it all goes to the pedal.

How it fits together on a Sunday

Here's a typical morning with every piece in use. You don't need all of it. With no pedal at all, the main admin's computer plays the tracks itself.

  1. TuesdayPlan the service

    A scheduler makes Sunday's service in Central Services, with its song list, and asks people from the worship and production teams. They get an email and tap yes or no.

  2. WednesdayGet the songs ready

    The worship leader opens Central Pedal Control at home, adds the songs (their own stems, a MultiTracks.com download or Central Tracks), checks the sections and sets the pads.

  3. Sunday, 8:00Upload to the pedal

    At church, Upload sends the setlist, tracks, pads and buttons to the Central Pedal. The pedal applies it when nothing is playing.

  4. Sunday, 8:15The desk comes up

    The main admin opens Central Control on the hub computer. Admins' computers for lights, cameras or the mix pair with it, and so do the pedals.

  5. Sunday, 8:30The band plugs in

    Musicians open Central Usage on a laptop, tablet or phone. Each picks a free in-ear mix and sees the chart for their instrument.

  6. Sunday, 10:00The service

    The pedal plays the tracks and sends each one to the desk on its own channel. The lights, lyrics, slides and charts follow every section.
