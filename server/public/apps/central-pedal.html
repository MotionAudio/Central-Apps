What it is

Central Pedal is the pedal on stage. It plays ambient pads and multitracks (stems, the click, spoken cues), shows chord charts and lyrics, and is a guitar, bass and keys rig, all from a touch screen and your feet.

We build it on a Raspberry Pi 4 and ship it ready to play, with its software installed. It boots straight into the Central Pedal app in about 15 seconds.

  • 12 footswitches, 6 knobs and 2 sliders, plus an optional expression pedal and a small touch screen.
  • Four outputs: tracks, pads, the click with spoken cues (for the band, never the house) and your guitar or keys.
  • Two HDMI ports for the app, lyrics, charts or the stage display, and USB for MIDI keyboards, foot controllers, a Stream Deck, an audio interface and USB sticks.

You get songs ready for it at home with Central Pedal Control, the same app on your computer, then Upload them.

Sounds you add

The pedal needs no key: it's yours, with every update, and its starter sounds are free. More pianos, synths, instrument sounds, plugins and pedals come with the sound plans. Each plan is a package key you get by email (it's on your account page too): on the pedal, open Settings → Packages → Add a package and paste it. A package key works on two devices at a time, so the same one can go on your pedal and your laptop.

Central Pedal Control still uses a P key, on two devices, with every update forever.

First time on

You set it up from your phone.

  1. Plug in the power and your outputs. A screen on HDMI 1 (the micro-HDMI port next to the power socket) is optional.
  2. On a phone, join the Wi-Fi "Central Pedal Setup". The pedal makes it whenever it has no network. The setup page opens by itself (if it doesn't, go to http://10.42.0.1).
  3. Pick the church Wi-Fi, the touch screen and the 4-digit code, then press Upload. If you use Central Control, type the main admin's code: that pairs the pedal with it. If not, pick any 4 digits. Central Pedal Control uses the same code to Upload songs.
  4. Wait for it to restart. The pedal joins the Wi-Fi, downloads the screen's driver and restarts once. If the Wi-Fi password was wrong, "Central Pedal Setup" comes back in about a minute and the page says why.
  5. With Central Control, the main admin then picks what the pedal does on the Devices tab: track player, foot controller, instrument rig or backup player.

To run the setup again (a new building, a new Wi-Fi): Settings → Wi-Fi → Turn the setup Wi-Fi on.

A Central Pedal on its own, with no Central Control, works too: it plays your tracks and pads and shows the charts and lyrics on its screens and on phones.

On stage: LIVE

LIVE (F8) is the screen for the service.

  • The setlist on the left (● means the song has its tracks). Tap a song to load it; while something plays, tap twice.
  • The song: its number, key, BPM, time left, what's next, and Click / Cues.
  • The sections in order, sized by length and coloured. The one playing fills up as it goes; tap one to jump there on the bar.
  • ◀ Prev section, ▶ Play / Pause, Next section ▶, ⟲ Vamp (repeat this section until pressed again), ■ Safe Stop (fades the tracks and pads out over 2.5 seconds; press again to stop at once) and Next song.
  • The pads (tap to crossfade), the instrument with the tuner, and the meters.

Before the service

  • Ready check looks at everything that could go wrong: the sound, the outputs (a click in the main mix is flagged), every song's tracks and sections, the temperature, power supply, storage and memory, and the pairing. Each problem has a button to fix it where it can.
  • 🔒 Lock for service locks the other tabs, the mixer and set-up actions. Playing, sections, pads, the instrument and the tuner keep working. Unlock with the pedal's pairing PIN.

After the service

📋 Report shows what happened: songs played, playing time, songs stopped early, safe stops, audio dropouts and the hottest temperature, every song's sections and how it ended, and everything minute by minute. ⬇ Download saves it as text.

Pads

Eight pads per song: tap to crossfade, tap again to fade out. Built-in pads play in any key with no samples; imported pads (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AIFF) have their key detected and are pitch-shifted to the song's key. Two texture channels with 100+ presets, effects on every pad, octave layers, auto-swell, freeze, and with LAYER on, up to four pads at once.

Footswitches, knobs and MIDI

Every footswitch, knob and slider has an action in the Pad layout and another in the Tracks layout, with a separate action when held. Change them on the Configure tab; the LEDs follow (on, off, or blinking on the beat).

  • Learn adds a new control: name it, press Learn, then press the pedal. It works with USB foot controllers, any HID pedal or keyboard key, and MIDI notes, CCs and program changes.
  • MIDI keyboards play the pads in that key, or the keys sounds on the Instrument tab. CC 64 holds freeze or sustain, and MIDI clock sets the tempo.
  • Elgato Stream Deck: Settings → Stream Deck → Connect a Stream Deck, with your own keys and pages.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: F1–F8 the tabs, 1–8 pads, Space stop (play / pause on Tracks), [ ] previous and next song, ← → sections.

A USB interface adds its outputs after those. Settings → Output routing moves any of them to any channel, or switches to stereo pairs, with a test tone for each.

  • HDMI 1 shows the app (or a big copy of the small touch screen with its four tabs, lyrics or charts). HDMI 2 shows lyrics for the projector by default, or charts, the stage display, slides from Central Control, or nothing. Settings → Screens.
  • The stage display shows the song, key and tempo, the section with a bar countdown, what's next, the lyrics and the beat, readable from across the stage.
  • Phones and tablets on the same Wi-Fi: open http://padpedal.local:8080 or scan the QR code in Settings, for charts, lyrics, a remote and an upload page. Viewing is free; controlling needs the pairing PIN.

The small touch screen

The 3.5" touch screen on the pedal has four tabs along its bottom edge. Tap a fader or drag along it; Restart and Shut down ask for a second tap.

TabWhat's on it
PadsThe 8 pads and Stop, with Tap, Freeze, Key, Layer and Next song (or the transport when tracks are on).
TracksPlay: the song's sections, play / pause, start, next section, repeat, click, key, next song. Mixer: the tracks and click levels, and every track's level and mute. Setlist: tap a song to load it.
InstrumentSound: electric, acoustic, bass, keys or off, the board or keys sound, the level. Pedals: every pedal on the board, on or off. EQ · Comp: the EQ, compressor and studio pedals' knobs as faders. Tuner · Level: the tuner, the instrument level, the pads volume, Auto set-up.
SettingsIn & out: input gain, input L or R, the instrument level, and which output each of tracks, pads, click and instrument goes to. Screens: what HDMI 1 and HDMI 2 show. Touch: which touch screen this is, calibrate the touch, flip it 180° for a screen mounted upside down. System: the version, address and pairing code; calibrate the touch, restart, shut down.

HDMI 1 and HDMI 2 show what Settings → Screens says. Pick Copy of the small touch screen for the same four tabs, big, on a monitor or TV.

Updates, backups and power cuts

  • Updates: the pedal looks each time it starts and installs a new version after a 10-second notice, never while something plays. Or Settings → Software updates → Install. Every download is checked before it's installed, and your songs and settings stay.
  • No internet at church? Put the newest central-control-source.zip from the download page on a USB stick and use Settings → Update software from USB….
  • Back up to USB stick saves your songs and their audio, charts, lyrics, pads, sounds, light shows, setlists and settings. Restore from USB… puts a backup back.
  • Power-cut safe mode is on by default: the system runs read-only from memory, so pulling the plug can't damage it. Your songs and settings are on a separate part of the card that keeps saving normally.

Troubleshooting

  • Blurry text on the screen. Settings → Screens → Resolution → Automatic, or the one marked native.
  • The edges are cut off on a TV. Turn on the TV's "Just scan", "1:1" or "Screen fit" setting, or use Edges cut off? in Settings → Screens.
  • A small touch screen on the pins stays white. Its driver isn't on yet. With nothing on HDMI and no screen picked, the pedal turns on the common 3.5" screen by itself and restarts once. If it stays white, open the pedal from a phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi (http://padpedal.local:8080, or its address with :8080), go to Settings → Touch screen, pick your screen (try 3.5" RPi Display – XPT2046, then Waveshare 3.5" (A), then (B)) and restart. You can also do it with an HDMI monitor plugged in.
  • No picture. Use the micro-HDMI port next to the power socket for HDMI 1. The pedal waits up to 20 seconds for a slow monitor and lays out a screen plugged in later by itself.
  • Ready check says the click is in the main mix. On a two-output device the congregation would hear it. Use a 4-output interface, or route the click to its own output.
  • Wi-Fi won't connect. Settings → Wi-Fi says why (wrong password, out of range). Hidden networks are under Other network…. Check the Wi-Fi country too. Or set it up again from your phone: Turn the setup Wi-Fi on and join "Central Pedal Setup".
  • A package won't add. A package key works on two devices at a time: release it on one (Settings → Packages, or on your account page on the website). If it says the package ended, renew it on your account page; a key from your church that was taken back stops working, so ask your main admin for yours.
  • Upload from Central Pedal Control can't find it. Both need to be on the same network; type padpedal.local or the pedal's address, and its pairing PIN.
