What it is

Central Pedal is the pedal on stage. It plays your pads and multitracks (stems, the click and spoken cues), shows chord charts and lyrics, loops, and turns into a guitar, bass or keys rig. You run all of it with your feet and a small touch screen.

We build each one on a Raspberry Pi 4, put the software on it, test it and ship it ready to play. When you switch it on it goes straight into the Central Pedal app.

  • 12 footswitches, 6 knobs and 2 sliders, plus a socket for an expression pedal and a 3.5" touch screen.
  • Outputs 1 and 2 on the Pi's headphone jack, 3 and 4 on the DAC board if yours has one, and more from a USB audio interface. You choose what goes where.
  • Two HDMI ports for the app, lyrics, charts or the stage display. USB takes MIDI keyboards, foot controllers, a Stream Deck, an audio interface and USB sticks.

If you have a computer, Central Pedal Control keeps your songs and plays the tracks, and the pedal becomes its foot controller. Without one, the pedal plays everything itself.

Sounds you add

The pedal needs no key. It's yours, with every update, and the starter sounds are free.

The sound plans add pianos, synths, instruments, effects, guitar pedals and pads. Each plan comes as a package key by email, and it's under Purchases on your account too. On the pedal, go to Settings → Packages → Add a package and paste it, then Libraries → Install.

One package key works on three of your devices, each a different app: say your pedal, your Central Control computer and your Central Usage tablet. Every plan includes Central Pads, pads in every key made for this pedal.

First time on

Your pedal arrives with its card in and its software on it. You set it up from your phone.

  1. Plug in the power and your outputs. A screen on HDMI 1 (the micro-HDMI port next to the power socket) is optional.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi "Central Pedal Setup". The pedal makes it the first time it starts. The setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, go to http://10.42.0.1.
  3. Pick the church Wi-Fi and type its password.
  4. Type a 4-digit code. If your church uses Central Control, type the main admin's code so the pedal pairs with it. If not, pick any four digits. Central Pedal Control uses the same code.
  5. Press Upload. The pedal joins the Wi-Fi and it's ready. If the password was wrong, "Central Pedal Setup" comes back after about a minute and the page tells you why.

To run the setup again, for a new building or a new Wi-Fi: Settings → Wi-Fi → Turn the setup Wi-Fi on.

Setlists and songs

Open Setlist and you'll see three buttons:

  • Load setlist shows the setlists your church has made before. Tap one to open it.
  • Create new: give it a name and a day, and add the people playing. You can search your Central Services teams.
  • Add songs opens the website on that setlist. On the pedal it shows a QR code you scan with your phone. Songs you own go straight in. With Central Tracks Unlimited any song does. Anything else you buy first.

If a computer runs Central Pedal Control or Central Control, it keeps the songs and plays them, and the pedal only stores what it's playing. With no computer, the pedal keeps one setlist at a time. When you open another one, it clears the old songs, except any that only exist on the pedal.

When the song or the section changes, the lyrics and charts change with it on every screen.

On stage: LIVE

LIVE (F8) is the screen for the service.

  • The setlist is on the left. A ● means the song has its tracks. Tap a song to load it; while something is playing, tap it twice.
  • The song: its number, key, tempo, time left, what's next, and Click / Cues.
  • The sections in order, sized by length. The one playing fills up as it goes. Tap one to jump there on the next bar.
  • ◀ Prev section, ▶ Play / Pause, Next section ▶, ⟲ Vamp (repeat this section until you press it again), ■ Safe Stop (fades tracks and pads out over 2.5 seconds; press again to stop at once) and Next song.
  • The pads, the instrument with its tuner, and the meters.

Tracks play without pads, and pads play without tracks. Use whichever you need.

Before the service

  • Ready check looks for anything that could go wrong: the sound, the outputs (it warns you if the click would reach the room), every song's tracks and sections, temperature, power, storage and pairing. Most problems have a button to fix them.
  • 🔒 Lock for service locks the other tabs and the set-up actions. Playing, sections, pads, the instrument and the tuner keep working. Unlock it with the pedal's pairing PIN.

After the service

📋 Report shows how it went: the songs played, songs stopped early, safe stops, audio dropouts, the hottest temperature, and everything minute by minute. ⬇ Download saves it as a text file.

Pads and effects

Each song has eight pads. Tap one to crossfade to it, tap it again to fade it out. Each pad has its own volume slider, and sliding it never starts the pad.

The built-in pads play in any key. A pad you import (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A or AIFF) has its key detected and is shifted to the song's key. Under the pads are two texture channels (rain, drones, risers and more), plus octave layers, auto-swell and freeze. Turn on LAYER to play up to four pads at once.

Effects on tracks and pads

Every track, every pad, and the whole tracks and pads groups can have a gate, EQ, compressor, reverb and delay. The basic version of each is free. Central Plugins adds more EQ bands, full compressor controls, bigger reverbs, tempo delays and the pro presets. A track's effects are worked out once and saved, so they cost the Pi nothing while it plays.

Looper

LOOPER (F9) turns the pedal into a loop pedal with three loops. Each loop can record, overdub, undo, redo, play reversed or at half speed, and has its own level and pan. It records the instrument, the audio input, or both.

  • One footswitch does it all. Tap to record, tap to play, tap again to overdub. Double-tap stops, and holding it for a second clears the loop.
  • In time with the song. While tracks or the click are playing, a loop is a set number of bars and starts on the bar line. With nothing playing, you tap to start and tap again to close the loop. The first loop sets the length and the others follow it.
  • The looper has its own row in Settings → Output routing. It never stops your tracks or pads.

Footswitches, knobs and MIDI

Every footswitch, knob and slider can do anything in the app: play a pad, switch tabs, move around the screen, set a level, mute a group, run the looper, change a pedal on your board. Each has one job in the Pad layout and another in the Tracks layout, plus a separate job when you hold it. Set them on the Configure tab. The LEDs follow along (on, off, or blinking with the beat).

Adding your own controls

Go to Learn → Add new control, pick Button, Knob or Slider, give it a name, then:

  • for a button: press it once;
  • for a knob: turn it all the way left, then all the way right;
  • for a slider: slide it all the way down, then all the way up.

It's saved under its name, and you can give it any job on the Configure tab. This works with MIDI foot controllers and footswitches, USB pedals, and any key on a computer keyboard.

  • MIDI keyboards play the pads in that key, or the keys sounds on the Instrument tab. CC 64 holds freeze or sustain, and MIDI clock sets the tempo.
  • Stream Deck: Settings → Stream Deck → Connect a Stream Deck, then lay out your own keys and pages.
  • Keyboard: F1–F9 switch tabs, 1–8 play pads, Space stops (or plays and pauses on Tracks), [ ] go to the previous and next song, and ← → move between sections.

The colours are yours to change too: Settings → Colours, with a few ready-made looks and a button to go back to ours.

Outputs and sound

OutputsWhere
1 and 2The Pi's 3.5 mm headphone jack (left and right)
3 and 4The DAC board, if your pedal has one
5 and upA USB audio interface, as soon as it's plugged in

In Settings → Output routing you send the tracks, pads, click, instrument and looper to any output, on one channel or a stereo pair. Several can share an output. The quick set-ups are 4 mono outputs, 2 stereo outputs and Everything on 1–2. Anything sent to an output that isn't there plays on 1–2 instead, and Settings tells you.

A USB audio interface works both ways. Its outputs become 5 and up, or you can make it the main output. Its inputs can feed the instrument and the pads' audio input; pick which inputs in Settings → Audio.

Settings → Audio → Test sound plays a tone on each output, shows where the sound is going (and fixes it if it's wrong), and can play a tone straight from the Pi to check the cable and headphones.

Screens

  • HDMI 1 shows the app, a big copy of the 3.5" screen, lyrics or charts. HDMI 2 shows lyrics for the projector, or charts, the stage display, slides from Central Control, or nothing. Choose in Settings → Screens.
  • The stage display shows the song, key and tempo, the section with a bar countdown, what's next, the lyrics and the beat, big enough to read across the stage.
  • Phones and tablets on the same Wi-Fi can open http://padpedal.local:8080 (or scan the QR code in Settings) for charts, lyrics, a remote and an upload page. Anyone can look; changing things needs the pairing PIN.
  • Each musician picks how they read the chart: chords, numbers or sheet music, for their own instrument and clef, with a capo if they want one. Charts follow key and tempo changes.

The 3.5" touch screen

The touch screen on the pedal has five tabs along the bottom. Tap a fader or drag along it. Restart and Shut down ask you to tap twice.

TracksPlay: the sections and the transport. Mixer: each track's level and mute. Setlist: go to a song, start the set, add songs from an A–Z list, change the order, or open another setlist with SETS. Tempo: tempo, tap, the click and its level, voice cues and count-in.

If taps land in the wrong place, go to Settings → Touch → Calibrate. Tap the five crosses (the four corners and the middle), then tap the dot to check it, and press Save. Test touch shows dots where you touch, so you can see how it's doing.

Updates, backups and power cuts

  • Updates: the pedal checks each time it starts and installs a new version after a 10-second notice, never while something is playing. You can also go to Settings → Software updates → Install. Every download is checked before it's installed, and your songs and settings stay.
  • No internet at church? Put the newest pedal update from the Downloads page on a USB stick and use Settings → Update software from USB….
  • Back up to USB stick saves your songs and their audio, charts, lyrics, pads, sounds, light shows, setlists and settings. Restore from USB… puts a backup back.
  • Power-cut safe mode is on from the start. The system runs read-only from memory, so pulling the plug can't damage it. Your songs and settings live on a separate part of the card that saves as normal.

Troubleshooting

  • It says it's playing but there's no sound. Go to Settings → Audio → Test sound → Check where the sound goes. It shows where the sound is going and fixes it if it can. If Play on each sound card is silent on the headphone jack too, check the headphones, the cable and the socket.
  • Taps on the 3.5" screen are off, or light taps are missed. Calibrate it: Settings → Touch → Calibrate.
  • The start-up report says "under-voltage", or the pedal feels slow. The power supply is too weak. Use the official Raspberry Pi 5.1 V 3 A supply.
  • Blurry text on the screen. Settings → Screens → Resolution → Automatic, or the one marked native.
  • The edges are cut off on a TV. Turn on the TV's "Just scan", "1:1" or "Screen fit" setting, or use Edges cut off? in Settings → Screens.
  • The 3.5" screen stays white. Its driver isn't on yet. From a phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi, open http://padpedal.local:8080, go to Settings → Touch screen, pick your screen (try 3.5" RPi Display – XPT2046 first, then Waveshare 3.5" (A), then (B)) and restart. An HDMI monitor works for this too.
  • No picture. Use the micro-HDMI port next to the power socket for HDMI 1. A monitor plugged in later is picked up by itself.
  • Wi-Fi won't connect. Settings → Wi-Fi tells you why (wrong password, out of range). Hidden networks are under Other network…. You can also start over from your phone: Turn the setup Wi-Fi on and join "Central Pedal Setup".
  • A package key won't add. It works on three of your devices, each a different app, so it can't go on two pedals. Release it from one (Settings → Packages, or Purchases on the website). If it says the plan ended, renew it on the website. If your church gave you the key and took it back, ask your main admin.
  • Central Pedal Control can't find the pedal. Both need to be on the same network. Type padpedal.local or the pedal's address, and its pairing PIN.
  • Still stuck? The pedal writes a start-up report to its card, central-pedal-log.txt. Put the card in a computer, open the "bootfs" drive, and send us that file from Talk to us.
