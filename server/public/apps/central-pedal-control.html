What it's for

Central Pedal Control is the Central Pedal app away from the pedal. Make the setlist, add songs and tracks, and set up the pads, footswitches, knobs and the instrument at home or in the office. Then press ⇪ Upload and it all goes to the pedal on the church Wi-Fi.

It's the same app the pedal runs, with the same tabs, so what you see here is what you'll see on stage. You can rehearse on its LIVE screen too.

Your key and your devices

It needs a P key, which gets every update forever. A P key works on two devices, both signed in with the same account. Every pack comes with one, or it's $50 on its own.

Upload and Download

  1. Get it ready at home: the setlist, the songs and their tracks, the pads, the footswitches and knobs, the instrument.
  2. At church, join the same Wi-Fi as the pedal, or use a network cable.
  3. Press ⇪ Upload (top right, File → Upload…, or Ctrl/⌘+U). Type the pedal's name or address (for example padpedal.local) and its pairing PIN.
  4. Tick what to send: songs and their tracks, pads and sounds, buttons and MIDI, instrument, setups, colours, the Stream Deck, the network audio and backup player settings, and the practice-mix and stem splitter settings. Outputs and screens only when your computer's outputs match the pedal's.
  5. On the pedal, a bar says what arrived: Apply now, Later or Discard. It never applies while something plays; it waits. Songs replace the pedal's setlist, or are added to it.

⇩ Download (Ctrl/⌘+D) does the opposite: the pedal's songs and setup come to your computer.

● Live on the pedal

Press ● Live (top right), type the pedal's name or address and its pairing PIN, then Go live. You are working the pedal itself, over the network: every tap and fader changes the pedal straight away, with no Upload. The same four tabs as the pedal's small touch screen are there: pads; tracks (sections and transport, the mixer with every track's level and mute, the setlist); the instrument (sound, pedals, EQ and compressor, tuner and level); and inputs and outputs. A network cable or 5 GHz Wi-Fi keeps it quickest. Close goes back to editing.

LIVE

The screen for the service: the setlist, the song's sections in colour, the transport with Vamp and Safe Stop, the pads and the instrument. It works exactly as on the pedal; see LIVE on the Central Pedal.

Pad

  • 8 pads per song: tap to crossfade, tap again to fade out. STOP, key − / +, tap tempo, LAYER (up to 4 pads at once), morph and FREEZE.
  • Built-in pads play in any key. Imported pads (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AIFF) have their key detected and are pitch-shifted to the song's key; set the root key yourself if it guessed wrong.
  • Textures: two channels with 100+ presets (rain, vinyl, choir air, tape hiss, shimmer beds…), each with its own level.
  • Effects on every pad: 6-band EQ, compressor, drive, auto-filter, phaser, chorus, tremolo, stereo width, tempo-synced delay, reverb with shimmer and an LFO.
  • Scenes (in the song editor) save the pads, the track mix and the instrument, and recall them on a section, a button or a MIDI cue, with a smooth, swell or cut transition.

Tracks

Add songs

+ Song gives you four choices:

  • My own (MP3 / WAV): one mixed file or a set of stems. Your own files stay on your devices and are never shared.
  • Multitrack: a MultiTracks.com download or any zip or folder of stems. Stems are recognised by name, the sections come from the Ableton Live set or MIDI file in the download, and Combine stems of the same kind makes, say, three drum stems one Drums track. Several songs… imports a whole folder at once.
  • Prime track: a Loop Community song's WAV stems.
  • Central Tracks: the Motion Audio library. Subscribers press Add to storage; otherwise Buy on the website.

Planning Center plans come in too: connect once with a Personal Access Token, pick a plan, and its songs arrive with their key, tempo, arrangement, chart, lyrics and audio.

After every import

Tempo and key are detected (with the runner-up guesses one tap away), along with the beat grid, the sections (intro, verse, chorus, bridge…) and the big hits. A click and a spoken guide are made from the grid if the song doesn't have them.

Playing a song

  • Any key, any tempo, changed on the fly with the stems locked together. Tempo and time-signature changes inside a song live in the song editor.
  • Flow: put the sections in the order you'll play them (Intro, V1, C, C, Bridge, C…). Live, hold a section button to go there next, ⟲ again to repeat, ⤼ skip to drop the next.
  • Mixer: a strip for every track with fader, meter, mute, solo and pan, plus the Tracks, Click and Cues masters.
  • When the song ends: stop, go to the next song ready, play the next after a gap, or segue straight into it (gapless or crossfaded).
  • Click and spoken cues ("Chorus", "Bridge", "1 2 3 4") go to output 3 for the band. Record your own cue words in your voice in the song editor.
  • Chord charts: upload a PDF or text chart, or press Listen & write chord chart. Sheet music… writes what one stem plays as notation, tab, a piano roll or Nashville numbers. Parts for every instrument makes a part for each player.
  • MIDI out: hits for the lights on every accent and section, the drum hits of split songs, program changes and clock. The same go over Wi-Fi to Central Control.

The stem splitter

Tracks → ✂ Stem splitter turns one WAV of a whole song into the tracks a band plays with: Lead Vox, BGVs, Drums, Perc, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Synth & Pads, plus a Click and a Guide. Press Install the first time (about 2.5 GB, inside the app's own folder, downloaded once); Remove it takes it all away. It runs on your computer, and an Apple Silicon Mac or an NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster. Check the tracks before adding them: play, solo, rename, merge or split one again.

Practice mixes

Tracks → 🎧 Practice mixes makes a mix for every musician: their part up, alone or left out, slower without changing the pitch or in another key, with a count-in. They're MP3s when ffmpeg is installed (the pedal has it; on Linux sudo apt install ffmpeg, on a Mac brew install ffmpeg), otherwise WAVs.

Instrument

  • Electric and acoustic guitar: the input, gain and noise gate, then a pedalboard drawn on screen. Tap a pedal to switch it, open it for its knobs, drag to reorder, Sync a delay to the tempo. 13 board presets (Clean Worship, Ambient Swell, Edge Dotted-Eighth…) and your own boards.
  • Piano & keys: a USB MIDI keyboard plays pianos, electric pianos, organs, pads, strings, basses and more, through the same pedals. Up to 8 layers with their own volume, pan, octave, key range (Split at C4 in one tap) and velocity range. Add your own .sf2 SoundFonts or folders of WAV samples.
  • Your own plugins on a computer: VST3 and CLAP on Windows, Mac and Linux, Audio Units on the Mac, LV2 on Linux. Add pedal → Your plugins and Piano & keys → Your instruments, each with its own window.
  • Mix and Level on every pedal, an IR loader for cabinets and acoustic bodies, a looper (REC / PLAY / DUB on one footswitch, in time with the song or free), the tuner and Auto, which listens and sets gain, gate and a board for you.

Configure and Learn

Configure lists every physical control with what it does in the Pad layout and in the Tracks layout, and a hold action for each button. Pick from the action list: play, sections, songs, scenes, pads, the instrument's pedals and boards, the tuner, the looper, the service lock, Ready check and more. The LEDs follow the mapping.

Learn: Add new button → name it → Learn → press the pedal. It works with the pedal's own pins, USB foot controllers and MIDI notes, CCs and program changes. Control board inputs finds the knobs, sliders and buttons on the board for you; Reset puts the defaults back.

I/O and Settings

I/O has every input and output on one page: Audio to Central Control (off, Ethernet, Wi-Fi or an audio interface; every track on its own channel), the four outputs and per-track direct outputs, inputs, MIDI, screens and the devices on the network.

Settings: colour presets or your own colours and the interface size; the audio engine (sample rate and latency); output routing and the output device; MIDI in; tempo, Ableton Link and MIDI clock; the Stream Deck; ProPresenter (each section can show a slide); the backup player; and software updates.

Setlists on every device

Every Central Pedal and every copy of Central Pedal Control can keep the same song library and setlists. Open Setlist → Setlists · Song library · Devices…:

  • Setlists: every setlist from every device, newest first. Open one, or + New setlist.
  • Song library: every song with its key, tempo, tracks and the setlists it's in. Delete… asks on this device or everywhere.
  • Devices: Link… once with the other one's pairing code. After that they copy each other's songs and setlists every few seconds, and the newest edit wins. If your network blocks discovery, use Link by address….

Troubleshooting

  • Upload can't reach the pedal. Both on the same network? Type padpedal.local or the pedal's IP address, and its pairing PIN from the pedal's Settings.
  • The pedal didn't change after Upload. Look for the bar on the pedal: press Apply now. It waits while something plays.
  • The stem splitter is slow. Studio mode runs several models one after the other, so it takes several times as long as Best. A Mac with Apple Silicon or an NVIDIA card helps a lot.
  • No mouse pointer. Update to 9.3.1 or newer: the pointer is only hidden on the pedal's own touch screen now.
  • "This key is now used on another device." A P key works on two devices. Release one in Settings → License, or on the website.
