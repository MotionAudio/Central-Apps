Central Pedal

The pedal at the worship leader's feet

A floor unit that plays your multitracks and pads, shows the band the lyrics and the chart, and runs your guitar or keys rig and a looper. It has a 7-inch touch screen, a 5-inch screen you can read from a step back, 8 footswitches, 14 knobs and 4 sliders, and a little screen beside each control that says what it does. We build and test each one. It's coming in the middle of 2027.

On stage

  • The song you're onLIVE shows the setlist, the song's key, tempo and time left, and each section as a button, in type big enough to read from where you stand.
  • Jump to any sectionTap a section on the screen or step through them with a footswitch. Vamp keeps the section going until you let it go, and Safe Stop fades everything out instead of cutting it.
  • A label beside every controlEach footswitch, knob and slider has a little screen next to it that says what it does right now. Give a switch a new job and its label changes too. There are 30 on top and 8 more on the back by the sockets.
  • Two screensThe 7-inch is the touch screen. The 5-inch is for reading from a step back: the section and time left, the lyrics, the chart or the setlist. You can move any page from one screen to the other.
  • Lock it for the serviceOne button locks the settings so nobody changes something by accident mid-song. Ready check looks over the setup before you start.

Tracks

  • Multitracks with a click and cuesYour stems play with a click and spoken cues like "Verse" and "Build". Each stem has its own fader with mute, solo and pan.
  • Your own songsBring MP3s or WAVs on a USB stick, send them from Central Pedal Control, or add them from Central Tracks. If all you've got is a stereo mix, the stem splitter pulls it apart.
  • Any key, any tempoPlay a song in a different key or at a different speed and it still sounds right. The chart changes with it.
  • It learns the songWhen a song comes in, the pedal finds the tempo, the beat grid, the key and the big hits, then lays out the sections. You can fix any of it in the song editor.
  • Songs flow into each otherAt the end of a song it can stop, load the next one, or go straight into it with no gap.

Pads and effects

  • Pads in all 12 keysWith Central Pads and Central Pads Extra you get 82 of them. Some are warm, some airy, some are choir or strings, and six of them move in time with the song.
  • Morph, freeze and layerStack up to four pads, blend slowly from one to another, or freeze the sound and let it hang while you talk.
  • Effects you can seeEach pad has its own EQ, compressor, drive, filter, phaser, chorus, tremolo, width, delay and reverb, with an LFO to move them. The editor draws each effect while the pad plays.
  • Your own soundsLoad an MP3 or WAV from a USB stick and it becomes a pad. The pedal works out what key it's in, so it stays in tune with every song.
  • Textures underneathTwo texture channels play under the pads. Think rain, vinyl crackle, or a drone that follows the key.

Instrument and looper

  • Plug in a guitar or bassINST A and B take a guitar or bass through real pedals, amps and cabinets. Every knob is on the screen, and you can put any of them on the pedal's own knobs. The tuner is one tap away.
  • Keys from a USB keyboardPlay pianos and synths with up to eight layers, each with its own split, octave and velocity range.
  • A looper with eight loopsRecord in time with the song or free, then reverse a loop, drop it to half speed or mute it.
  • Set the controls your wayThe 8 footswitches, 14 knobs and 4 sliders can each do whatever you want, with a second job when you hold them. MIDI controllers, a Stream Deck or a computer keyboard can learn any job too.

Sound and power

  • Six outputsSend the click and cues to the in-ears, the music to front of house and the pads somewhere else. You pick where each part goes in Settings.
  • A battery insideIf someone kicks the power cable out mid-song, the pedal keeps playing. It runs from a 45 W USB-C power supply.
  • With the rest of your teamCentral Control can get the lyrics and the chart from the pedal, and every track on its own channel over the network for the in-ear mixes. Central Pedal Control gets the songs ready at home and uploads them with the pedal's PIN.
  • It updates itselfEvery few hours it checks for an update and installs it during a quiet minute. It never updates while something is playing. You can also update it from a USB stick.

The other apps

They're separate apps, but on the same Wi-Fi they find each other and share the work.

Central Pedal Control

Get the pedal ready from home

This is where the week's prep happens. Build the setlist, add songs and tracks, set up the pads and the instrument, and decide what each button on the pedal does. When you're done, press Upload and it all goes to the pedal.

See what it does

Central Control

The desk runs the whole service

Central Control runs the room from the desk: lights, lyrics, slides, cameras, the stream, in-ears and the run sheet. If you have more than one computer back there, give each one its own jobs and they work together.

See what it does

Central Usage

Each musician's chart, sound and mix

Central Usage is the app for the people on stage. Each musician and singer gets their own chord chart, the lyrics, their instrument sound and their own in-ear mix. It works at church and at home.
