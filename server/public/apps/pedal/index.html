Central Pedal

The pedal at the worship leader's feet

Pads, multitracks, chord charts, lyrics, a looper and a guitar and keys rig, in one floor unit with 12 footswitches, knobs, a touch screen and two HDMI ports. We build it, test it and ship it ready to play.

Everything Central Pedal does

On stage: LIVETracksPads and effectsLooper and instrumentScreens and sound

On stage: LIVE

  • The song you're on, and what's nextKey, BPM, time left and the next song, big enough to read from where you stand.
  • Sections you can jump toTap a section, or step with a footswitch. Vamp repeats the section; Safe stop fades out on the bar.
  • Next song crossfadesThe pad moves into the next song's key and tempo without a gap.
  • Lock for serviceOne button keeps fingers off the settings during the service.

Tracks

  • Multitracks with click and spoken cuesPlay your stems with a click and cues like "Verse", "Chorus" and "Build". Mute or turn down any stem live.
  • Your own songsAdd MP3s or WAVs from a USB stick, from Central Pedal Control, or from Central Tracks.
  • Change key and tempoEvery song can play in another key or at another tempo, and the chart follows.
  • Tracks or pads, or bothTracks play without pads, and pads play without tracks.

Pads and effects

  • Pads in every key82 pads in all 12 keys with Central Pads and Central Pads Extra: warm, airy, choir, strings, shimmer and more, and six that move in time with the song.
  • Morph, freeze and swellBlend between two pads with the expression pedal, freeze the sound, or swell into the next section.
  • Effects on every stem and padEQ, compressor, reverb and delay come free; the plans add better ones. Plugins you make yourself play here too.
  • A volume for every padA slider on each pad, plus bus levels and mutes from the footswitches.

Looper and instrument

  • A three-loop looperRecord, overdub and stop three loops, from the 3.5" screen, the HDMI screen or Central Pedal Control.
  • A guitar and keys rigPlug in a guitar or a USB keyboard and play through pedal boards and the keys sounds.
  • Footswitches, knobs and MIDI12 buttons, 6 knobs and 2 sliders do whatever you set: tabs, moving around the screen, bus levels, any action.

Screens and sound

  • A 3.5" touch screenPads, tracks, effects, setlists, scenes and the looper, right at your feet. It shows mouse clicks from HDMI too.
  • Two HDMI portsEach port shows its own choice: the full app, the lyrics for the room, or the chart.
  • Outputs your wayHeadphone jack on 1-2, a DAC board on 3-4 and a USB audio interface after that. Each bus goes where you want.
  • Updates itselfIt looks every few hours and installs in a quiet minute, never while something plays. Updates from USB work too.
