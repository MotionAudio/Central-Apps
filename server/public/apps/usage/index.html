Central Usage

Each musician's chart, sound and mix

Central Usage is the app for the people on stage. Each musician and singer gets their own chord chart, the lyrics, their instrument sound and their own in-ear mix. It works at church and at home.

MusicianSinger
 per musicianWhat it does

Each key works on two devices, so a player can have one at church and one at home.

What Central Usage does

Charts and lyricsYour soundStudio and controls

Charts and lyrics

  • A chart that keeps upIt highlights where the band is in the song. It's in your key and your clef, and it shows your instrument's part.
  • The chart you read bestChords over the words, Nashville numbers, a lead sheet or notation.
  • Lyrics for the singersThe words move along with the song and the next lines come up before you need them.

Your sound

  • Your own in-ear mixTurn the tracks and the band up or down from your own phone or tablet.
  • Play an instrumentPlug in a keyboard or a guitar. There are 136 sounds built in, and the plans add more.
  • Plugins you already ownOn a computer you can use your own VST3, Audio Units, CLAP and LV2 plugins.

Studio and controls

  • StudioRecord ideas and loops. It runs on the same engine as Central Production, and Open in Central Production takes your work straight there.
  • Learn any controlMIDI pedals, footswitches, keyboards and Stream Deck all work. Press Learn and move the control.
  • Two people, one deviceUser 1 and User 2 can share a computer, each with their own part and mix.
  • Change the coloursYou can change any colour in the app.

Central Pedal

The pedal at the worship leader's feet

A floor unit that plays your multitracks and pads, shows the band the lyrics and the chart, and runs your guitar or keys rig and a looper. It has a 7-inch touch screen, a 5-inch screen you can read from a step back, 8 footswitches, 14 knobs and 4 sliders, and a little screen beside each control that says what it does. We build and test each one. It's coming in the middle of 2027.

See what it does

Central Pedal Control

Get the pedal ready from home

This is where the week's prep happens. Build the setlist, add songs and tracks, set up the pads and the instrument, and decide what each button on the pedal does. When you're done, press Upload and it all goes to the pedal.

Central Control

The desk runs the whole service

Central Control runs the room from the desk: lights, lyrics, slides, cameras, the stream, in-ears and the run sheet. If you have more than one computer back there, give each one its own jobs and they work together.
