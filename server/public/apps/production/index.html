Central Production

Make tracks, pads, sounds and plugins

Central Production is a free studio for making your own sounds. Record a song, build a pad or an instrument from a few samples, or put together your own reverb. Then use it in your apps, or sell it on Make & sell.

What Central Production does

Recording and editingGetting a song ready for the bandPluginsGetting your work out

Recording and editing

  • Start from what you're makingFile → New project asks what you're making: a track, a loop, a sample, a pad, an instrument, a plugin or a pedal board.
  • Multitrack recordingCount-in, punch in and out, and loop takes. Each track can use its own input on your audio interface, and it measures the latency for you.
  • Tempo and time signatureChange them anywhere in the song. Loops follow your song's tempo and key.
  • Mixing and editingSends, buses and automation, a piano roll, and a drum grid with quantize and humanize.

Getting a song ready for the band

  • Move sections aroundDrag the chorus in front of the verse and its music goes with it. You can repeat a section too, or take one out and the rest closes up.
  • A new key and tempoKey & tempo moves the whole song at once. The MIDI goes to the new key and the audio is re-pitched and stretched to fit. Drums keep their pitch.
  • A click and spoken cuesMake click & guide tracks puts a click on its own track, and a voice that says "Chorus" or "Bridge" a bar before the section starts.
  • Know how loud it isThe meter in the toolbar shows LUFS and the true peak while the song plays. Check the grade & loudness shows the stars Central Tracks will give your mix before you publish it.
  • Run it from a footswitchUnder Controls, press Learn and then a footswitch, pad or knob on any MIDI controller. It can start and stop the song, record, jump to the next section or set a volume.

Plugins

  • Your VST3 and CLAP plugins, liveThey play on every track while the song plays. Turn a knob and you hear it about a fifth of a second later. You only need to freeze a track if your computer is working hard.
  • Play a keyboard through themNotes from a MIDI keyboard go through the track's plugins, with only a few milliseconds of delay.
  • Delays that stay in timeAny delay can follow the song's tempo, even when the tempo changes. Pick the note you want, from a whole note down to a 1/32, dotted or triplet.
  • Build your own pluginsDrag in blocks like a filter, delay, reverb or drive, add knobs and wire them up. If you'd rather write code, you can.
  • They work in the other appsA plugin you make plays on the pedal, in Central Control and Central Usage, on the website and in Production.

Getting your work out

  • ExportSave as WAV or MP3, as stems, or as an SF2 or SFZ instrument. Plugin automation is included in what you export.
  • Straight to the Central PedalExport for Central Pedal makes one zip with the parts, the click, the spoken guide, the chart and the sections. Central Pedal Control reads it as a multitrack, sections and all.
  • Install into my appsPuts what you made straight into Central Control and Central Usage on this computer.
  • PublishSend it to Make & sell or Central Tracks, and get paid when people use it.
  • Saved to your accountThe desktop app and the website open the same projects.

The other apps

They're separate apps, but on the same Wi-Fi they find each other and share the work.

Central Pedal

The pedal at the worship leader's feet

A floor unit that plays your multitracks and pads, shows the band the lyrics and the chart, and runs your guitar or keys rig and a looper. It has a 7-inch touch screen, a 5-inch screen you can read from a step back, 8 footswitches, 14 knobs and 4 sliders, and a little screen beside each control that says what it does. We build and test each one. It's coming in the middle of 2027.

See what it does

Central Pedal Control

Get the pedal ready from home

This is where the week's prep happens. Build the setlist, add songs and tracks, set up the pads and the instrument, and decide what each button on the pedal does. When you're done, press Upload and it all goes to the pedal.

See what it does

Central Control

The desk runs the whole service

Central Control runs the room from the desk: lights, lyrics, slides, cameras, the stream, in-ears and the run sheet. If you have more than one computer back there, give each one its own jobs and they work together.
