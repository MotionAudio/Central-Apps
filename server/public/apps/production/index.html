Make tracks, pads, sounds and plugins

A free studio and sampler. Make tracks, loops, samples, pads, instruments, plugins and pedal boards, then put them in your own apps or sell them on Make & sell.

Everything Central Production does

Recording and editingPluginsGetting your work out

Recording and editing

  • Start from what you're makingFile → New project asks: a track, loop, sample, pad, instrument, plugin or pedal board.
  • Multitrack recordingCount-in, punch in and out, and loop takes, with your interface's inputs per track and latency measuring.
  • Tempo and time signatureChanges anywhere in the song; loops follow your song's tempo and key.
  • Mixer and automationSends, buses, automation, a piano roll and a drum grid with quantize and humanize.

Plugins

  • Real VST3 and CLAP pluginsLive while you record, and frozen into the track for playback.
  • Make your own pluginsDrag blocks (filter, delay, reverb, drive, chorus, compressor, pitch, LFO, knobs) and wire them up, or write code.
  • They play everywherePlugins you make play in every app: the pedal, Central Control, Central Usage, the website and Production.

Getting your work out

  • ExportWAV, MP3, stems, or SF2/SFZ instruments.
  • Install into my appsPuts what you made into Central Control and Central Usage on this computer.
  • PublishSend it to Make & sell or Central Tracks, and get paid when people use it.
  • Saved to your accountThe app and the website open the same projects.

Central Pedal

The pedal at the worship leader's feet

Pads, multitracks, chord charts, lyrics, a looper and a guitar and keys rig, in one floor unit with 12 footswitches, knobs, a touch screen and two HDMI ports. We build it, test it and ship it ready to play.
