Refund policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

In short: unused keys can be refunded within 30 days. A built Central Pedal can be returned within 30 days. Faulty hardware is repaired or replaced for one year. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time. Free trials cost nothing.

License keys and church packs

  • Within 30 days of purchase, we refund any key that has never been activated on a device.
  • For a church pack, we refund its unused keys at the share of the pack price they were bought at.
  • Renewals ($20 a key) are final: they aren't refunded.
  • Refunded keys stop working.

Central Pedal (built)

  • Return it within 30 days of delivery, in working order and with everything it came with, for a full refund of the pedal. Return shipping is paid by the buyer unless the pedal arrived damaged or faulty.
  • If it stops working because of a defect within one year, we repair or replace it, and we pay the shipping.
  • Damage from drops, liquid or modifications isn't covered.

Central Tracks

  • Unlimited subscription: cancel any time under Billing. It stays on until the end of the month already paid. We don't refund part of a month.
  • Single tracks: because the files are delivered right away, purchases are final. If a file is broken or isn't what was described, contact us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund it.

Central Services

A monthly plan: cancel any time under Billing. It stays on until the end of the month already paid. We don't refund part of a month.

Free trials

A free trial (for example 7FREE) costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund. When it ends, the trial keys stop working until you buy keys.

How to ask

Contact support with your church's name and the order. Refunds go back to the card that paid, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions? Contact support.
