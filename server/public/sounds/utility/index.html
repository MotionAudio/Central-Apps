What's in Central Utility

118 backgrounds

Moving and still backgrounds for the seasons of the church year and for ordinary Sundays. There are some for youth and kids too.

51 slide themes and lower thirds

Each theme matches a background and lays out a title, a verse and its reference. There's a lower third for the stream as well.

14 click sounds

Wood block, rim, hi-hat, cowbell, clave, shaker, side stick, triangle, a plain tick and a few beeps. Each one has its own accent for the first beat.

24 lighting looks

Ready-made looks for Central Control's lights, with names like Sunrise, Ocean and Ember.

Countdowns and stream overlays

A countdown to put up before the service starts, and overlays for the stream.

"This Sunday"

Central Control knows where we are in the church year and suggests a background and theme for this Sunday.
