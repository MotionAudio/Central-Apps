Packs for your whole team

Your church's account holds every key and slot, and you hand them out from the Teams page. Each pack has a main admin, admins, users, Central Pedal Control and Central Services slots. The pedal is separate, so you can start with what you already have.

How keys and slots work
P
Central Pedal Control
Yours for good, on two devices, with every update we ever make. The pedal itself needs no key.
A
Main admins and admins, Central Control
One computer per key, with ten releases of updates (buy during 10.0 and you're covered up to 11.0). Renew for $20 whenever you like. Up to 2 main admins per church.
U
Musicians, Central Usage
Two devices per key, so players can have one at church and one at home. Your apps keep working even if you never renew.
S
Sound plans, package keys
Paste one in any app's Settings → Packages. Three of your devices, each a different app, for as long as the plan.
Slots
Central Services slots
One per person on your teams, no key to type. Take someone off a team and their slot is free for the next person.

Questions

Do I pay once or every year?

Church packs, the pedal and the app keys are paid once. Sound plans and Central Utility are yearly, and Central Tracks Unlimited is monthly. Every price includes tax.

What's in a church pack?

A main admin and admins (Central Control keys), users (Central Usage keys), a Central Pedal Control key and Central Services slots for your team. The pedal is separate, so you can start with what you already have.

Which sound plan do I need?

Keys players: Central Keys. Other musicians: Central Instrument. The pad pedal: Central Pads (it comes with every other plan). Better effects: Central Plugins. Guitarists: Central Pedals. Lyrics backgrounds and slide themes: Central Utility. The helper above picks for you.

How many devices does a sound plan work on?

One package key works on three of your devices at once, each a different app: for example Central Usage, Central Control and your pedal.

What does a church bundle give us?

A package key with every sound plan, Central Pads and Central Utility for each person, for a year. You hand them out on the Teams page and can move one to someone else at any time.

Do the apps stop working if we don't renew?

No. Keys you bought keep working. Central Control keys include ten releases of updates; renew for $20 whenever you like. Sound plans keep their sounds working while the plan is active. If one ends during a service, whatever is playing finishes the song first.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Type a trial code in the cart, or use the demo church's keys from the Help Center to try every app.

What about refunds?

See our refund policy. In short: tell us within 30 days if something isn't right and we'll make it right.
