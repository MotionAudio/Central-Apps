Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Motion Audio makes Central Pedal, Central Pedal Control, Central Control, Central Usage, Central Services and the Central Tracks library. This page says what we keep about you, why, who helps us run it, and how to get it deleted.

In short: we keep what the apps need to work for your church: your account, your church's keys and schedule, and your settings. We never sell it. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your card number. You can delete your account at any time from the website or from any app.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password. We can't read your password.
  • Admins' phone numbers: the main admin and admins give a mobile number. A sign-in code goes to it when they sign in on a new device. We use it for nothing else.
  • Your church or production: its name, who's in it, and each person's role (main admin, admin or user).
  • License keys and devices: which keys your church bought, and how far each one's updates go. Also the device each one is on (the name you gave it and a random id the app makes). This is how a key works on one device, or two for users.
  • Discounts and trials: which discount codes your church used and whether it had its free trial, so each is used once.
  • Settings that follow your account: app preferences you change, so your devices match.
  • Central Services: your church's teams, services, song lists, who was asked to serve and their answers, notes, and the dates people say they're away.
  • Central Tracks: tracks you publish (audio files, title, artist, key, tempo), what you buy or add, and what publishers earn.
  • Orders: what was bought, the amount, and a shipping address for a built pedal.
  • Support messages: what you send us, with the app and version you were using.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong in an app or on this website, the app sends the error message, the app's version, the kind of device, and the account's email if someone is signed in. We use these only to fix problems.

Tracks you load onto your own pedal or computer stay on your devices. They don't come to us unless you publish them to Central Tracks yourself.

Who helps us run it

  • Stripe: payments, the card on file, receipts, and payouts to publishers. Stripe handles card numbers and bank details. We never see them.
  • Render: hosts this website and its database.
  • Cloudflare R2: stores audio files and the nightly database backups.
  • Resend: sends our emails (invites, receipts, reminders, password resets).
  • Twilio: sends the sign-in codes by text message.
  • GitHub: serves the app downloads and updates.

Each of them uses the information only to provide their service to us.

Who sees what

People in your church see the church's name, who's in it, the schedule, and the song lists. The main admin and admins also see every key and the device it's on. We don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you have one. When you delete it, your name, email and phone number are removed right away, your keys are freed, and you're taken out of your church. We keep records of past orders and payouts, without your details, because tax and accounting rules require it. Nightly backups are kept for 30 days, then deleted. Error reports and support messages are kept for up to two years.

Deleting your account

On the website: Account → Your account → Delete my account. In any app: Settings → License → Delete my account. The main admin hands the role to someone else first, or closes the church if they're the only one in it.

Your choices

  • See or change your name, email and phone in your account.
  • Ask for a copy of what we keep about you, or ask us to correct it: contact support.
  • Stop the reminder emails by answering the invite or the service request. Receipts, sign-in codes and password emails always go out.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Admins sign in with a code sent to their phone. Each key works only on the devices it was activated on.

Children

The apps are for churches and productions, not for children under 13. We don't knowingly keep information about children under 13. If you think we have some, contact support and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this website and email account holders before it takes effect.

Questions? Contact support.
