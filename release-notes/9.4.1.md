## Central 9.4.1: the small screen's own touch settings

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal
- **Settings → Touch on the small 3.5" screen:** pick which touch screen this is (‹ ›, then Use this screen and
  Restart now), calibrate the touch (touch the two crosses), and flip the picture and the touch 180° for a screen
  mounted upside down. It also says whether the touch panel was found.
