## Central 10.5.0: more sounds, more tracks, a bigger looper, and loops you make yourself

A big batch of new sounds and songs, two new things in the apps, and the sound libraries' move to Cloudflare.

### New sounds (Settings → Packages → Libraries: install the new versions)
- **Central Pads More** - a third pads download with the same Pads key: 24 more pads in every key, each a 24-second loop (twice as long as before, so its slow movement takes longer to come round). Sunday Shimmer, Deep Worship, Analog Strings (a 70s string machine), Glass Choir, Prayer Room, Cathedral Choir, Rising Tide, Organ Pad, Choir and Strings, Still Waters, Chapel Harmonium, Pentecost Fire, Night Sky, Communion, Tape Strings, Kingdom Come (the big one for the last chorus), Advent Waiting, Gospel Organ (the Leslie on fast), Rain on the Roof, and five made from real recordings: Celtic Air, Glacier, Soft Brass, Harvest and Heartstrings. Plus six more moving pads (dotted eighths, sixteenth shimmer, a slow breath, push and pull, a quarter swell, a half-time pulse).
- **Every pad at one loudness.** Before, a smooth pad could come out up to 8 dB louder than a lively one at the same fader. Now Central Pads, Central Pads Extra (1.1) and Central Pads More all sit at the same level, and so do the ambient loops.
- **Central Pads 1.3** - seven more ambient loops in every key: Piano Arps, Organ Swell, Strings Rise, Bell Tower, Guitar Arpeggio, Wurlitzer Haze and Hum Drone.
- **Central Keys 1.4** - real electric pianos: a Wurlitzer EP200, a CP80 electric grand and a Pianet T (Greg Sullivan's recordings), with six presets - Wurli Worship, Wurli Soft Ballad, CP80 Pop Grand, CP80 Eighties Chorus, Pianet Dreamy and Wurli and Pad Bed. And the Pop Grand: AKAI's Splendid Grand (public domain), the bright, even piano of a thousand records, with three presets.
- **Central Instrument 1.3** - a Lo-fi Kit, a Vintage Kit (Swirly Drums, a round 70s kit), a Quiet Upright Bass and a Banjo, and twelve more drum and percussion loops: a worship pocket, a tom groove, a four-bar builder, gospel 12/8, a country two-step, soft pop, two lo-fi beats, a processional march, tambourine and shaker, a djembe groove and timpani under a hymn. Every groove now comes at three tempos instead of two, so the app stretches it less to fit your song, and each loop goes through a drum bus (gentle glue and a touch of tape) so it sits under a band.
- **Central Plugins 1.3** - twelve more boards (keys glue, a Wurli studio chain, a vocal-space pad, lo-fi and mono-stage keys, shimmer, dotted and tape-echo electrics, fingerstyle and sparkle acoustics, sub-glue and broadcast bass) and a Wurli Dream preset.
- **Central Pedals 1.3** - 26 more cabinet and body impulse responses (a tweed deluxe, a jazz clean 2x12, a greenback 4x12, a 1x15 blues combo, a modern clean 2x12, a little practice amp - each close, off the edge and in the room - two more bass cabinets, and OM, 12-string, mandolin, ukulele, resonator and acoustic bass bodies) with eighteen boards built on them.

### The looper: up to eight loops, each with its own input
- **Add loop** on the Looper tab (and a button job) takes you from three loops up to eight. Remove the last one when you don't need it.
- **Each loop picks what it records:** the instrument, the audio input, both - or one input of your audio interface. So loop 1 can be the bass, loop 2 the vocal mic, loop 3 a keyboard. The list shows every input the interface has.
- The Central Pedal's screen pages through the loops three at a time.

### Make loops in Central Usage's Studio
- **New → New loop** gives you 4 or 8 bars that the Studio loops while you record or draw. **Hear it as a loop** plays it round and round.
- **Add to my loops** puts the finished loop straight into this computer's apps as one of your own (no plan needed): it shows up in the texture channels, the Instrument tab and the loop browser. One loop, or one per track.
- The export is seamless: the reverb and delay tails wrap round to the start.

### Behind the scenes
- The sound libraries and hymns now live in Cloudflare (libraries.motionaudio.org). The GitHub copies stay as they were for older apps; nothing new goes there. GitHub is still where the code lives, where the apps are built, and where the installers and updates come from.
