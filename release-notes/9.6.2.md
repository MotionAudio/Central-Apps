## Central 9.6.2: when a pedal doesn't start, it says why

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal
- **A start-up report on the SD card.** 1 and 4 minutes after every start the pedal writes `central-pedal-log.txt` to
  the card's boot partition: the version, any service that failed, the first start (data partition, Wi-Fi radio),
  Central Pedal's own log, the screens' log, the network, the disks and the screens the Pi sees. Turn the pedal off,
  put the card in any computer and open the "bootfs" drive to read it - no commands to type.
- **No more sitting on a login prompt.** When the screens can't start, the main screen says so, shows the last lines
  of the reason and where the report is, and keeps trying.
