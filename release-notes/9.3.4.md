## Central 9.3.4: light bars, strobes and a 3D stage; text that fits; the mouse pointer; 3.5" screens

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Control: lighting
- **The stage in 3D.** The flat stage plot is now your stage at its real size, with its trusses and the audience. Every
  light shows its colour, its beam through the haze and the pool of light where it lands; moving heads turn with their
  pan and tilt. Drag to turn, right-drag to slide, the wheel to zoom; Audience, Corner, Top and Side views; Bigger.
- **Place your lights.** Click a light: drag it along its truss or across the floor, pick where it hangs and how high,
  and where it points (At the stage, At the audience, Straight down, Straight up, Back wall, or by angle). Moving heads
  can be hung or standing.
- **Stage & trusses.** Type your stage's width, depth and height, and add trusses (how far from the front edge, how
  high, how wide). Lights on a truss move with it.
- **Light bars.** Every cell has its own colour: chases, rainbows and the new Wipe, Bounce and Sparkle run cell by cell
  along the bars. New bar types (4, 8, 12, 16 cells, RGB, RGBW, RGBWA+UV); imported fixtures find their cells by
  themselves.
- **Strobes.** Hold the new **Strobe** button (or S, or Strobe on the phone remote); a **Strobe rate** fader; in a
  preset, strobes locked to the bar, beat, ½, ¼ or ⅛; a strobe burst on every hit. New strobe types and two new
  presets: Strobe on the beat, and Bars: colour wave.
