## Central 9.1.1: a new look for Motion Audio: the website, Central Control and Central Usage

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### New brand
- **Motion Audio mark:** an M made of two overlapping pad swells, in brass.
- **New icons for all five apps:** graphite with a brass symbol for each one.
  - Central Pedal: the stompbox.
  - Central Pedal Control: a swell being edited.
  - Central Control: the faders.
  - Central Usage: the in-ears.
  - Central Services: the schedule.
- The icons and launch screens are new on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad.
- **One typeface everywhere:** Archivo, bundled with the apps so it works with no internet.

### The website
- **Store:**
  - A dark stage at the top, with pad swells and the five apps.
  - The pedal now has its own row.
  - The church packs sit side by side.
  - How P, A and U keys work is explained in one line each.
  - Each app has a row in a price list, with Central Services monthly.
- **Account:** your church and teams come first, then the smaller cards.
- **Privacy, Terms and Refunds** pages now look the same as the rest of the site.
- **Download page:** the same look, with each app's icon.

### Central Control and Central Usage
- **Dark studio look:**
  - Graphite panels, brass for what's on and what to press, warm white text.
  - Every tab, card, dialog and slider is restyled.
  - Lighting, mix, in-ears, lyrics, slides, video, I/O and the setup screens all match.
- **Tabs:** they sit in one strip, and the open one is lit.
- **Header:** the Motion Audio mark, and the app's own name (Central Usage now says Central Usage).
- **Phones:**
  - The in-ear page, the remote and the camera page match the new look.
  - The Central Usage phone app's start screen matches too.
- Chord charts keep their monospace letters so chords stay over the right words. The chords are gold and the current section is blue.

### Fixes
- **Pads:** when a section swells the pads, each layer keeps its balance. Before, every layer jumped to the same level and lost the mix you set.
- Make an account: choosing "I'm the main admin" now hides the church code box (it used to stay visible).
- On a phone, the staff and church tables scroll inside their card instead of making the page scroll sideways.

### Checked
A new browser test clicks through every part of the website. It covers:
- the cart with a discount code and a free trial;
- making the main admin's account, with the phone code;
- confirming the email;
- adding someone on the Teams card, and their invite link;
- support and forgot password;
- every Motion Audio staff card;
- the store at phone width.

Central Pedal, Central Pedal Control and Central Services get the new look next.
