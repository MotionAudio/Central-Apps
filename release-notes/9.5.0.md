## Central 9.5.0: sound plans, set up a pedal from your phone, Downloads after you buy

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Sound plans
- **Four yearly plans, one key each:** Central Keys (pianos, electric pianos, organs, synths, pads) $50, Central
  Plugins (EQ, compressors, reverb, delay and other mixing effects) $120, Central Instrument (basses, guitars,
  strings, brass, winds) $80 and Central Pedals (boost, drive, fuzz, wah, modulation, amps, cabs, acoustic) $50 a
  year, or all four for $220. Church bundles of all four plans for 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20 people.
- **A plan is a package key** (it starts with S). It comes by email; on any app go to **Settings → Packages → Add a
  package** and paste it. It works on two devices at a time. A church's main admin gives its bundle's keys to people
  on the Teams page, and each one gets theirs by email. Anyone can buy their own plan, also someone in a church.
- **A free starter set** stays with every app: 15 pedals, 4 boards (Clean Worship, Acoustic Dry, Bass Clean DI, Keys
  Stage) and 3 sounds (upright piano, electric piano, organ).
- **Nothing breaks when a plan ends:** a saved board keeps its pedals (bypassed, with a lock and the plan's name),
  locked sounds go quiet, and stepping skips what's locked. Renew and it all comes back.
- **More sounds:** electric guitars (clean, jazz, direct, distorted), nylon guitar, ukulele, ocarina and two synths,
  plus the boards Worship Grand, Ambient Keys, Bass Fingerstyle and Clean Strat-ish. Credits in sounds/CREDITS.txt.
- Until the Motion Audio website opens, every plan stays unlocked.

### Central Tracks
- Publish and buy **sounds** (.sf2 or samples), **pedal boards** and **click sounds**, each graded from a short demo
  clip like a track. They install themselves when you add them.
- Central Tracks Unlimited ($77 a month) covers the church account, the main admins, the pedal and Central Pedal
  Control, and includes every community item.

### Keys
- **Central Pedal needs no key** any more. Central Pedal Control (P), Central Control (A) and Central Usage (U) always
  need one; Central Services is your account.

### A new Central Pedal, set up from your phone
- **The SD card maker asks nothing:** put the card in, run
  `curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/sd-card.sh | sudo bash`, pick the card and type ERASE. A
  32 GB card gets every sound; a 16 GB card all but the big grand piano.
- **First start:** with no network the pedal makes its own Wi-Fi, **Central Pedal Setup**. Join it on a phone and the
  setup page opens (or go to http://10.42.0.1). Pick the church Wi-Fi, the touch screen and the 4-digit code (the
  main admin's, or any 4 digits - Central Pedal Control uses the same one), then **Upload**. The pedal joins the
  Wi-Fi, gets the screen's driver and restarts once. A wrong password brings the setup Wi-Fi back with the reason.
- It comes back by itself when the saved Wi-Fi is gone for two minutes (a new building), and **Settings → Wi-Fi →
  Turn the setup Wi-Fi on** runs it again. The small screen and HDMI say what to do while it's on.

### The website
- **Buying:** the cart asks **Just me** or **A church or team**. Just me lands on **Downloads**: each app with its
  Download button, how to install it on your computer, iPad or phone, and your key; the pedal's shipping and phone
  setup; your sound plan keys. The same comes by email. A church lands on **Next: add your people**: everyone added on
  Teams gets an email with their app, how to install it and their key.
- Sales from the Sale page also take money off a sound plan's first year. People can renew their own keys' updates.
