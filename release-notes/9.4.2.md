## Central 9.4.2: steadier taps on the small screen; HDMI 1 always the full app; a one-line SD card

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal
- **Taps on the small 3.5" screen land on the button you touch.** Its touch panel's first reading jumps while the
  finger settles; a tap now goes off on the steady readings that follow (about 30 ms), so buttons near the edges
  work too.
- **HDMI 1 shows the full Central Pedal app** as soon as the pedal starts, also on a pedal where an older version had
  switched it to the copy of the small screen. HDMI 2 stays the projector lyrics; Settings → Screens changes either.
- Pages always come fresh after an update (no old copy kept by the screen).

### A new SD card in one line
- On an Ubuntu PC, with the SD card in: `curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/sd-card.sh | sudo bash`.
  It asks for the Wi-Fi, the small touch screen and the pedal's key, builds `central-pedal-<version>.img` and writes
  it to the card you pick. The image is now called **central-pedal**, and it carries every app file.
