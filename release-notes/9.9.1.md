## Central 9.9.1: fixes for the pedal

**Download:** everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### The 3.5" screen
- **Effects show straight away.** Tracks → Effects and Pads → Effects no longer sit on "Waiting for the Central Pedal
  app…". The pedal keeps its own copy of the effects, scenes, controls and sound lists, so the pages fill in at once,
  even right after a restart. A change made while the app isn't running is kept and goes in as soon as it is.

### The big screen
- **The Pad tab doesn't go blank when you scroll any more.** A style meant for the LIVE tab was stretching the Signal
  chain list into a huge empty box, which pushed the rest of the page out of sight.

### Sound
- **The headphone jack gets the sound.** The pedal now finds the jack under its new name on the current Pi software,
  stops the system from remembering an old output (like the HDMI monitor), and checks every few seconds that the app is
  still playing to the right place.
- **Settings → Audio → Test sound:** a tone on each output, a **Check where the sound goes** button that shows (and
  fixes) where the app is playing, and **Play on each sound card**, which plays a tone straight from the Pi. If the jack
  is silent even there, it's the headphones, cable or socket.

### USB audio interfaces
- **Use its inputs:** pick the interface and its input (1, 2, both, or a pair like 3-4) for the pads' audio input and
  for the instrument.
- **Use its outputs:** they're outputs 5 and up, and every channel of a bigger interface is now available. Or switch
  on **Use the USB interface for outputs 1–2** to make it the main output instead of the jack.
