## Central 9.8.0 (draft - plans)

### Central Pads, and the plans' new prices
- **Central Pads** is a new sound plan: 12 pads for the pad pedal - warm, air, choir aah, choir ooh, shimmer, strings,
  glass, swell, worship, ambient drone, deep and heaven - in all 12 keys. Each is a seamless stereo loop of the key's
  chord, played as it is (no pitch shifting). **$40 a year** on its own.
- **Every other plan now includes Central Pads**, and each costs $20 more: Central Keys **$70**, Central Plugins **$140**,
  Central Instrument **$100**, Central Pedals **$70**, all of them **$240** a year. Church bundles: 4 people **$700**,
  8 **$1,380**, 12 **$1,860**, 16 **$2,590**, 20 **$2,920** a year. Sales still come off a plan's first year.
- Install it in **Settings → Packages → Libraries → Central Pads Library**, then pick a pad's sound under **Central Pads**
  on the Pad tab. Without a plan, a Central Pads pad plays the free Warm Pad.
- **Central Plugins** works in Central Control (main admin and admins), Central Usage (on the instrument) and the
  Central Pedal (on tracks and pads).

### One package key, three of your devices
- A package key now works on **3 of your devices**, each a different app - say Central Usage, Central Control and your
  Central Pedal (never two pedals or two Central Usages). They're your own devices: an app signed in with someone else's
  key can't use your package. Release one in Settings → Packages or on your account page.
