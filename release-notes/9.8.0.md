## Central 9.8.0: Central Pads, sets from Central Pedal Control, effects on every track and pad, a fuller 3.5" screen

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pads, and the plans' new prices
- **Central Pads** is a new sound plan: 12 pads for the pad pedal - warm, air, choir aah, choir ooh, shimmer, strings,
  glass, swell, worship, ambient drone, deep and heaven - in all 12 keys. Each is a seamless stereo loop of the key's
  chord, played as it is (no pitch shifting). **$40 a year** on its own.
- **Every other plan now includes Central Pads**, and each costs $20 more: Central Keys **$70**, Central Plugins **$140**,
  Central Instrument **$100**, Central Pedals **$70**, all of them **$240** a year. Church bundles: 4 people **$700**,
  8 **$1,380**, 12 **$1,860**, 16 **$2,590**, 20 **$2,920** a year. Sales still come off a plan's first year.
- Install it in **Settings → Packages → Libraries → Central Pads Library**, then pick a pad's sound under **Central Pads**
  on the Pad tab. Without a plan, a Central Pads pad plays the free Warm Pad.
- **Central Plugins** works in Central Control (main admin and admins), Central Usage (on the instrument) and the
  Central Pedal (on tracks and pads).

### One package key, three of your devices
- A package key now works on **3 of your devices**, each a different app - say Central Usage, Central Control and your
  Central Pedal (never two pedals or two Central Usages). They're your own devices: an app signed in with someone else's
  key can't use your package. Release one in Settings → Packages or on your account page.

### Sets: Central Pedal Control keeps your songs, the pedal plays one set
- Songs, tracks, pads and sounds stay on the computer or iPad that runs Central Pedal Control. **Upload this set** sends
  the setlist with its tracks, the pad sounds it uses and only the keys / instrument sounds its songs use.
- Before it sends, it shows what goes and how big it is, what's already on the pedal and whether it fits. Files go in
  pieces and are checked; pressing Upload again carries on where it stopped. Nothing changes on the pedal until all of
  it has arrived - then the pedal switches when nothing is playing (or **Switch now**) and only then removes the old
  set. Pedals, plugins, the free sounds and installed libraries always stay.
- The pedal shows which set it holds (by the project name, in the setlist and on the 3.5" screen). Songs or pads
  changed on the pedal are flagged: the next Upload offers **Bring them back first**.

### Effects on every track and every pad
- **Gate, EQ, compressor, reverb and delay** on each track, each pad, the whole tracks bus and the whole pads bus, with
  presets (Vocal, Drums, Bass, Keys, Guitar stems; Clean, Warm, Bright pad). The basic version of each is free; the
  extra EQ bands, every compressor and gate control, hall / plate / cathedral reverbs, dotted and ping-pong delays and
  the pro presets come with **Central Plugins**. A pad saved with them plays the nearest basic version while locked.
- A track's effects are worked out once in the background and saved, so the Pi does no extra work while it plays.
- Tracks still play without pads and pads without tracks.

### Buttons, knobs and sliders
- **Pad volume:** every pad has its own volume slider (big screen) or bar (3.5"); sliding it never plays the pad. A
  knob or slider can set any pad's volume.
- **Tabs:** next / previous tab, or straight to Pad, Tracks, Instrument, Settings or LIVE.
- **Move around the screen without touching it:** up / down / left / right, OK, back and scroll, with a clear ring.
- **Each bus:** a level and a mute for tracks, pads, click + voice and instrument (mutes are never saved).

### The 3.5" screen
- **Pads:** Key (all 12 keys, one tap) and Sound (volume, reverb, tone, fade, freeze, layer, textures).
  **Tracks:** Click · Tempo (tempo, tap, click and its level, voice cues, count-in) and more transport buttons.
  **Instrument:** pick any board or keys sound from the list; play-any on / off. **Settings:** Wi-Fi with an on-screen
  keyboard, what every button / knob / slider does, updates and adding a package key.
- **Much quicker to the touch:** a pressed button lights up at once, only the changed part of the screen is redrawn and
  the screen runs at 50 frames a second.

### Sound and start-up
- **No sound on the headphone jack fixed:** the pedal now uses every sound output it has (it stopped looking after the
  first two - with HDMI plugged in, the jack was left out). It unmutes and sets the levels itself.
- A bus routed to an output that isn't there (3-4 with no DAC board) plays on 1-2, and Settings says so. New quick
  set-up **Everything on 1-2**, and **A DAC board is fitted** to switch the DAC's outputs off.
- **After an update the screens reload by themselves** (before, a pedal could show the old page with the new version).
- **Quicker, cleaner start:** no Debian text, login banner or "failed" lines on the screen - black, then the app; no
  firmware pause, no camera check, fewer services; a shorter wait for HDMI with the 3.5" screen.
- **The mouse pointer shows whenever a mouse is plugged in** (hidden for touch).
- The start-up report on the card adds start-up time, power (a weak supply shows "under-voltage" - use the official
  5.1 V 3 A supply) and the sound cards.

### Sound libraries
- Bigger **Central Keys** (grand pianos, mallets, bells, pads), **Central Instrument** (drum kits, guitars, basses,
  saxes, world instruments), **Central Plugins** (Textures: risers, swells, drones, impacts) and **Central Pedals**
  (28 cabinet IRs). Download them in Settings → Packages → Libraries; they work with the plan's key.
