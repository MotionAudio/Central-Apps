## Central 10.4.2: the website, help and manuals, rewritten

Nothing about how the apps work has changed in this one. I went through everything you read, on the website, in the help guides, inside the apps and here on GitHub, and rewrote it so it sounds like a person explaining it rather than a sales page.

### What's different
- **The website** reads plainly now, from the home page through Pricing, the app pages, the emails you get with your keys, and the privacy and refund pages.
- **The help guides and manuals** open with what the guide gets you and what you need on hand. Steps are numbered when the order matters, and each one says what to do if it doesn't go the way it should.
- **Help inside the apps** uses the same words as the guides, and the menu names match the apps exactly. The pedal's sound test is under Settings → Sound → Test sound.
- **The download page on GitHub** is up to date. It has all six apps, including Central Production, picks the right file for your computer, and shows the current version.
- **Each release on GitHub** now ends with a short "Which file do I need?" section.
