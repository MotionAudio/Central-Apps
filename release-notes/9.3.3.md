## Central 9.3.3: a tidier Central Control, website and Central Services

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

Same look, better layout, from small laptop screens to big desk monitors.

### Central Control (and Central Usage)
- **Top bar:** always two tidy rows: the tabs with your role, countdown and lock; then talk, the song and the connection.
- **Lighting:** the stage plot and faders get more room; the presets above the faders show whole rows (scroll for more),
  every fader fits, and the buttons under the plot stay together. Fixtures and Output cards no longer touch or wrap.
- **Service:** the role checkboxes line up on every run-order row, and "Announcements" fits in its column.
- **Devices, I/O and Settings:** even spacing in every card, the admin code field isn't cut off, and Settings cards pack
  into columns without holes.
- **Mix, In-ears and Video:** no empty boxes before the first song, "+ Channel" and "All 128" on one line, and the
  Preview and Program monitors fill their panels.

### The website and Central Services
- Phones: the menu is one sliding row, the keys table and People list stack neatly, and nothing scrolls sideways.
- The store's key notes, the Central Tracks list, the sign-in cards, the Teams card and the account cards line up.
- Central Services (the website page and the app): one tidy column, a two-row header on phones, and bigger tap targets.
- The manuals: the facts under each title and "On this page" take less room on phones.
