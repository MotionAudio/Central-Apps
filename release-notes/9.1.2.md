## Central 9.1.2: Central Pedal, Central Pedal Control and Central Services in the new look

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal and Central Pedal Control
- **Studio, the new standard look:** graphite, brass and warm white, in Archivo.
  - If you never changed your colours, the pedal switches to Studio by itself.
  - If you picked your own colours or another preset, they stay as they are.
  - Midnight, the old look, is still in Settings → Appearance.
- **Every screen matches:** LIVE, Pad, Tracks, Instrument, Configure, Learn, I/O and Settings, plus the start-up screen.
- **The phone and tablet pages match too:** the remote, the stage display, lyrics, slides and upload.
- **Built for the stage:**
  - Play stays green and Safe stop stays red.
  - The open tab is lit.
  - Text keeps strong contrast for a dark stage.
- The waveform and loop marker now follow your accent colour, whichever one you pick.

### Central Services
- **The studio look:** graphite and brass, the Motion Audio mark, and the tabs in one strip.
- Service times are in brass, so the schedule is easy to scan.
- Request answers show as clear chips: green for yes, orange for no, gold for not yet.
- It looks the same on the website, the computer app and phones.

### The website
- Sharing a link to the website shows a picture and a title in texts, Facebook and Slack.
