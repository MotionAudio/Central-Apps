## Central 9.3.1: Central Control opens on Linux, the mouse pointer is back in Central Pedal Control, and church accounts

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Control on Linux
- **It opens again.** Some computers still had a launcher (a desktop icon, a menu entry or a pinned dock icon) pointing
  at the old place, `/opt/Central Usage/central-usage`, and showed "not found". Installing 9.3.1 points every Central
  Control launcher it finds at the app.
- **Central Control is Central Control everywhere.** On Linux the package, the program and the command are now
  `central-control` (they were `central-usage`, the old name). The menu entry, the logo and your settings stay the same.
  Updating from the app or with the one-line command replaces the old package by itself.
- To update now from a terminal:
  `curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/install.sh | bash -s control`

### Central Pedal Control
- **The mouse pointer shows.** It was hidden on computers as if they were the Central Pedal's touchscreen. Only the
  Central Pedal's own screen hides it now (Settings → Mouse cursor still decides there).

### The website: church accounts
- **A church signs up with its own account**, using the church's email (like office@yourchurch.org). That account buys
  and pays (the receipts and the card are in the church's name), holds every key and Central Services slot, and adds the
  main admins (up to 2, each with their own account), admins and users.
- **The church account uses no Central Services slot.** Everyone else on a team uses one.
- Churches made before this keep working as they are: whoever made the church stays its main admin.
- New pages: every app explained, with pictures and a full manual; install guides for every computer, phone and tablet;
  and how accounts work.
