## Central Pedal 8.3.0 · Central Control 2.3.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

### The main admin is the center - a Raspberry Pi is no longer needed
- The **main admin's Central Control** now holds the team: pairing, roles and jobs, the Devices tab, the service
  lock, the services and countdowns, and where the in-ear audio host is. Every device pairs with the main admin
  (pairing PIN), and finds it on the network by itself.
- **Without any Central Pedal**, everything works: roles, devices, locks, services, lighting, mix, in-ears,
  talkback, video, slides and recording.
- Phones and tablets can open Central Control straight from the main admin's computer: `http://<main admin>:8095/cu/`.

### Tracks, charts and lyrics: the main admin's player, or a Central Pedal
- **No pedal:** the main admin's computer runs the tracks, charts and lyrics in the **player window** - the Central
  Pedal app, now built into Central Control. Devices tab → **▶ Open the player on this computer** - it pairs with the
  main admin by itself. Close its window and it asks: keep playing (hidden) or stop.
- **Add Central Pedals** (as many as you like): on each one's screen type the main admin's pairing PIN. The first
  one takes over the tracks; the main admin's player window then keeps the song library on standby.
- On the **Devices tab** the main admin picks **what each pedal does**:
  - **Track player** - plays the tracks and sends the charts and lyrics (one at a time; every Central Control and
    phone follows it and moves over by itself when you pick another).
  - **Foot controller** - its buttons, footswitches, knobs and MIDI start / stop, change song and section on the
    track player; its screen shows the player's song, section and what is next.
  - **Instrument rig** - only its own instrument (boards, pedals, keys sounds).
  - **Standby** - keeps the song library, does not play.
- **Songs, tracks, charts and setlists** are copied between the main admin's player and every Central Pedal.
- Each pedal's status: online, version, temperature, load and the song playing.

### Talkback: Band and Production
- The main admin has **🎸 Band** and **🎬 Production** next to TALK: pick one, hold TALK (or tap to latch) and
  everyone in that group hears you - in their in-ear mix if they have one (the music dims), otherwise on their
  computer's headphones.
- On the **Devices tab** each device has a **Talk group**: users start in the Band, admins in Production.
- Everyone else's talkback - the lighting admin's mic, another admin, a musician - goes **only to the main admin's
  headphones**.

### Platforms
- **Central Pedal runs only on the Raspberry Pi.** The separate Central Pedal for Windows / Mac / Linux is retired (its
  player is inside Central Control now) - uninstall it; it gets no more updates.
- **Central Control:** Windows, Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux; the user side (User 1 / User 2) on iPhone and
  iPad, opened from the main admin's computer.

### Raspberry Pi: Wi-Fi, touch screens, screens
- **Wi-Fi fixed:** connecting from Settings → Wi-Fi now works (it goes through the pedal's system helper, with the
  radio switched on and the country set), handles WPA3 and **hidden networks** ("Other network…"), and says why when it
  can't connect (wrong password, not in range…). New: **Forget this network** and the **Wi-Fi country**.
- **Touch screens by size:** Settings → **Touch screen** lists 2.2" - 4" screens on the GPIO pins (the common 3.5"
  "RPi Display" with the XPT2046 touch controller, Waveshare (A) / (B) / (C), Adafruit PiTFT, OzzMaker, TinyLCD,
  Tontec, Watterott, HY28B…), HDMI touch screens (3.5" - 10.1") and ribbon-cable (DSI) screens (the Raspberry Pi
  Touch Displays, Waveshare DSI). Screens that need their maker's driver get it downloaded when picked.
- **The screens:** with a small touch screen, **HDMI 1 shows a copy of it** (or pick lyrics, chord charts, the app,
  the stage display); **HDMI 2 shows lyrics or chord charts**. With an HDMI or ribbon-cable touch screen, it shows the
  app and the other port shows lyrics or charts. The app (it plays the sound) always runs, even when no screen shows it.
- **Mouse fixed:** the small screen's touch panel no longer moves the mouse pointer on HDMI; HDMI touch screens only
  touch their own screen.

### Also
- A Central Pedal that the main admin unpaired plays on its own until it is paired again.
- Changing the pairing PIN on the main admin reaches every pedal and device that is online.
- A device not paired with the main admin shows a small "Pair…" bar instead of interrupting.
