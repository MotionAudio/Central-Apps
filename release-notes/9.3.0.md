## Central 9.3.0: a second main admin's computer that can take over as the hub, and Central Services slots in the app

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Two main admins, two computers, one hub
A church can have two main admins. The hub is the main admin's computer: it runs the songs and tracks, lighting,
slides, the mixer and every paired device. Now the other main admin's computer can stay in sync with it and take over
whenever it needs to.

- **On the hub:** Devices → *Second main admin's computer* → **Keep … in sync**. That computer copies everything:
  the song library and tracks, the lighting rig and cues, the slides, the mixer, the cameras, every paired device and
  the PINs. After the first copy it only copies what changed, every minute. The Devices tab shows when it last did.
- **Take over as the hub:** on the in-sync computer, Settings → **Take over as the hub**, with the main admin's role
  PIN. Every phone, tablet, computer and Central Pedal stays paired and finds the new hub by itself.
  - If the hub is on, it makes one last copy and hands over. The old hub becomes an admin computer and keeps itself in
    sync with the new one, so you can swap back any time.
  - If the hub is off or broken, the last copy is used. When the old computer comes back, it sees the new hub and
    steps down to an admin computer by itself.
- Each person's own preferences, screens and microphone stay on their own computer.

### Central Services: your slots on the Teams screen
Schedulers now see how many of the church's slots are in use ("9 of 15 in use") at the top of the Teams screen.
Main admins and admins get a link to add more on the website ($5 a slot, once). Taking someone off a team frees their
slot for the next person.
