**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

Central Control and Central Usage now play the plugins you already own, right next to the built-in pedals:

### Effects on your board
- **Add pedal → Your plugins** lists every effect on the computer, by format, with search.
- We look in the standard plug-in folders. You can add a folder of your own (for example D:\Plugins\VST3), and **Scan again** after installing something new.
- Your plugins sit on the board like the other pedals: on and off with the footswitch, mix and level, in scenes, and in boards you save.
- **Open its window** shows the plugin's own screen, the same as in your DAW.
- **Quick controls:** the plugin's first controls as sliders in the app, so you can reach them without its window.

### Instruments for the keys
- **Piano & keys → Your instruments:** put Kontakt, Keyscape, Pianoteq, Vital or any instrument plugin on a layer.
- They work with everything layers already do: splits, octaves, velocity ranges, volume and pan.
- Each layer with a plugin has its own window button.
- MIDI keyboards play them, including the sustain pedal, the mod wheel and pitch bend.

### It just remembers
- Everything you set in a plugin, in its window or from the app, is saved with it on that computer and comes back next time.
- A board you save keeps its own copy of each plugin's settings.
- Each plugin is checked on its own when we look for plugins. A plugin that crashes or hangs is listed under "didn't load" with the reason, and the rest carry on.
- A plugin that's missing (uninstalled or moved) says so, and the rest of the board keeps playing.

### On Linux
The Instrument tab can run on either of two engines:
- **The Central Pedal's own engine:** its real LV2 pedals, through PipeWire.
- **The computer engine:** this one also plays your own VST3, CLAP and LV2 plugins.

Switch at the bottom of the Instrument tab. We remember your choice.

### Also
- The Instrument tab in Central Control and Central Usage now has the same polished controls as the Central Pedal app.
- Keys: choosing a plugin instrument clears an old "no SoundFont" warning.
- The latency readout is rounded (about 10 ms).
