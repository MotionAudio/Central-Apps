## Central Pedal 8.7.0 · Central Control 2.6.0 · Central Usage 2.6.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.7.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

### Add songs: four clear choices
**+ Add songs** in Central Pedal and Central Pedal Control now asks where the track comes from:
- **My own (MP3 / WAV)** - one mixed file or a set of stems. Your own files stay on your devices and are never shared.
- **Multitrack** - a MultiTracks.com download or any zip / folder of stems.
- **Prime track** - a Loop Community song's WAV stems.
- **Central Tracks** - the Motion Audio community library (below).
- Planning Center is still there as the fifth tab.

### Central Tracks
- Search the library by song, artist or key, and filter by Pad, Multitrack or Prime.
- **Subscribers** (Central Tracks Unlimited, $60/month): press **Add to storage** on any track.
- **Not subscribed:** press **Buy**, finish on the Motion Audio site (on a pedal, open the link on your phone), then
  **I've paid - add it**.
- Added tracks download, are analysed (tempo, beats) and become songs in your library, then copy to your pedals.
- Only tracks published on the Motion Audio website appear in the library.
- Set the Central Tracks server address in the tab. Until then the tab shows a preview of the library.
