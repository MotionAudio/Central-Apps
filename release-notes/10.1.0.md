## Central 10.1: a new lighting page, Talk, and sounds made by the community

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Lighting (Central Control and Central Usage)
- **The stage gets the whole width.** The 3D stage now runs edge to edge, and the song's waveform and timeline sit along
  its top. Under it: the songs, the presets for the section you're in, then every preset, then every light.
- **Pause** stops the lights where they are. Press it again to carry on.
- **Song settings and scenes:** each song has its own settings and scenes. Deleting a scene asks first. Add scene opens
  a preset picker, so a new scene starts from a look you already like.
- **10 quick slots:** drag any preset onto a slot and fire it with one press, a Stream Deck key or a MIDI note.
- **Setlists:** open the worship leader's setlist and either simulate it (the stage plays it without the real lights)
  or run it live.
- **Your stage:** set its size and shape, with better lights and haze on the 3D view.
- Stream Deck and MIDI sliders can fade lights and presets.

### Central Control
- **Mix is now called "Online mix".**
- **Talk:** hold the Talk button (at the right of the song bar, in Central Control and Central Usage) to speak into
  everyone's in-ears, and let go to stop. A quick tap keeps it on until you tap again.
- **Setup asks what each computer does**, also on computers set up before 10.0.
- **Admin code:** the main admin picks a 4-digit code when signing up. It's needed to change a computer's job later.
- **Libraries download from inside the app.** Settings → Packages → Libraries now has a Download button next to each
  library. It unlocks with your key. No more downloading a zip and picking the file.
- **Setlists in Central Services:** setlists made in the apps show up in Central Services too.

### Made by the community
- **Anyone can make and share sounds on the website:** samples, loops, tracks, pads, instruments, plugins and pedal
  effects. There's a studio in the browser for making them, or you can upload your own.
- **They come with the yearly packs.** Nothing extra to buy: what people share shows up in the pack it belongs to.
- **Creators get paid** from a share of each month's pack money, by how often their work is downloaded and how many
  stars Motion Audio gave it (2 stars counts twice as much as 1).
- **Everything is checked first.** Motion Audio staff listen to and test every upload before it's offered, and give
  it its stars.
- **Community plugins play live** on the Central Pedal and in Central Usage. Each one runs walled off on its own: it
  can't reach the internet or your files, and if it crashes or freezes it's bypassed and the sound keeps going.
  Settings → Packages & key → Community turns them off on a device. Not on Windows yet, and not yet in the Studio or the
  track and pad effects.
