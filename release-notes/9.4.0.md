## Central 9.4: the small touch screen gets tabs; Central Pedal Control goes live; Pedal Control on the iPad

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal: the small 3.5" touch screen
- **Four tabs, in the app's look:** Pads, Tracks, Instrument and Settings.
- **Tracks:** Play (the song's sections, play / pause, start, next section, repeat, click, key, next song), Mixer (the
  tracks and click levels, every track's level and mute) and Setlist (tap a song to load it).
- **Instrument:** Sound (electric, acoustic, bass, keys or off; the board or keys sound; the level), Pedals (every pedal
  on the board, on / off), EQ · Comp (the EQ, compressor and studio pedals' knobs as faders) and Tuner · Level.
- **Settings:** In & out (input gain, input L / R, the instrument level, which output tracks, pads, click and
  instrument go to), Screens (what HDMI 1 and HDMI 2 show) and System (address, pairing code, calibrate the touch,
  restart, shut down - asked twice).
- Faders: tap or drag along them.
- **HDMI "Copy of the small touch screen"** has the same four tabs, big, for a monitor or a TV.

### Central Pedal Control
- **● Live:** work the pedal itself over the network - pads, tracks, the mixer, the setlist, the instrument and the
  inputs and outputs. Every change happens on the pedal right away, no Upload.
- **On an iPad:** open `http://padpedal.local:8080/control/` in Safari on the pedal's Wi-Fi, then Share → Add to
  Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app, with ● Live, Upload and Download.
