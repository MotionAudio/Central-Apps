## Central 10.2: Central Production, run sheets, slides, real lights and a new look

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### A new look across every app
- All six apps and the website now look like one family: graphite, brass, warm white.
- Less on each screen. Each screen has one obvious thing to press, and the rest is under "More…" next to what it changes.
- **Search everything with Ctrl+K / ⌘K:** songs, setlists, any setting (it opens and highlights it), lighting presets, slide decks and actions like "Start stream".
- **Settings looks the same everywhere:** one page, groups in the same order in every app, and a search box.
- Every button in every app was clicked and checked. The broken ones are fixed.

### Central Production (new app, free)
- A studio for making tracks, loops, samples, pads, instruments, plugins and pedal boards. File → New project asks what you're making.
- Mac, Windows and Linux, plus a website version. Both open the same projects, saved to your account.
- **Recording and editing:**
  - Multitrack recording with count-in, punch and loop takes.
  - Tempo and time-signature changes.
  - A mixer with sends, buses and automation.
  - Piano roll and drum grid.
  - Loops that follow your song's tempo and key.
- **Your audio interface's inputs per track,** with latency measuring. Real VST3 and CLAP plugins: live while you record, and frozen into the track for playback.
- **Getting your work out:**
  - Export WAV, MP3, stems, or SF2/SFZ instruments.
  - "Install into my apps" puts what you made into your other apps.
  - "Publish" sends it to Make & sell or Central Tracks.
- The packs unlock the better sounds inside it.
- The Studio in Central Usage now runs the same engine, with "Open in Central Production".

### Make your own plugins
- Drag blocks (filter, delay, reverb, drive, chorus, compressor, pitch, LFO, your own knobs) and wire them up. People who write code get a code mode.
- Plugins you make play in every app: the pedal, Central Control, Central Usage, the website and Production, Windows included. They can't touch the computer, so they're safe.
- Eight examples to start from, including a shimmer reverb, a tape delay and a tremolo.

### Sounds
- **Central Pads: 41 styles,** including warm modern worship pads (Sunday Keys), pads made from real piano, strings, harp and organ recordings, and every one in real stereo.
- **New swells and transitions** in every key: swells, risers, downlifters and an arrival hit, plus a room bed for between songs.
- **Keys and synths:** each sound has its own character now. Soft Grand and Soft Upright are really soft and dark.
- **Loops:** 20 drum grooves and 8 ambient loops in all 12 keys.
- **The hymn tracks are rebuilt as modern worship multitracks.** Real recorded instruments (a concert grand, multi-mic drums, real basses and guitars, strings). Arrangements that build and drop. A proper mix. A guide track that calls out each section.
  - Hymns: Amazing Grace, Joyful Joyful, Come Thou Fount, Be Thou My Vision, Doxology, plus new ones: It Is Well, Holy Holy Holy, Blessed Assurance, Nothing but the Blood.
- **Hear before you download:** a 30-second preview of every pad, loop, track and instrument, on the website and in every app.

### Running the service (Central Control)
- **Run sheets:** answer a few questions and it builds a timed run sheet. Next fires the song, lyrics, lights, slides, camera and stream together, with a timer for each item.
- **Pre-service check:** every pedal, computer, key, sound output, mic, light, camera, screen and the stream, checked green/amber/red with a plain fix. It runs by itself 30 minutes before service.
- **The band's screen:** current lines in yellow, next lines in white, with chords, a clock, time left and messages from the booth. Every part of it can be changed.
- **Slides:** a full slide designer with themes, templates, transitions and lower thirds. It imports pictures, PDFs and PowerPoint.
- **Any picture works** as a slide or a background: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC from iPhones, and more.
- **Scripture in seconds:** type "John 3:16" and it's slides plus a stream caption. King James, World English, American Standard and Reina-Valera 1909 are built in.
- **Automatic shorts:** after a service, the built-in AI picks the strongest sermon moments and worship high points, and makes vertical clips with captions. You review them and post them.
- **Planning Center and CCLI:**
  - Planning Center plans import, and songs from SongSelect and ChordPro import.
  - The copyright line goes on screen automatically, and the CCLI report fills itself in.
  - Signing in directly to Planning Center turns on once Motion Audio is registered with them.

### Lights
- **2,272 real lights** from 223 makers, built in. Pick yours by maker and model, or make your own profile.
- **Program lights in a scene:** colour, brightness, position, zoom, gobo, strobe and effects for each light, all live.
- **Each light's settings:** moving or fixed, limits, how it's hung, whether the auto lights may use it.
- **Screens and projectors on the 3D stage,** each showing what the real screen shows (lyrics, slides, cameras) as it happens.
- **Your room:** draw the auditorium and seats, and see how the light falls on the congregation.
- **Lights anywhere:** overhead, behind, on trusses.
- **Build my stage:** a few questions build your whole stage. Or trace a floor plan or photo, use templates and props, and import your light list.
- **45 lyric backgrounds** built in, moving and still.
