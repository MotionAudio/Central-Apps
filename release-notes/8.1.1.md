## Central Control 8.1.1 · Central Usage 2.1.1

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself.

### Updates ask first, every time the app opens
- Every app (the Pi, Central Control on a PC, Central Usage) looks for a newer version **each time it opens**, and once a
  day while it runs, from this project's GitHub releases (already set; nothing to type).
- A new version asks **"Update available - want to update now?"** with **Yes** / **No**. No asks again the next time
  the app opens. On the Central Control, Yes waits until the pads and tracks are stopped.

### Central Usage
- **Lighting**: three tabs - **Show**, **Fixtures**, **Output**. Show has the songs and the presets on its right side,
  scrolling on their own (hide the panel with ⇥). "Tell it the show" is now a small chat window: **💬 Tell it**.
- **Video**: the Switcher fits on one screen - Main and Stream on top, smaller, and **every camera (up to 48) plus
  the slides** below without scrolling ("All on one screen", or a fixed grid with pages).
- **I/O**: laid out the way the signal flows - **1 In** (the Central Control, its audio, MIDI) → **2 This computer**
  (audio host, interface / Dante, listening) → **3 Out** (screens, lights).

### Fixed
- **"Could not reach the update server [Errno -3]"**: that is the Central Control not finding github.com - its network has
  no working DNS (or no internet). It now asks public DNS servers itself when the network's DNS fails, says plainly
  when there is no internet, and tries again every 10 minutes until it gets through (the Wi-Fi often comes up after the app).
