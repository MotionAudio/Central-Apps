## Central Pedal 8.11.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.11.0 · Central Control 2.10.0 · Central Usage 2.10.0 · Central Services 1.0.0 (new)

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Setting up a church, start to finish
1. **Make the church account** on the Motion Audio website.
2. **The main admin.** It can be the same account, or someone else: type their name and email and they get a link
   to take over as main admin.
3. **Buy the keys** (or a church pack). The church's page shows how many keys of each kind are left.
4. **Teams.** Three sections: **Admins** (Central Control), **Users** (Central Usage) and **Pedal Control** (Central
   Pedal Control). Press **Add members**, type each person's **name, email and role**, then **Done**.
5. **Everyone gets an email** with: a link to make their account, the download link for *their* app, and their key.
6. **On their device:** make the account, download the app, connect to Wi-Fi, type the key. Done.

The Teams page shows who's invited, who made their account and who has set up their app. **Resend** sends the
email again; **Remove** frees the key right away. Users can't see or change Teams. Without email set up on the
website yet, each person's invite link can be copied and texted instead.

### Keys and devices (so they can't be shared)
- **Admin (A) key:** one device at a time.
- **User (U) key and Pedal Control (P) key:** two devices (say a church iPad and a phone at home), **both signed in
  with the same account**. A third device is refused and names the two it's on. Someone else signing in on the
  second device is refused, and that device loses the key.
- **A pedal's own P key:** one pedal.
- **Release key** (in the app's Settings, or on the website) frees a device as before.

### Settings follow your account
Admins' and users' preferences are saved to their account. On a user's two devices a change on one shows up on the
other (the newest change wins; changes made offline go up when it's back online).

### Wi-Fi on first open
If the device (or the Central Pedal) is already online when it first opens, the Wi-Fi step is skipped.

### Updates on every app, every time it opens
- **Central Pedal, Central Pedal Control, Central Control, Central Usage, Central Services** on Windows, Mac and
  Linux: they look for a new version every time they open (and once a day). A new one installs by itself after a
  10-second notice, never while something plays. **Not now** skips it until next time.
- **Android** (Central Usage, Central Pedal Control, Central Services): they look every time they open too. After the
  10-second notice the new version downloads and Android shows **Update** - Android needs that one tap.
- **iPhone / iPad:** from the App Store once it's there.
- An update never touches your songs, setlists, settings, key or sign-in.

knows who you are:
