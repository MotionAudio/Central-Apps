## Central Control 8.1.0 · Central Usage 2.1.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ (the right file for your computer) · install steps in INSTALL.md.
Now for **Raspberry Pi, Windows, Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux**, and **iPhone / iPad / Android** in the browser.
Everything already installed updates itself.

### Everywhere
- **Windows and Mac versions** of Central Control and Central Usage, built from the same code as the Pi and Linux
  versions with every release. The installers aren't signed with paid certificates yet: Windows asks once
  (*More info → Run anyway*), the Mac once (*System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*).
- **iPhone, iPad and Android**: open `http://padpedal.local:8080/cu/` and *Add to Home Screen* - Central Usage
  as User 1 / User 2 (chart or any instrument's part, lyrics, your in-ear mix). It always has the Central
  Control's version.

### Central Control 8.1
- **The same songs and setlists on every Central Control.** Link the Pi and Central Control on your computers
  once with the other one's code; setlists made on one show up on the others to open, and every song copies
  with its stems, charts and light show. Newest edit wins. The Pi keeps the tracks of setlist songs only.
  Deleting asks: from this setlist, on this device, or everywhere.
- **Parts for every instrument**: guitar (chart with capo and chord diagrams, tab), bass, keys (both hands),
  vocals (melody with words), trumpet / clarinet / alto and tenor sax / horn in F / trombone in their keys,
  violin, cello, drums. *Listen to every track* writes down what each track plays (drums too); without a
  track the part is written from the chords. Charts heard from the audio use the bass track on its own.
- **Lyrics + chords from the vocal** (Central Control on a PC, and Central Usage): an offline speech model hears
  the words in the vocal track and writes the lyric chart with each chord on its word.
- **More sounds**: 40 more pads (121), 30 more pedals (239), 17 more keys banks (old piano, bright upright, synth
  brass, synth and electric basses, harp, clarinet, tenor sax, recorder, glass, hang, xylophone, kalimba).
- **Touch select**: give any knob, slider or the encoder the *Touch select* job (Configure). Tap any setting on the
  screen - a pedal's knob, a track fader, the pad sound, the lyrics size - and that knob changes it. It picks up from
  where the setting already is (an arrow shows which way to turn), stays on it until you tap something else (tap it
  again to let go), and the 3.5" screen shows what it holds.
- **Saved setups**: save what every button / knob / slider does, volume / tone / reverb / fade, the instrument (boards,
  pedals, keys sounds), textures, colours and the projector look under a name and bring it back with one tap
  (Settings → Saved setups, or the bar on Configure). Each song can have its own setup. Setups stay on the device
  they were made on; songs, tracks and setlists are what go to your other devices.
- **Pairing PIN**: the first time a Central Control starts it asks you to pick its 4-digit pairing PIN - every other
  device pairs with it. Change it in Settings → Wi-Fi & devices. It must differ from the main admin's PIN.
- **Tuner** (Instrument tab): one Tune button, a footswitch opens the Instrument tab with the tuner up, choose to
  mute the instrument or keep playing, and the 3.5" screen shows the tuner while it is up.
- **Fixed**: 85 pedals had knobs pointing at controls their plugin doesn't have, so board presets (Clean
  Worship, Edge Dotted-Eighth, Modern High Gain) didn't set their delays. Every knob is now checked against
  its plugin.

### Central Usage 2.1
- **Roles**: one **Main admin** (with a PIN and a pairing code, and a People list), **Admins** with jobs
  (Lighting, Production, Mix, In-ears, Slides & lyrics, or General), **User 1** and **User 2**. Users switch
  between User 1 and 2 themselves; the admin side needs the main admin's PIN. *Forget role* starts over.
- **Video tab** (main admin and Production): up to 48 cameras (NDI, USB / HDMI capture, SRT / RTMP / RTSP,
  phones), multiview, **Main** (full screen) and **Stream** outputs with Cut / Auto, lyrics lower third and
  slides, streaming to several places at once, NDI for OBS / vMix, recording, and two stream audio sources from
  the interface or Dante.
- **In-ears**: **64 stereo mixes**; each mix goes back over Wi-Fi to its person, out of the interface to the
  in-rack transmitters, or both. **Dante** (Dante Virtual Soundcard or Dante cards) on Windows and Mac, and
  several audio devices at once (up to 128 inputs).
- **Chart tab**: any instrument's part instead of the chart.

### Good to know
- NDI needs the free NDI Tools (ndi.video); Dante Virtual Soundcard is Audinate's paid licence.
- The lyrics speech model downloads once in Settings (about 500 MB, or 140 MB for the fast one).
- Phones as cameras need the app's certificate accepted once (steps on the phone page).
- An App Store version for iPhone / iPad is ready to switch on with an Apple Developer account (IOS-APP-STORE.md).
