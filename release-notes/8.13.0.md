## Central Pedal 8.13.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.13.0 · Central Control 2.12.0 · Central Usage 2.12.0 · Central Services 1.2.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Admins sign in with email, password and their phone
- The **main admin and admins** sign in with email and password, plus a **6-digit code texted to their phone**.
  That happens once per device; the device is then trusted for 30 days.
- The first time, they add their mobile number. Adding a phone takes **two codes**: one texted to the phone and one
  emailed. That way, knowing the password isn't enough to put someone else's phone on an admin's account. Every new
  phone also gets an email notice.
- Changing the phone (Account → Change phone) needs the password and a code texted to the new number.
- Someone made an admin after signing in signs in again, with the code, before using the admin tools.
- Works on the website, in every app's sign-in, in Central Services, and in Central Tracks on the pedal.
- Users sign in with email and password, as before.

### Safer sign-in for everyone
- Only **failed** tries count toward the guessing limit, so a whole church signing in on the same Wi-Fi is never slowed
  down. 20 wrong tries from one address, and it waits a few minutes.
- Someone added on the Teams page who signs up **without** the email's link joins once they **confirm their email**.
  Nobody can take an invite just by typing someone's address.
- A new password signs every other device out and asks admins' devices for the code again.

### The main admin's controls
On the website (Account → Your church → **People**), the main admin can:
- make anyone an **admin** or a **user**;
- **Make main admin**: hand the role to someone else in the church (with your password). You stay an admin.
- **Take out** someone, and add people on the Teams card as before.

### Billing stays with the church
- Purchases and the church's Central Tracks subscription are billed **in the church's or production's name**.
- **Billing: card & receipts** (main admin) opens Stripe's page to change the card on file and see invoices.
- When the main admin changes, the billing and card stay with the church; receipts go to the new main admin.
- A main admin's Central Tracks subscription now covers everyone in the church.

### Confirm your email
New accounts get a link to confirm their email. Receipts, keys, invites and password resets go there, so checkout
waits until it's confirmed. Accounts made from an invite's email link are confirmed already.

### Delete my account
On the website (Account → Your account), in every app (Settings → License), and in Central Services (Account). It
removes your name, email and phone, frees your keys, and takes you out of your church. The main admin hands the role
to someone else first (or closes the church if they're the only one in it).

### Privacy policy, terms, refunds
New pages on the website (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refunds`), linked from the footer, the sign-up form, every app's
Settings → License, and Central Services → Account.

### Central Services: away dates and song lists
- **Away:** mark the days you can't serve. Schedulers see who's away, and asking a whole team skips you on those days.
  A request you hadn't answered for one of those days turns into a no. Schedulers can mark someone else away.
- **Songs:** each service has a song list (title, key, BPM). Add it when you make the service, or **Edit songs**
  later. It's in the request email and in the day-before reminder.

### Technical support and error alerts
- **Contact support** on the website (footer → Support), in every app (Settings → License, and Help → Contact technical
  support… on a computer) and in Central Services. It goes to the support email. Reply goes straight to the person, and it
  says which church, app and version they were using.
- **Error alerts:** when the website or any app runs into an error (the page, the computer app itself, or the Central
  Pedal's driver), the support email hears about each new kind right away; repeats at most once a day. Staff see them
  all on the website: Account → Errors, support, backups.

### Nightly backups
The whole database is saved every night to the file storage and kept 30 days. Staff can **Back up now**. Restoring
into a new database is one command (server/SETUP.md → Backups).

### For whoever runs the website
New settings: `SUPPORT_EMAIL` (support and error alerts), and `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`,
`TWILIO_FROM` for the text messages. Until Twilio is set up, sign-in codes go by email. The backup job is in
`render.yaml`. See server/SETUP.md steps 2c and 2d.
