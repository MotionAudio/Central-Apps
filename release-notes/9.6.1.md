## Central 9.6.1: the by-hand SD card starts with the blue 3.5" screen on

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Building the SD card by hand
- `build-image.sh` (and `setup-pi.sh` on a Pi) now turns on the common blue 3.5" touch screen (XPT2046) from the
  first boot, with its touch panel and calibration, and HDMI 1 still shows the full Central Pedal app, so any setting
  (another screen too) can be changed there. `TFT=none` for no small screen, or another screen's id from
  `screens.json`; a screen that needs its maker's driver gets it while the card is built.
- With no Wi-Fi given, the pedal still makes **Central Pedal Setup** for a phone, as with the one line.
- The one-line SD card maker is unchanged.
