## Central 9.7.0: a factory test on a new pedal's first start, phone setup without the screen step, outputs your way

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Building pedals
- **The first start of a card built by hand is a factory test** on HDMI 1, with a keyboard and mouse: every
  footswitch (and its light), knob, slider, the expression pedal and the encoder; the 3.5" screen (test pattern, the
  four corners, calibration, and Change screen - the pedal restarts straight back into the test); a test sound on each
  output; the instrument input; MIDI, Wi-Fi, temperature and power, storage, sounds and version. Pass / Fail / Not
  fitted on each row, Save report, then Finish.
- **Unplug it and the next start is the customer's:** the Wi-Fi used for testing is forgotten, the touch screen you
  set is kept, the setup Wi-Fi comes on within seconds and both screens say "Please connect to Central Pedal Setup on
  your phone's Wi-Fi". The phone only asks for the Wi-Fi and the 4-digit code.

### Outputs
- 1–2 are the Pi's headphone jack, 3–4 the DAC board, 5 and up a USB interface. Tracks, Pads, Click + voice and
  Instrument each go to one output (mono) or a pair (stereo), and several can share an output (mixed there). Quick
  set-ups: 4 mono outputs (the default) or 2 stereo outputs. Plug in an interface while the pedal is on and it offers
  to restart the screens to use its outputs.

### Setting up a pedal
- The website and the download page now show the start-up steps for a pedal that arrives with its card in: plug in,
  join "Central Pedal Setup" on your phone, pick the Wi-Fi, type the 4-digit code, Upload.
