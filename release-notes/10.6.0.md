## Central 10.6.0: Central Production gets a song ready for the band

A Track project in Central Production can now go all the way to the Central Pedal. You can rearrange the song, move it to the key your singer needs, give it a click and spoken cues, check how loud it is, and export a pack the pedal reads with its sections already marked. You can also run the DAW from a footswitch.

### Move sections around, with their music
Open **Edit the song** above the DAW and the sections sit in a row under Arrangement. Drag one to move it. ⧉ plays it again straight after itself, and ✕ takes it out and closes the gap. The clips, notes and automation in that section go with it, and a repeated section keeps its lyrics and chords. Right-click a section on the ruler for the same thing.

One limit for now: it needs one tempo and one time signature for the whole song. If your song changes tempo partway through, it tells you and leaves the song alone.

### A new key and tempo for the whole song
**Key & tempo** in the toolbar (or in Edit the song) moves everything at once. The MIDI goes to the new key, and the audio is re-pitched and stretched from the original recording so it doesn't lose quality each time you change your mind. Drums keep their pitch. Every clip, section and automation point stays on its beat, and it's a single undo. Before you press OK it tells you how far you're going ("Down 2 semitones · 17% slower").

The key is worked out from the relative major, so going from G to Em doesn't move anything.

### Click and spoken cues
**Make click & guide tracks** adds two tracks: a click, and a voice that says the section's name a bar before it starts, like "Verse 2", "Chorus" or "Tag". Name your sections the usual way and they'll have a cue. If the song starts a bar or more before the first section and that section's name has no cue, the guide counts you in instead. Press it again after you change the sections and it remakes the two tracks rather than adding more.

### How loud it is
The toolbar has a loudness meter now. While the song plays it shows the last three seconds in LUFS, and underneath, the whole play and the highest true peak. The peak turns orange above -1 dB. Click the meter to start it again.

**Check the grade & loudness** renders the mix and shows the stars Central Tracks will give it, using the same checks the website runs when you publish, along with its loudness and a plain tip if it's too quiet, too loud or peaking.

### Export for Central Pedal
**Export → Export for Central Pedal** makes one zip, named the way the pedal reads it (Grace Song - Grace Band - 72bpm - G.zip). Inside is a WAV for each part (drums, bass, keys, guitar, vocals, and the rest as music), a click, the spoken guide, the full mix, Markers.mid with the sections and the tempo, the chart, and song.json. In Central Pedal Control, press **+ Song**, choose **Multitrack** and pick the zip. The sections come in already marked, so the pedal can jump straight to them.

The part each track goes into comes from "What each track is" in Edit the song, the same setting Central Tracks uses.

### MIDI and foot controls
**Controls** in the toolbar lists what a MIDI controller or footswitch can do: play and stop, stop and go back, record, go to the start, loop, the click, the previous or next section, add a section, undo and redo, tap the tempo, and set the selected track's volume or pan or the master volume. Press **Learn**, then press the footswitch or pad, or move the knob. A footswitch acts when you press it, not when you let go. A note you've set to a control doesn't also play a sound. They're kept on this computer.

### Behind the scenes
- Rearranging slices clips at the section lines with a short fade on each cut, and moves warped audio's starting point in the original file so a re-pitched clip stays put through a later key change.
- A clip you've pitched by hand in the editor keeps your change when the song's key moves, and moves with it.
- A track named Click (or any track set to Click) no longer goes into the Central Tracks export as an extra music stem when the export already makes its own click.
- New tests (central-production/test-song.mjs) run the pedal pack through the pedal's own scanner (sources.py), and check that a -20 dBFS tone reads -20 LUFS.
