## Central 9.1.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Free trials
- New code **BETATEST**: everything free for **3 months**. It works like TESTRUN (6 months) and 7FREE (7 days).
- A trial gives **unlimited keys** for its whole length. You make them as you need them: add people on the Teams card
  (each gets an email with their key), or press **+ Admin key**, **+ User key** or **+ Pedal Control key** on your
  church's page. It no longer starts with one of each.
- One free trial per **church or production company**, ever. That's the only limit: a person isn't limited.

### Release to all devices (Motion Audio staff)
On the website: Account → **Releases**. **Release to all devices** lets every Central Control and Central Usage install
that version, even those whose key's updates ended before it. Use it for an emergency fix. Apps pick it up the next time
they open, or within 6 hours. **Stop** takes it back.

### The codes page (Motion Audio staff)
Account → **Codes**: every discount and free trial code, what it does, whether it's on, and every church that used it,
with the trials running now. Change or add codes on the Prices card.

### Refunds
Renewals ($20 a key) are final: they aren't refunded.
