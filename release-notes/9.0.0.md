## Central 9.0.0: one version number for all five apps

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### New version numbers
Central Pedal, Central Pedal Control, Central Control, Central Usage and Central Services are all **9.0** now.
- **Full releases:** 9.1, 9.2 … 9.9, then 10.0.
- **Fixes and small updates:** the third number, for example 9.1.1 and 9.1.2.

### Updates that come with each key
- **Central Pedal and Central Pedal Control (P keys):** every update, forever.
- **Central Control (A keys) and Central Usage (U keys):** ten full releases from the version you bought at. Bought at
  9.0, you get every update through 10.0; bought at 9.4, through 10.4. Fixes to those releases are included.
- **Renew** a key for **$20** (per key; a user's church and home devices share one key): ten more releases, added on
  from where its updates ended. On the website: Account → Your church → tick the keys → Renew.
- **Nothing ever stops working.** You never have to update to keep using an app, or renew to keep a version you have.
  Past a key's updates, the app keeps its version and says once that a newer one is out.
- Each key on the church page shows how far its updates go.

### Discount codes and free trials (in the cart)
- **USAGE10:** 10% off the software. **2026:** 50% off the software. That means keys, church packs and renewals; not
  the built pedal, and not monthly plans. Each code works **once per church**.
- **7FREE:** everything free for **7 days**. **TESTRUN:** everything free for **6 months**.
  - "Everything" means as many admin, user and Pedal Control keys as you like (Teams page, or + Admin / + User /
    + Pedal Control key), plus Central Services and Central Tracks Unlimited.
  - When the trial ends, the trial keys stop and the apps ask for a key you bought. Songs, setlists and settings stay.
  - One free trial per church or production company, ever (and one per main admin). It needs a confirmed email.

### Central Services is now monthly
By how many accounts are in the church: **$10** a month up to 10, **$25** up to 25, **$50** up to 50, then
**$0.50 a month** for each one past 50. The price follows the church's size. The main admin starts it on the website
(Account → Central Services) and changes or cancels it under Billing. It isn't part of the church packs any more.
