## Central 10.8.0: a keyboard on Ctrl+K, drag and drop, MIDI instruments, and a lot more free sounds and plugins

Most of this one is for Central Production, but the keyboard and the new plugins reach the other apps too. Your songs, setlists, projects and settings stay as they are.

### Play from the computer keyboard with Ctrl+K
- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and a keyboard pops up at the bottom of the window. The row from A to the apostrophe plays the white keys, the row above it the black ones. Z and X move it down and up an octave, C and V make it softer and louder, and holding Tab is the sustain pedal. Esc puts it away. You can click the keys with the mouse too.
- In Central Production it plays the track you've selected (or the armed one). In Central Usage it plays the Instrument tab's sound and the Studio's tracks. In Central Pedal Control on a computer it plays the pedal's instrument, or the pads when you're on the Pads page.
- When a MIDI keyboard is plugged in, the keys you press light up on the pop-up too.
- The search that used to be on Ctrl+K is now on **Ctrl+P** (**⌘P**). Where there's nothing to play, like Central Control, Ctrl+K still opens the search.

### Drag and drop in Central Production
- Drop audio files, or a whole folder of stems, anywhere on the song and each one becomes its own track. Click and guide tracks go to the top, then drums, bass, keys, guitars and vocals. A tempo in the file names ("Song 72bpm") sets the song's tempo when it's empty.
- Drop a MIDI file and you get an instrument track for each part, with the right sounds picked from the General MIDI program. On an empty song its tempo, tempo changes and time signature come too.
- Drop one file on a track to put it on that track, at the spot you dropped it.
- Drag a track by its name to move it up or down. Drag the ⤓ handle on a track, or on a selected clip, out of the window and you get a 24-bit WAV of it, ready to drop into another program or a folder.

### Your own instruments
- Drop an SFZ, a Decent Sampler (.dspreset) or a SoundFont (.sf2) on an instrument track and it plays that instrument. A few WAVs named by note ("Piano C4.wav") become an instrument spread across the keys. Drop single hits on a drums track and they become a kit, with the hi-hats choking each other.
- The new **Samples** tab in the browser lists drum hits, effects and single notes from your libraries. Drag one onto a track, or click it to hear it.

### MIDI instruments and the pro tools
- **+ Track → External MIDI instrument…** makes a track that plays a synth or a keyboard you've plugged in: pick its MIDI port and channel, and the input its sound comes back on. Its notes go out ahead of time so they land in time. **Record its sound to an audio track** prints it.
- Each instrument track can have MIDI effects in its Sound panel: a scale that keeps every note in key, a chord on each note (or chords from the song's key) and an arpeggiator that follows the tempo. They work on what you play live and on what's recorded.
- **Capture** (Ctrl+Shift+C) turns what you just played into a clip, even when you weren't recording.
- Notes you let go of under the sustain pedal now record as long as the pedal holds them, and the pitch wheel is recorded with the clip.
- Quantize has a **Swing** slider. A track's menu has **Save as a MIDI file…** and **Bounce to audio**, which makes an audio track that sounds like it, with its volume, pan and sends, and mutes the original.

### 50 more plugins: 82 in all, 27 of them free
- New free ones in every app: Plate Reverb, Hall Reverb, Slapback, Overdrive, Bitcrusher, Low-Pass and High-Pass Filters, Telephone, Vibrato, Doubler, Ring Modulator, Pitch Shifter, Utility, and five Airwindows ports by Chris Johnson (Density, Purest Drive, Spiral, Clipper and Warm).
- New with Central Plugins: Cathedral, Chamber, Spring, Gated and Ducking Reverbs; Analog, Multi-Tap, Shimmer and Ducking Delays; a Channel Strip and a Vocal Chain set up the way a desk would; Parallel Compressor, Expander, Vintage EQ, Air Band, Auto Filter, Envelope Filter, Synth Filter, Multiband Saturator, Lo-Fi Tape, Uni-Vibe, Harmonic Tremolo, Ensemble, Micro Pitch, Octaver, Harmonizer, Haas Widener and a Maximizer. Four more are Airwindows ports: ButterComp, Tube, Console Preamp and Slew.
- The Add effect list in Central Production has a search box at the top and headings by kind, and it stays on screen even when you open it near the bottom. The pedal's plugin picker and Central Control's channel inserts group them by kind too.
- Plugins written in the plugin builder's code language run about a quarter faster, which leaves the Central Pedal more room. They sound the same.

### More free sounds
Central Starter, the free library every app installs on its own, goes to 1.1:
- 138 samples for the Samples tab. Kicks, snares, claps, hi-hats, cymbals and toms, hand and world percussion, bass hits, guitar, impacts and whooshes, a cymbal swell and its reverse, and some atmospheres. They're public-domain recordings from Sonic Pi's sample set and the Versilian Community Sample Library.
- Three drum kits made from them: an acoustic kit, an electronic kit and tabla with hand drums.
- A real accordion (FreePats' recording of a Hohner), a tubular glockenspiel and a Nyunga Nyunga mbira, with presets.
- Central Production now installs Central Starter on its own as well, like Central Control and Central Usage already did. The pedal installs it without the samples, since those are for the computer.

None of the new free sounds were taken out of a paid library, so the plans keep everything they had.

### Good to know
- VST3 and CLAP instruments on Central Production's tracks aren't in this release. Your VST3 and CLAP effects still play on tracks, and your own instruments still play in Central Usage's and the pedal's Instrument tab.
- Every new sound and plugin is public domain, CC0, MIT or CC-BY, and CREDITS.md lists where each one came from.
