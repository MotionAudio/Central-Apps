## Central 10.0.1: sound on the headphone jack, settings that apply at once

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### The Central Pedal
- **Sound comes out of the headphone jack.** With the DAC board switched on, the board took outputs 1-2 and the jack
  vanished from the list - so on a pedal without the board, nothing was heard. Now the jack is always outputs 1-2
  ("Headphone jack L / R"), the DAC board is always 3-4, and a USB interface comes after. The pedal opens the jack
  itself.
- **No more turning the pedal off after a change.** The DAC board switch, routing and a USB interface apply straight
  away (the sound restarts for a moment). The few that really need a restart - safe mode, a different touch screen -
  restart the pedal by themselves once nothing is playing, with a countdown and Cancel.
- **Updates install by themselves.** The pedal looks every 3 hours and installs in a quiet minute. If it can't, Settings
  → Software updates says why, with Try again.
- **Each HDMI port shows its own choice.** A monitor on the second port alone shows HDMI 2's choice (say, the lyrics),
  and moving the cable while the pedal is on switches it.
- **Settings, one group at a time:** a list on the left (Sound, Appearance, Saved setups, Screens & touch, Wi-Fi &
  devices…), bigger text, no more big empty gaps.
- **The 3.5" screen follows the mouse.** Click something on the HDMI app and the 3.5" screen goes to the same tab and
  lights the same button. A finger on the 3.5" always wins.
- A pedal without a speech engine no longer keeps asking for the spoken section cues.

### Computers (Linux)
- **Updates wait for the computer's own updates** instead of failing with "apt-get stopped with code 100", say the real
  reason if they can't install, try a second way, and as a last resort open the installer for you.
- The apps' packages install on the newest Ubuntu versions.
- **Smaller downloads:** 10.0 packed the lighting AI into Central Usage too (and twice into Central Control). Central
  Usage is back to about 340 MB; Central Control carries the AI once.
