## Central 10.7.0: a smoother Lighting page and a cleaner finish on every app

This one is about how the apps feel. Nothing you've set up changes, and your songs, setlists and settings stay as they are.

### Lighting opens and moves more smoothly (Central Control)
- The first look at **Lighting** no longer stalls. The 3D stage gets ready ahead of time, while the app is sitting idle.
- The live picture now draws once for each frame of your screen, in step with it, and not at all while the window is hidden. That's less work for the computer and smoother movement.
- Hovering, dragging, scrolling and holding an arrow key ask for one redraw per frame instead of one for every movement.
- Opening the tab no longer draws the whole page twice.

### A cleaner finish on the plain controls
- Drop-down lists, tick boxes, round buttons and scroll bars now match the rest of the apps: a dark background, brass when they're on, and thin dark scroll bars instead of your computer's light ones. A brass ring shows where the keyboard is. This is in Central Pedal Control, Central Control, Central Services and Central Production.
- The colour along the top of each pad and track section follows its rounded corners now, instead of curling at the ends.
- **Learn** and **Controls** only show pins, the ADC and the wiring diagram to people who build their own pedal. Everyone else sees "on the pedal" and a plain note.
- When Central Control can't find the pedal, its chip says "Central Pedal not found · retrying" instead of a cut-off address.
- The role cards in Central Control's setup sit two by two, with their text lined up.
