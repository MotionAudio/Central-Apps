### Add a song
**+ Add song** (Tracks, or the setlist) starts with **your songs**: everything in this setlist and your song library.
Pick one to add tracks to it, or press **＋ New song**. Then choose one of four ways:
1. **Your own stems:** WAV or MP3 files, one per instrument. Each becomes a track, and the tempo, key and sections are
   found.
2. **The whole song, split:** one file of the finished song. The stem splitter separates it.
3. **MultiTracks.com or Prime:** the Ableton set or stems you downloaded. The sections and tempo come with it.
4. **Central Tracks:** the Motion Audio library.

Planning Center plans and "just the song, no tracks" are still there.

### The stem splitter, upgraded
- **Best quality.** A RoFormer vocal model (BS-RoFormer, or Mel-Band RoFormer / MDX23C) takes the vocals out, and
  Demucs splits the band into drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest.
- **Split further:**
  - lead and backing vocals, with a karaoke model
  - the drums into kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, ride and crash, with DrumSep
- Each step shows its progress and the time left.
- Every setting is yours: the vocal model, the band model, shifts, overlap, segment size, half precision on a GPU,
  the device, the file format, and which stems to keep.
- Install once (about 2.5 GB). If the best-quality models can't install on a computer (an old Intel Mac), Demucs
  still works on its own.

### Sections, a click and a guide after every import
- Songs without sections get them from their tracks: intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, instrumental, tag and
  outro. They are worked out from where the vocals sing, the harmony and the loudness, on 2- and 4-bar phrases. This
  replaces the old "every 8 bars" guess.
- After a split, the click (from the beat grid) and the spoken guide (section names and the count-in) are switched
  on. Both are settings in the stem splitter.
