## Central 9.9.0: a loop pedal, a better-feeling 3.5" screen, and more you can do from it

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### A loop pedal
- **A new LOOPER tab** on the Central Pedal (HDMI 1 / 2), in Central Pedal Control, and on the 3.5" screen (Loops · Mix · Setup).
- **Three loops**, each with record, overdub, play, stop, undo, redo, clear, level, pan, reverse, half speed and mute. Each
  loop records the instrument, the audio input or both.
- **One footswitch works it like a loop pedal:** tap to record → play → overdub → play; double-tap stops; hold for a
  second clears.
- **In time with the song:** while tracks or the click play, a loop is a number of bars and starts on the bar line
  (a one-bar count-in is optional); with nothing playing it's free - tap to start, tap to close. The first loop sets
  the length and the others stay in step with it.
- Every looper control can go on a button, knob, slider or MIDI, and is saved in setups. The old looper actions run
  loop 1. The looper never stops your tracks or pads.
- It has its own row in Settings → Output routing (the instrument's output unless you pick another).

### The 3.5" screen's touch
- **Light taps count** - no more pressing hard or tapping twice.
- **Taps land where you touch:** a new 5-point calibration (the four corners and the centre), then a check - tap the dot
  and it shows how far off it landed; **Save** or **Redo**. On the 3.5" in Settings → Touch, or from Settings → Screens
  on HDMI.
- **Quicker:** the touch is read first, before the screen finishes drawing.
- **Smooth sliders:** the jumps a touch screen gives as the finger lifts no longer move them.
- **Test touch** shows dots where you touch and how steady the readings are (also written to the start-up report).
- Note: a weak power supply makes this kind of touch screen less accurate and slows the Pi. If the start-up report says
  "under-voltage", use the official 5.1 V 3 A supply.

### More from the 3.5" screen
- **Setlist:** go to any song, start the set, add songs from the song library (A–Z), move songs up or down, take them out.
- **Effects** on Tracks and Pads: each track, pad and both buses - on / off, the preset, and each effect's main amount.
  The better ones show a lock with Central Plugins; the basic versions always work.
- **Scenes:** this song's scenes, lyrics next / previous, projector blank, and what HDMI 1 and 2 show.
