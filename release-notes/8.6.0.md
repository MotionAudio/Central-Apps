## Central Pedal 8.6.0 · Central Control 2.6.0 · Central Usage 2.6.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.6.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

### Backup player
- On the main admin's **Devices** tab, make a second Central Pedal (or the main admin's own player window) the
  **Backup player**.
- It plays the same song, at the same place, in step with the track player, silently. It sends no MIDI hits, clock or
  ProPresenter cues.
- If the track player stops mid-song (its link drops or it goes quiet), the backup checks with the main admin. Then it
  fades in where the song is, with the pads that were playing, and the service carries on.
- If only the backup lost its Wi-Fi, the main admin says the track player is still there, and the backup stays silent.
- **Settings → Backup player:**
  - take over by itself, or show a **TAKE OVER** button, or off
  - how long a silence counts as stopped
  - the fade-in
  - how close it keeps in step
  - the network delay to allow for
  - pads on or off
  - the on-screen alarm

### Elgato Stream Deck
- Plug a Stream Deck into the Central Pedal, the Mac (Central Pedal Control) or the main admin's computer. Supported:
  Original, MK.2, XL, Mini, Neo and Plus.
- Every key can run any action, the same as a footswitch: play, sections, pads, scenes, the click, Safe Stop, and
  more.
- Each key's label, icon, colour and "on" colour are yours. So are the pages, brightness, dim when idle, hold time and
  rotation.
- Keys light up with what they control: the playing pad, the current section, the click on.
- On the Central Pedal it works with no setup: the image gives the app access to the Stream Deck.

### Practice mixes (Tracks → 🎧 Practice mixes)
- Makes a file of each song for each musician:
  - their part louder, alone, or left out
  - the click and the guide on or off
  - slower to learn it (the pitch stays) or in another key
  - with a count-in
- **The mixes are yours:** rename, add or remove them, and set each one's level for each part (click, guide, drums,
  bass, keys, guitars, vocals, other) from Solo through ±dB to Off. Each mix can have its own tempo and key.
- **Everything else is a setting too:**
  - which songs
  - the click: from the click track or made from the beat grid; its sound, subdivision, level and accent
  - the click and guide in one ear with the band in the other
  - the level (normalize to a peak, or as mixed)
  - the fade-out, and cutting the silence at the end
  - WAV 16/24-bit or MP3 (128-320 kbps), 44.1 or 48 kHz
  - file names (`{n} {song} {mix} {key} {bpm} {tempo}`), the folder, a playlist for each mix
- **Where they go:**
  - Kept on the Central Pedal: the band opens **http://<pedal>.local:8080/practice/** on their phones to play or
    download them. That page can be open to everyone on the network, need a practice code you give the band (not the pairing PIN), or be off.
  - Copied onto a USB stick.
  - Downloaded.
  - In Central Pedal Control, copied into any folder on the Mac.

### Stem splitter (Tracks → ✂ Stem splitter)
- Splits a song that is one stereo file into stems with Demucs, on your own computer, and brings it back as a song with
  tracks. Tempo, key and sections are found as for any import.
- Choose 4 stems (drums, bass, vocals, other), 6 stems (+ guitar and piano), or just vocals / no vocals.
- **Every Demucs setting is here:**
  - the model, including the fine-tuned best-quality one
  - shifts (passes), overlap and segment length
  - CPU, Apple GPU or NVIDIA GPU, and parallel jobs
  - WAV 16/24/32-bit float, FLAC or MP3
  - what to do when a stem would clip
  - which stems to keep, a new song or this one, and keeping the original as a muted track
- **Nothing is installed until you press Install** (about 2 GB, all inside the app's own folder). Remove it any time.
  You can also point it at your own Demucs.
- It is best on a Mac with Central Pedal Control, or on the main admin's computer: a minute or two a song. It also runs
  on a Central Pedal, slowly.

### Central Pedal → the main admin, each on its own channel
- When the main admin makes a Central Pedal the **track player** or the **instrument rig**, it starts sending to the
  main admin's computer by itself. Each track, the click, the pads and the instrument go on their own channels, over
  Ethernet if it is plugged in, otherwise Wi-Fi. MIDI hits go too.
- The in-ear host now takes the **instrument rig's** channels as well as the track player's.
- Set it up in Central Pedal Control and **⇪ Upload** it with the rest. Set by hand, it stays the way you set it.
- The Ready check warns when network audio is off and fixes it in one tap.
