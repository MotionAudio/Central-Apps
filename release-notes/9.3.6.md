## Central 9.3.6: the 3.5" screen's touch; HDMI 1 keeps the app; Central Usage's Settings

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal
- **Touch on the small 3.5" screen works.** Its panel says "touched" a moment before it says where, so a tap was
  read with no place (or the last tap's place). Each tap now lands where you touch it.
- **HDMI 1 keeps the Central Pedal app** when you pick a small touch screen, so Settings is always on the big screen.
  HDMI 1 and HDMI 2 show what Settings → Screens says; "Copy of the small touch screen" is still there to pick.
- Settings → Touch screen says whether the small screen and its touch panel were found.

### Central Usage
- Settings no longer shows the lighting's AI model or the lyrics-from-the-vocal model - those are Central Control
  (admin) work. Updates and About say Central Usage.
