## Central 10.1.1: sound on the Pi's headphone jack, a clearer Central Services sign-in

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### The Central Pedal
- **Crackles with no music on the headphone jack: fixed.** The Pi's own jack moves sound in bigger chunks than a sound
  card, and it was fed with the small buffer the other outputs use - starved like that it crackles instead of playing.
  It now has a bigger buffer of its own and is never put to sleep when things go quiet (each wake-up was a click).
- **Settings → Output device → Headphone jack buffer:** Safe (the default), Low delay, or Biggest. Applies straight away.
- **Test sound counts the jack's dropouts** since the pedal started. If it finds any it says so and steps the buffer up by
  itself; press Test sound again to check.

### Central Services
- Signing in with no website address set says the website isn't online yet (and where to enter its address), instead of
  "Failed to fetch". With an address that can't be reached, it names the address and says to check the connection.
