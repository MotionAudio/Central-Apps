## Central 10.0

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

This is a big one. Here's what's new, app by app.

### Setlists
- **Load setlist, Create new, Add songs.** Create a setlist, give it a name and a day, and add your team (search the
  people on your Central Services teams, as many as your church has slots for). **Add songs** opens the website on that
  setlist: songs you own, or anything with Central Tracks Unlimited, go straight in; anything else you buy first. On the
  pedal itself you get a QR code to open it on your phone.
- Setlists are saved on the church account, so every device sees the earlier ones.
- **The computer runs the tracks.** When Central Pedal Control or Central Control is around, it keeps and plays the
  tracks and the pedal becomes the foot controller. If the computer goes away, the pedal takes over in about 5 seconds.
  With no computer, the pedal keeps one setlist at a time; songs that only live on the pedal are never deleted.
- Tracks still play without pads, and pads without tracks.

### Charts and lyrics
- **They follow the tracks** on every screen: next song, section jumps, key and tempo changes. A screen that opens late
  catches up at once.
- **Read it your way:** chord chart, number chart, sheet music or words only, for your instrument (B♭, E♭, F…), clef
  (treble, bass, alto, tenor) and capo. Each phone, tablet or computer remembers its own choice.
- **Edit sections by the bar:** a ruler with every measure marked; drag where a section starts, type its length in bars,
  split it, or take it out.
- Cleaner automatic charts and lyrics: steadier chords spelled for the key, no "[Music]" or made-up sign-offs, lines
  that start on the bar.

### Central Control
- **A setup window** when it's first opened: your church and key, Main admin or Admin, then what this computer does -
  lyrics, lights, cameras, in-ears, tracks, live stream mix, slides. Any mix, on as many computers as you like; one key
  per computer. Each computer shows only its own jobs, and if two pick the same job they're asked which one runs it.
- **Smarter auto lights:** it finds the beat and the bar, follows a live band's tempo, makes each chorus a little bigger,
  keeps faces lit while someone's singing, works with whatever lights you have, never flickers, and keeps strobes to a
  safe speed (you can switch that off).
- **The lighting AI helper is in the download** - nothing extra to install. (It makes the Central Control download about
  485 MB bigger.)

### Central Usage: Studio
- A studio for the people playing instruments: up to **4 MIDI devices** at once (keyboard, drum pads, Launchpad…),
  instrument tracks with layered or split sounds, drum tracks and audio tracks; a clip grid with scenes and a timeline;
  piano roll and drum steps; count-in, loop recording and overdub; a mixer with effects on every track; export to WAV.
- Free with Central Usage. The better sounds and effects come with the sound plans.

### Every app: controls and colours
- **Learn any control:** Learn → **Add new control** → Button, Knob or Slider → name it → press it once (or turn it all
  the way left then right, or slide it down then up). Then give it any job in the app. MIDI pedals, footswitches,
  controllers, computer keys and Stream Decks all work, in all four apps.
- **Colours:** Settings → Colours changes the colour of everything, or pick one of 8 looks. "Back to our colours" puts
  the default back. The 3.5" screen follows the pedal app.

### Central Pedal
- Everything from 9.9.1 (the 3.5" Effects page, the Pad tab going blank, sound on the headphone jack, USB interfaces in
  and out, Test sound).
- The Tracks tab's key and tempo buttons are clearer: KEY DOWN ♭ / KEY UP ♯, SLOWER / FASTER, with the key and tempo
  between them.
- Fixed: the Tracks tab could grow taller than the HDMI screen, scraps of the pad editor showed under Perform, symbols
  drew as boxes on the 3.5" screen, Stream Deck settings didn't save.

### More to play
- **Central Tracks hymns**, in our own modern arrangements: Amazing Grace; Joyful, Joyful, We Adore Thee; Come, Thou Fount
  of Every Blessing; Be Thou My Vision; Doxology. Full stems, click and guide, sections, lyrics and a chord chart. In
  Central Tracks Unlimited, or $20 each.
- **Central Keys:** 10 new synths. **Central Instrument:** an electric upright bass (plucked and bowed) and 10 drum loops.
  **Central Pads:** 8 more pad styles and 3 ambient loops in all 12 keys. **Central Pedals:** 30 more pedals and 8 boards.
  (The 10.0 libraries need the 10.0 apps.)

### The website
- **Purchases** under the account icon: every key with a Copy button, its downloads and how to install it.
- Downloads come from the Motion Audio website; nothing sends you to GitHub any more.
- Clearer pages and guides throughout, and a phone menu you can actually reach.
