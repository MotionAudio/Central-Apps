## Central 10.3: live plugins, shorts that post themselves, twice the content, a new website

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Production
- **Plugins play live on every track.** VST3 and CLAP plugins now play during normal playback on as many tracks as you like, each track's delay lined up. Freezing is now optional, to save the computer's power.
- **Knobs and automation:** a plugin's knobs can be automated with lanes, and its own window controls the copy that's playing.

### Automatic shorts and social media (Central Control, main admin)
- **The AI learns your church.** Each week it learns from what you approve, trim, delete and post, and from how your posted shorts do on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. "What the AI has learned" in Settings shows it in plain words, with Reset.
- **It looks for the key moments.**
  - Sermons: the main points, the big idea, the call to action, scripture explained, a story's payoff.
  - Worship: the first chorus lift, the bridge build, the last big chorus, spontaneous moments, the room singing.
- **Settings → Social media:** how many shorts, how long, the mix, platforms, captions, hashtags, a title template and the schedule.
- **Link your accounts from the app:** YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.
- **Approved shorts post on a schedule** you set, per platform. Each one links to the full live stream. You can post now, skip or retry, and there's a history.
- **The whole worship set and the whole sermon** can each go up as their own full video. The worship video has song titles; the sermon video has chapters at each main point.

### Twice the content, each in its own pack
- **Central Pads:** 82 pads.
  - New: Sunday Keys variants, ambient guitar, synth swell, seasonal pads.
  - 18 pads made from real orchestral and world instruments.
  - Six moving pads that pulse in time with the song.
  - More swells and risers.
  - The new ones come in a second download, Central Pads Extra, that unlocks with the same key.
- **Central Plugins:** 32 polished effects.
  - Space: vocal plate, lush hall, worship shimmer, dotted-eighth delay, tape echo.
  - Dynamics: FET and opto compressors, bus glue, multiband, de-esser, transient shaper, true-peak limiter.
  - Tone and colour: 8-band EQ, tape saturation, rotary and more.
  - They work in every app, including Central Control's channel inserts. The engine is cleaner too: smoother shimmer, denser reverb, better compression, no aliasing on drive.
  - The Dotted Eighth delay follows the song's tempo.
- **Central Pedals:** 55 new pedals.
  - Drives and fuzzes, bass and acoustic preamps.
  - Amps with cabinets.
  - Compressors, modulation, octaves and harmonizer, wahs and filters.
  - Six kinds of delay and six reverbs, plus EQ and utility.
  - 68 ready-made boards in all.
- **Central Tracks:** 20 hymns, including Christmas (O Come All Ye Faithful, The First Noel, Away in a Manger, It Came Upon the Midnight Clear) and Easter (Jesus Christ Is Risen Today). Every song now builds to its biggest final chorus, with more variety in the keys and guitar parts.
- **Central Utility:**
  - 118 backgrounds: more for every season, nature, stained glass, youth and kids.
  - 51 slide themes and 52 templates: announcements, sermon series, fill-in-the-blank, scripture, giving, welcome loops, baptism, communion, prayer, kids.
  - Matching countdowns, lower thirds and overlays.
  - 15 more free backgrounds as well.
- **When a plan runs out:** anything already playing finishes the song, then says which plan it needs.

- **Help:**
