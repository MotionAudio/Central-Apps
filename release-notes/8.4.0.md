## Central Pedal 8.4.0 · Central Control 2.4.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

### LIVE: one screen for the whole service
- A new first tab, **● LIVE**, puts everything on one screen:
  - the setlist
  - the song: its key and BPM, the time left, and what comes next
  - its sections, coloured by kind and sized by length; tap one to jump there
  - the transport: **Play / Pause**, Prev / Next section, **⟲ Vamp** (repeat the section until you press it again),
    **■ Safe Stop** and Next song
  - the pads and the instrument, with the **tuner** right there
  - meters, and the Pi's temperature in °F
- **Safe Stop** fades the tracks and pads out over 2.5 seconds instead of cutting them. Press it again to stop at once.
- Changing song while something plays takes **two taps**, so a stray touch can't stop the band.
- It fits small HDMI touch screens (800×480) up to big monitors, and it is where the Central Pedal opens.

### Service lock
- **🔒 Lock for service**:
  - What stays locked: the other tabs, the mixer, tracks mode, seeking and the set-up actions. That covers the
    screens, footswitches and MIDI.
  - What keeps working: playing, sections, pads, the instrument and the tuner.
- Unlock with the Central Pedal's PIN. The lock stays on through a restart.
- The small screen shows LOCKED.

### Ready check before the service
- One list of what is fine and what to fix:
  - **Sound:** the audio is running.
  - **Click:** it has an output of its own. A click in the main mix is flagged, because the congregation would hear it.
  - **Songs:** every song in the setlist has its tracks and sections, and tracks mode is on.
  - **The Pi:** its temperature, power supply (under-voltage), storage and memory.
  - **Pairing** with the main admin.
- Fix buttons where they can help. When everything is green: **Lock for the service** in one tap.

### Service report
- LIVE → **📋 Report** shows any day. The Central Pedal writes everything down as it happens and keeps it for 120 days.
- **The totals:** songs played, playing time, first to last, songs stopped early, safe stops, audio dropouts, the
  hottest temperature (°F) and any low-power moments.
- **Every song:** when it started, how long it played, the sections that played, and how it ended (to the end,
  stopped at 2:13, Safe Stop), with jumps, vamps and dropouts.
- **Everything that happened,** minute by minute. **⬇ Download** saves the report as a text file.

### MultiTracks.com and Loop Community (Prime) downloads
- A download, as a zip or a folder of WAV stems, becomes a song.
- **Its sections come from the Ableton Live set or MIDI file** in the download, and its tempo fixes the beat grid.
- **Combine stems of the same kind:** three drum stems become one Drums track, for fewer faders and less memory. The
  click and the guide stay separate.
- A memory check warns before importing a pack that is too big for the Pi.
- **Several songs…** imports a whole folder of downloads at once.
- The import is easier to find: Tracks → **+ Add songs** opens on it.
- Prime and Playback themselves only run on an iPad, iPhone or Mac. This brings their songs to the Central Pedal.
