## Central Pedal 8.9.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.9.0 · Central Control 2.8.0 · Central Usage 2.8.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

### The stem splitter: one WAV of the whole song → all the tracks
Give it the finished song and it comes back as the tracks a band plays with:
**Lead Vox, BGVs, Drums, Perc, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Synth & Pads**, plus a **Click** and a **Guide**.

**Studio mode** (the new default, when the best-quality models are installed):
- Several vocal models vote on the vocals: BS-RoFormer with Mel-Band RoFormer (Kim). You choose the models and how
  they vote (average, median, loudest, quietest…). If they can't work together on a computer, the first model works
  alone and the splitter says so.
- Demucs FT (at least 2 passes) takes the drums and bass. The 6-stem model takes the guitar, keys and the rest.
- Then lead and backing vocals, **the reverb and echo off the vocal** (UVR DeEcho-DeReverb, or the Mel-Band RoFormer
  de-reverb), and the drum kit piece by piece.

**The clean-up pass** (after any split):
- **Bleed removal.** Each track is rebuilt from the song by how much of each sound it owns, so a guitar left in the
  vocals goes back to the guitar, and the tracks add up to the song again. You set how much and how strong.
- **Perc:** whatever is in the drums but isn't the kit (shakers, tambourine…).
- **The kit** as Drums (MultiTracks style), as Kick / Snare / Toms / Cymbals, or every piece on its own.
- The silence at the start and end is trimmed, every track by the same amount. This is skipped when you add to a song
  that already has tracks.
- Mono tracks (bass, kick…) are written as mono files.
- Silent tracks are left out, with a note.
- The reverb taken off the vocal can be kept as its own track (Vox FX).

**Check before adding.** A new screen lists every track:
- play them together, solo or mute any of them
- rename a track or change its role
- merge tracks (tick them, then Merge)
- split one again: lead and backing, reverb off, kit pieces, guitar / keys / the rest, or take the vocals out

Then **Add**. The tempo, key, beat grid and sections are found. A **Click track** (from the beat grid) and a
**Guide track** (your cue words before each section, and "1, 2, 3, 4" before the song comes in) go to the click out.

### Lighting: the drum hits still reach Central Control
- A split song keeps its **tempo and beat grid** for Central Control. It also gets its **drum hits**: kick, snare,
  hi-hat and crash, found from the separated kit pieces. With the drums as one track, they are found by band.
- The pedal sends each drum hit as **its own MIDI note on the lights channel**: kick 36, snare 38, hi-hat 42 and crash
  49 by default. Each one can be changed or turned off in Settings → MIDI out (and on the I/O tab). The big "hit" note
  stays as it was.
- **Central Control** puts the drum hits on the song's lighting timeline:
  - The crash flashes "Hit" by default.
  - Kick, snare and hi-hat can each get a flash preset of their own (Lights → Output → Lights and the music → Drum hits).
  - The pedal's drum notes flash live when the timeline doesn't have the hits already.

### Every setting is yours
Studio on or off, the vocal models and how they vote, the band models, de-reverb (and its model), keep the reverb,
bleed removal (amount and strength), trim, mono, Perc, how the kit comes back, the silence level, drum-hit
sensitivity, the track names (MultiTracks style or plain), the review, the Click track (level, clicks per beat) and
the Guide track (with or without the count). The drum hits' notes and presets are settings too.
