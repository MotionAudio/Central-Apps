## Central 9.1.4: studio compressor, EQ, limiter and de-esser for every instrument

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

These work on the Central Pedal and in the Instrument tab of Central Control and Central Usage (Windows, Mac and Linux). You'll find them under **Studio** when you add a pedal.

### Studio EQ
- Seven bands, the way a mixing desk does it:
  - a low cut and a high cut;
  - a low shelf and a high shelf;
  - three bells, each with its own frequency and width.
- A live curve above the knobs shows exactly what you're doing to the sound as you turn them.

### Studio Compressor
- Smooth, musical compression with a soft knee, and it keeps the level up for you as it works.
- Ratio now reads as a real ratio (2.5 : 1, 4 : 1, ∞ : 1).
- It starts at 2.5 : 1 with the threshold at −24 dB, so it's doing something useful the moment you add it.
- **Gain reduction meter** (computers): see how hard it's working while you play.

### Studio Limiter
- Nothing gets past the ceiling, so a hard strum or a dropped cable won't hurt anyone's ears or the PA.
- Its gain reduction shows on computers too.

### Studio De-esser
- Tames harsh highs and string squeak without dulling the rest of the sound.

### Three new boards to start from
- **Studio: Acoustic Channel Strip:** feedback control, EQ (low cut, less boxiness, a little sparkle), compressor and limiter.
- **Studio: Bass Channel Strip:** a tight low end, a bit of string definition, firmer compression and the limiter.
- **Studio: Keys Polish:** a clean low end, a touch of air, gentle compression, a plate reverb and the limiter.

### Small things
- **Reset** on a pedal now goes back to that pedal's starting settings.
- Two pedals that shared a name now have their own: the older Guitarix one is now **Guitarix Compressor**.
