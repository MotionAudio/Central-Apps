## Central Pedal 8.9.1 · Central Pedal Control 8.9.1 · Central Control 2.8.1 · Central Usage 2.8.1

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself (it asks first).

Fixes for the stem splitter and the lights from 8.9.0:
- **Split again** no longer adds the new tracks twice when two tracks are being split again at the same time.
- **Split again → Kit pieces** works on MP3 and FLAC drum tracks (they are converted for the clean-up first).
- Splitting a drum track again brings its **drum hits** for the lights along.
- A split again that stopped (the app was closed, the computer restarted) no longer leaves the row stuck at "splitting…".
- Settings saved before 8.9 keep the drum kit as separate pieces, the way you had it; Studio always splits everything.
- On a Central Pedal, the stem splitter warns that Studio with the kit pieces can need more memory than a 4 GB Pi has.
- **Lights:** the drum notes are kept under 60 (60 and up are the section notes). In Central Control, a drum note
  with no flash preset works like the old "hit" note again.
- A blank Click level is 70 % again (it made the Click track silent).
