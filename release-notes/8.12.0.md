## Central Pedal 8.12.0 · Central Pedal Control 8.12.0 · Central Control 2.11.0 · Central Usage 2.11.0 · Central Services 1.1.0

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Forgot password
- **Forgot password?** is on the website's sign-in, on an invite's sign-in, in every app's first-open account step,
  and in Central Services. Type your email and a link to set a new password comes by email.
- The link works **once, for an hour**. Up to 3 links an hour per account.
- Saving the new password signs you in there. **Every other device signs in again** with the new password (so an
  old sign-in on a lost phone stops working).
- It never says whether an email has an account, so nobody can use it to find out who's a member.

### Reminders for invites nobody answered
If someone on a Teams page hasn't made their account **3 days** after their invite, they get a reminder with the same
link, download and key. Another goes out after **7 days**, then no more. The Teams page shows "reminded 1×" / "2×".

### Move people between teams
Each person on the Teams page has **Move to…** (Admins, Users, Pedal Control). Moving someone:
- gives them a key for the new app and frees their old key right away (the old app asks for a key at its next check);
- emails them the new app's download and the new key (and to sign in, if they already have an account);
- makes them an admin in the church when they move to Admins, and a user when they move out (never the main admin).
If there's no key of the new kind left, it says so and nothing changes.

### Central Services: the day before
From 9 a.m. the day before a service (or as soon as it's less than 18 hours away), everyone who said **yes** gets
"you're serving tomorrow" with their role and the service's notes, and anyone who **hasn't answered** gets "please
answer". Nobody who said no gets one, and nobody gets two.

### For whoever runs the website
Reminders go out by themselves every hour from the website (no extra job). Email needs `RESEND_API_KEY` and
`MAIL_FROM` (server/SETUP.md, step 2b). `TIME_ZONE` sets the reminders' time zone (America/Chicago by default).
