## Central 10.4: quicker plugins, keyboards through plugins, delays in time, a lighter studio

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Production
- **Plugin knobs answer quickly.** A knob turned on a VST3 or CLAP plugin while the song plays is heard in about a fifth of a second, down from more than half a second. Settings → Audio → Plugin response sets it:
  - Fast (about 100 ms) for a quick computer;
  - Balanced (about 200 ms), the default;
  - Safe (about 400 ms).
  
  If the computer can't keep up, it gives the plugins more time by itself and tells you.
- **Play a keyboard through a track's plugins.** Notes from a MIDI keyboard on an instrument track go through its VST3 and CLAP plugins. Settings → Audio → Keyboard through plugins:
  - Lowest delay: the track's SoundFont sound and its plugins go straight to your sound card, a few milliseconds.
  - Through the whole track: everything on the track, about 10 to 20 ms more.
- **Exports keep plugin automation.** Exports, stems and frozen tracks include every automated plugin knob, on its exact sample.
- **Lighter on the computer.** On our 32-track test song, playing used about 40% less of the computer, with no dropouts:
  - the timeline draws each clip once instead of every frame;
  - the meters only redraw when they move;
  - silence isn't sent to the plugins, and a plugin with nothing to play rests;
  - the sound travels to the plugins without being converted to text.
- A plugin knob goes back to where you set it when its automation lane is deleted.

### Every delay can follow the song's tempo
- **Sync to tempo on every delay.** Turn it on and pick a note, from a whole note to a 1/32, including dotted and triplet notes. Turn it off and the delay keeps its own time in milliseconds. This works on:
  - pedal-board delays (Central Pedal, Central Control, Central Usage);
  - the pedal app's track, pad and bus delays;
  - Central Control's shared delay and channel inserts;
  - Central Production's Delay;
  - the Central FX delays (Delay, Tape Echo, Ping-Pong).
- **Tempo changes.** Delays in Central Production follow tempo changes in the song, exports included.
- **Plugins you build** can sync a Delay block in one click: pick a note and it gets a Tempo knob the apps turn for you.
- Delays saved before 10.4 keep the sound they had. A few third-party pedals with no exact time knob are set by ear (listed in Help).
