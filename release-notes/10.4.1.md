## Central 10.4.1: Test sound finds where the sound stops, and a quicker pedal screen

**Download:** from the Motion Audio website's Downloads page · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Pedal
- **Test sound follows the tone.** Press an output under Settings → Sound → Test sound and the pedal listens along the way:
  - whether the app's tone reaches the outputs, and on which output;
  - whether the outputs reach the sound card.
  
  It says where the sound stops and fixes what it can:
  - moves the app's sound back to the outputs;
  - makes the outputs again;
  - restarts the app's sound;
  - turns on a muted jack.
- **A pedal with only its headphone jack plays it straight**, without the combined output in between. That's one less step for the sound to get lost in, and less delay.
- **The test tone is longer (1.2 s)** and waits until the sound is running, so it isn't cut short.
- **A quicker screen.** On a Pi-speed test, the pedal's page uses about a third less of the processor on Settings and Tracks, and about three quarters less on Pads:
  - the meters, graphs and beat light only redraw when they change;
  - the graphs rest when nothing is playing;
  - the colours are read once instead of every frame.
