## Central 9.6.0: pan everywhere, effects and a stream mix in Central Control, smarter lights, play any sound

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Pan
- **Every track and channel has a labelled PAN** (L 30 / C / R 30; double-tap or hold puts it back in the centre): the
  Central Pedal's Tracks tab and mixer, the small 3.5" screen (a PAN bar beside each track), the HDMI copy, Central
  Pedal Control's Live view and the iPad app, and Central Control's Mix tab. Footswitches and MIDI can pan a track.
- Tracks arrive in Central Control at the pan they have on the pedal.
- **In-ears:** every channel has its PAN for the sound desk and for each person (phones too), and everyone can switch
  their own mix between Stereo and Mono.

### Central Control: effects on every channel
- Every channel has a high-pass, a **gate**, a **4-band EQ** and a **compressor**, plus sends to one shared **reverb**
  and one shared **delay** (the delay follows the song's tempo from the Central Pedal), like a sound desk. 13 presets
  (lead vocal, speech, acoustic, kick, snare…). The reverb and delay also come back in each in-ear mix, at a level each
  person sets for themselves.

### Central Control: the live stream mix
- **A Stream mix** with its own level and pan for every channel (the room doesn't change), or start from the main mix.
  Its own compressor, a limiter at -1 dBFS, meters and ON AIR.
- It goes to an output pair of the interface; to a virtual sound device for OBS or Zoom (Linux: "Central Control
  Stream"; Windows and Mac: VB-CABLE or BlackHole, linked from the Mix tab); straight to YouTube, Facebook or any RTMP
  server (with a picture when there's no camera); and into the Video tab's stream, from this computer or another one,
  with a sync delay. It's also recorded with the service (WAV and MP3).

### Smarter auto lighting
- Each song is read as **Intimate, Mid or Upbeat** (tempo, drums, level, key) and each section gets its energy and
  whether it builds or drops; change any of it in the new Auto panel on the Show tab.
- Intimate: warm, slow breathing fades, faces always lit, no strobes. Upbeat: strong colours, movers out over the room,
  chases on the beat, white flashes with strobes and blinders on the big hits.
- Every fixture has a job, and the live layer follows the music's level, beats and hits as it plays. The old auto is
  still there as Classic.

### Play any sound (Central Instrument)
- Plug in a guitar, bass or acoustic and play any installed sound: single notes play the sound you pick, chords play a
  guitar synth that follows at once. Auto, Notes or Synth; dry guitar blend; sensitivity; a footswitch; PLAY ANY on the
  small screen. About 6 ms for guitar notes, about 2 ms for the synth.

### More sounds and pedals
- **An orchestra** (Central Instrument): strings (violins, violas, cellos, contrabass, pizzicato), brass (horn, trumpet,
  trombone, tuba) and woodwinds (flute, clarinet, oboe, bassoon), with the boards Worship Strings, Cello Pad, Brass
  Swell and Flute Lead. From VS Chamber Orchestra 2: Community Edition by Versilian Studios.
- **22 new pedals:** compressors, gates, EQs, a tempo delay and four reverbs (Central Plugins); ping-pong, saturation,
  bit crusher, auto-wah, chorus, phaser and two pitch shifters (Central Pedals). New boards Studio: Warm Keys, Auto-Wah
  Funk and Bass: Octave Down. A pedal whose plugin isn't installed yet is left out of the chain instead of stopping it.
