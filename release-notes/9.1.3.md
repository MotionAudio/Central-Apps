## Central 9.1.3: graphics with real polish, a website you can edit yourself, and warmer words

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Lighting (Central Control)
- **The stage plot looks like a lit stage:**
  - Each light throws a pool of its colour on the floor, and pools blend like real light.
  - Moving heads show their beam where they point.
  - Fixtures look like metal cans with glowing lenses.
- **The show timeline:**
  - Sections are labelled chips.
  - The waveform is smooth and lights up in brass as it plays.
  - Hits are gold diamonds and cues are rounded markers in their preset's colour.
  - The playhead has a soft glow.

### Tracks (Central Pedal and Pedal Control)
- **The song timeline:**
  - The waveform is smooth, in each section's own colour.
  - What's still to play is dimmed.
  - Section names are on chips, with the one playing highlighted.
  - A and B markers are labelled, the repeating section has a dashed outline, and the playhead glows.
- **Meters and faders:** LED-style meters (green, amber, red) and console fader caps on every stem.

### In-ear mixing
- **On stage (Central Usage and phones):**
  - Every channel is a strip with its colour, a real fader cap and an LED meter.
  - Pan is a small knob.
- **At the desk (Central Control):** console-style strips with LED meters, fader caps and lit mute and solo buttons.

### Pads and the LIVE screen
- **Pads look like backlit hardware pads:** dark until they play, then lit in their own colour with a glow.
- **The big buttons have depth:**
  - Play glows green when it's on.
  - Safe stop is deep red.
  - Each button presses down when you hit it.
- New standard pad colours that suit the graphite look.

### Edit the website yourself
- Sign in as Motion Audio staff, then press **Edit this page** (bottom left). From there you can:
  - click any words and type;
  - click a price and type a new one (it's live as soon as you save);
  - replace a picture;
  - hide a section;
  - add a new section after any section: text, questions and answers, quotes, or a picture.
- **Editors:** Account → **Website editors**. Add anyone else who should edit. They can change words, pictures and sections, but prices stay with staff.
- The Privacy, Terms and Refunds pages can be edited the same way.
- **Safety:**
  - Only plain formatting and links are kept, never scripts.
  - Pictures are checked to be real images.
  - Editors confirm their sign-in with a code.
