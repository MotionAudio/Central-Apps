## Central Control 8.0.0 · Central Usage 2.0.0

### Central Control
- **The audio goes to Central Usage.** Every track goes on its own channel, plus
  the click, pads, the tracks mix and the instrument. It travels over Ethernet or
  Wi-Fi, or through the audio interface. Choose which on the new **I/O tab**,
  which lists every input and output.
- **One Learn layout** for the 12 buttons, 6 knobs and 2 sliders. Every function
  can go on any control, including Play all, Stop all and the next song.
- **Pads follow the tracks.** They follow the key and tempo, start and stop with
  the tracks, swell by section, and each song can have its own pad.
- **The Tracks tab** has the mixer on the page and one combined waveform with
  every section in its standard colour.
- **Colours of everything** (Settings), **rehearsal tools** (A–B loop, section
  back / next, slow down), a **guitar tuner**, and **song notes** on the stage
  display.
- **More sounds:** 81 pads, 59 more pedals, a bass board, a piano bank, and an
  on / off live **looper**.
- **Slides on HDMI 2**: pictures, video and lyrics from Central Usage.

### Central Usage 2.0
- **Admin and User** roles for each computer. There can be several of each, and
  only the Central Control plays, stops and skips the tracks.
- **Mix**: each track gets a fader, pan, mute, high-pass, 3-band EQ and
  compressor. The tracks go out over USB summed, or each on its own output.
  You can listen to any mix and record the service.
- **In-ears**: 128 channels and 48 mixes. Users pick a free mix and set their own
  levels. The mix plays back over Wi-Fi to their device (local), or leaves the
  interface for their pack (cloud). Phones get it too.
- **Lighting**:
  - Auto, Semi-auto and Manual modes, with the waveform and the hit lines.
  - "Tell it the show" in plain words, with an optional local AI model.
  - Record what you press, and record over any part again.
  - Change every light's colour in a list.
  - A **Songs** tab with any number of scenes. Scenes follow any tempo, and work
    with or without tracks.
  - A **Presets** tab, one output per **universe**, and a click you can hear,
    that the lights follow, and that can go to an audio output. The lights also
    react to the music.
- **Slides**: pictures, video and lyrics go to a second screen, the Central
  Control's HDMI 2 and NDI.
- **Instrument**: the same guitar / bass / keys rig as on the Central Control.
- **I/O** tab and **Settings**.
