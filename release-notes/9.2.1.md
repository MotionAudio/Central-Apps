## Central 9.2.1: Central Control is called Central Control everywhere, and a one-line install on Linux

**Download:** https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/ · everything already installed updates itself when it opens.

### Central Control's name
Central Control's download still carried its old name (Central Usage). Now it's Central Control everywhere:

- The download is **CentralControl-9.2.1-…**, next to **CentralUsage-…** for the musicians' app.
- **Windows:** the installer says Central Control.
- **Mac:** the app is **Central Control.app**. An update replaces the old "Central Usage.app" and opens the new one.
- **Linux:** the app lives in /opt/Central Control. The app menu, the `central-usage` command and the sandbox setting all follow it there.

Your settings, songs, library and keys stay where they are. Computers that already have Central Control update the usual way.

### Two apps, two jobs
- **Central Control** is for the main admin and admins: lighting, mix, in-ears, slides, lyrics and video. It no longer offers to become User 1 or User 2. A computer that was set that way is offered Admin and pointed to Central Usage.
- **Central Usage** is for musicians and singers: chord chart, instrument (with your own plugins), lyrics and your in-ear mix.
- Other gear now sees Central Control by its own name: the audio engine, Art-Net lighting and network audio.

### Linux: install with one line
Open Terminal, paste the line from the app's card on the download page, and press Enter. For example:

```
curl -fsSL https://motionaudio.github.io/Central-Apps/install.sh | bash -s control
```

- It finds the newest version, checks the download, and installs it with everything the app needs.
- The app then appears in your app menu and updates itself.
- Ubuntu, Debian, Mint and Pop!_OS get the .deb; any other Linux gets the AppImage with a menu entry.
- Use `usage`, `pedal-control` or `services` for the other apps, or leave the app name off and it asks.
