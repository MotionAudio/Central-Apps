## Central 10.5.1: the stock sounds are free in every app, and more of everything in the packs

The plainest pads, grooves, loops, synths and cabinets are now **stock**: a free library every Central app installs on its own. The packs lose them and gain richer ones in their place - eight pads, six grooves, three ambient loops, twelve boards and eight hymns.

### Central Starter - free, in every app
A new library, **Central Starter**, with no plan and no key:
- **Twelve pads** for the pad pedal in all 12 keys: Warm, Air, Choir Aah, Shimmer, Strings, Glass, Swell, Deep, Felt, Organ, Analog and Bells (the ones that were Central Pads' plainest).
- **Ten synths** made by us (saw, square and soft leads, synth brass, super saw, string machine, sub and synth bass, a plucked string, FM electric bells) with their seven presets - they were Central Keys'.
- **Six drum grooves** at three tempos each (worship eighths, rim and hat, four on the floor, half-time, brushes ballad, waltz) and two ambient loops in every key (Drift, Breath) for the texture channels.
- **Three plain cabinets and an acoustic body** for the IR loader, and **seven boards** built from the free pedals (clean, worship delay, crunch and chorus electrics, an acoustic, a bass DI and clean keys).

The Central Pedal downloads it about a minute and a half after it starts (and looks for a newer version once a day); Central Control and Central Usage do the same a minute after they open. Nothing happens offline - it tries again later - and nothing when the disk is nearly full. It shows first in Settings → Packages → Libraries, marked *Free · comes with every app*, and on the website's Downloads page.

### What the packs gained (Settings → Packages → Libraries: install the new versions)
- **Central Pads 1.4** - eight more pads, each in every key: Sunday Choir (the worship choir bed), Hallelujah Brass, Mercy Seat (quiet, for the altar), Living Water (bells and running water), Altar Call (electric piano over low strings), Overture (the film opening), Prairie (an ambient guitar under a wide sky) and Hope Rising (the build into the last chorus). Three more ambient loops in every key: Vinyl Piano (the pop grand through an old record), Shimmer Guitar and Flute Air.
- **Central Instrument 1.4** - six more grooves at three tempos: an anthem ride in half-time, an acoustic set (cajon, tambourine and shaker), a soft bossa nova and a 70s pocket on the Vintage Kit, a lo-fi 6/8 and a celebration.
- **Central Plugins 1.4** - six more boards: a vocal bus polish, a worship electric swell bed, keys tape warmth, an acoustic live-stream chain, a bass parallel squash and a wide, soft pad bus.
- **Central Pedals 1.4** - six more boards: a clean amp into the free Starter 1x12, a Uni-Vibe worship clean, an octave-up lead, a spring tweed rhythm, and bass and acoustic chains into the Starter cabinet and body.
- **Central Keys 1.5** - the synths moved to Central Starter (they're free now); everything else is as it was.

### Eight more Central Tracks hymns (54 in all)
Come, Ye Thankful People, Come (for Thanksgiving) · Immortal, Invisible, God Only Wise · Count Your Blessings · There Is Power in the Blood · O Worship the King · What Child Is This · Go, Tell It on the Mountain · Christ Arose (Low in the Grave He Lay). Two of them in a real 3/4 waltz (a new band style), one in E minor (the first), and Go, Tell It starts with its refrain, the way it's sung.

### Behind the scenes
- A library can be free (plan "free"): the installers, the website's list and the apps' Libraries page all know the Free plan, and a loop set can be shared between a free library and a pack ("only" / "except" in libraries.json).
